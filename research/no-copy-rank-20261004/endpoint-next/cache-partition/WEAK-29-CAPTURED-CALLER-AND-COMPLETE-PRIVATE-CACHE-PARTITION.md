# Weak-2.9: captured-caller versus complete-private dependency partition

Publication copy: nonmathematical provenance wording and/or local paths were sanitized. Original/public SHA-256 values are recorded in `INVENTORY.json`; historical source/audit pins refer to the original versions.

Date: 2026-10-04. New source-qualified cache audit.

## Result

For M independent COMPLETE weak-E records at one already exposed physical caller θ, the largest cache justified by the cited finite-program contracts is the union of exact common subcomputations whose **entire transitive private read set is empty**. It includes top-level complete zero trajectories, their original-gradient queries, fixed coefficients and genuinely captured incoming labels. It does not include random source ancestors merely because they are expensive, occur before the final force query, have the same law, or are called an anchor.

With exact-key duplicate queries first identified, write S for this captured subgraph and T_i for the remainder of complete record i. The valid work form is

    Q_E,all = Q_S + sum_(i=1)^M Q_(T_i),

and, for identical fixed source templates, Q_E,all = Q_S + M Q_T. This replaces M(Q_S+Q_T), saving only (M−1)Q_S. Every private history, observation, decoder, finite proxy iteration and nested moving-zero computation remains in T_i. There is no source-supported decomposition into one sampled expensive old ancestor plus M cheap terminals.

Two qualifications are essential:

1. A complete source zero F_actual(θ;0) is θ-measurable. A subsource zero f(y_i(θ,W_i);0) usually is not: its entering caller y_i still reads W_i. The latter belongs to T_i.
2. E_i=F_actual(θ;W_i)−H_fin(θ;W_i) uses one master record inside i. This permits genuine same-query aliases within i; it does not identify any private record in i with the independent record in j.

The certificate gives symbolic finite enumerations and source-relative contracts, not a selected numerical host transcript. Consequently an actual numerical Q_S or Q_T is **not recoverable from the supplied document alone**. The exact counting procedure, available literal module counts and required instantiation fields are given below. No numerical count is invented.

## 2. The exact conditional sampling boundary

Let C=σ(θ, declared fixed construction data) be the captured information for this call. The declared data includes the original potential/oracle identity, generation, physical heat/domain, geometry, source order, covariance rows, moment list, finite iteration and compiler orders, numerical versions, and precision schedule. These choices are fixed before θ differentiation as required by W29.

Write each master record as

    W_i = (W0_i, Z_tw,i),     i=1,...,M.

W0_i is the COMPLETE original source record, not just the terminal Gaussian. It includes every random history, observation, decoder, original local-statistic bank, internal proxy auxiliary, finite source replay and descendant record owned by the actual source. Z_tw,i is the additional weak-2.9 twin bank. At fixed θ the W_i are independent complete copies. Aliases inside each W_i remain the original aliases.

The other two-arm records are Ω_H and Z_E. Both are independent of every W_i and of one another conditional on θ. Ω_H is the whole positive-gradient-compiler record, including its nested source calls; Z_E is the unchanged numerical Gaussian keep. M in this note is W29's N, not its compiler order m.

### Actual randomness captured before the call

CW7 §1 explicitly fixes θ, including the **once-refreshed phase momentum and all previous physical labels, before new banks**. CW7 §3 and CW7-ret §5 state that the common phase G0 is sampled once and remains captured, with physical injection

    c sqrt(A) S_t sigma_t = O(sqrt(A) t^(3/2)).

This is genuine already-captured random state. It may be shared because it is part of the entering information C. It is not refreshed by copying a force's private tape. Its actual caller/phase derivative and any already-incurred production work remain charged at their original ownership level.

This explicit fact does **not** move an OLD-R13 ancestor computed inside W0_i into θ. Nor does fixing a child caller locally make that caller globally C-measurable. If the child caller is y_i(W_i), it is fixed only for that child's conditional law; the enclosing weak-E bank still owns its ancestors.

For the generic W29 interface, no additional random core is declared pre-sampled and captured. If a concrete host has further incoming captured labels, list their provenance and ownership explicitly. A label newly formed from W_i cannot be added to θ afterward to justify cross-bank reuse: that changes the conditional program and its target.

## 3. Dependency test and maximal source-certified cache

Expand the chosen finite VALUE implementation into its actual DAG, retaining admitted whole blocks only with their complete declared input/read sets. First apply only proved pathwise identities and exact common-subexpression aliases. In particular, do not use a law identity, small Lp error, or equality of means as a simplification rule.

For every node v define R(v), the set of owned fresh-record coordinates on which its executed value depends:

- R(captured input or fixed coefficient)=empty.
- R(W_i-coordinate) contains that coordinate with its full bank/ancestor identity.
- R(v) is the union of the read sets of all inputs and entering labels actually consumed by v, after certified exact eliminations.
- A call's local private zero sets only that call's private coordinates to zero. It does not clear its entering-label read set.
- For a whole block, include all externally read parent labels and every internally owned record; a black-box name does not hide dependencies.

The certified cache S is the set of common exact-key nodes with R(v)=empty, closed under their predecessors. T_i contains the remaining nodes with bank-i read sets. A genuinely common computation already supplied with θ need not be reproduced; its upstream cost belongs to producing θ. A new θ-dependent source-zero computation has to be executed once and is included in Q_S.

This is maximal for the exposed, exactly simplified transcript under the given read-set certificates. Further special cancellations for a particular potential could enlarge it, but require their own pathwise proof and replacement transcript. No universal algebraic minimization of an unspecified source is asserted.

## 4. Same-record aliases and semantic keys

At the module boundary one E copy executes

    E_fin(θ;W_i) = F_actual(θ;W0_i) − H_fin(θ;W0_i,Z_tw,i).

The F side ignores only the genuinely new twin coordinates. The H side uses the finite protected/signed graph built from the chosen retained source. Their inputs share the SAME W0_i. This is necessary for the pointwise remainder and does not assert that all their original-gradient query points are equal: retained substitution, protected-row removal, twin feedback, and finite iteration change queries generically.

A reusable original query needs a certified exact key. A sufficient full key is

    (original oracle / potential identity,
     complete source and finite numerical version,
     actual heat and native query width,
     exact point expression and captured-label environment,
     identities and aliases of every owned primitive record read,
     exact row / sign / filter / iteration / anchor versions,
     VALUE or requested directional/adjoint action).

For VALUE queries, two output readouts may reuse one recorded original value when this key agrees. Full labels that are semantically irrelevant may be removed only by a proved exact identity. Runtime coincidence of two independent numerical queries is not a uniform reduction in the certified count.

For FIRST/HVP work, agreement of the primal query is not agreement of arbitrary tangent directions. One may share the stored primal and aggregate reverse cotangents at that exact node. Distinct requested forward directions still require their actual priced actions unless linear combination into a single required action is explicitly valid for that output. No full Hessian or derivative of an HVP becomes a free oracle.

Permitted examples:

- A single same-record source value feeds both a physical readout and another exact readout.
- Identical top-level q_i^0, F0 or complete-zero nodes shared by many empirical copies.
- One actual T_old feeds every observation of its own decoder.
- A source query genuinely common to F and H within the same bank after exact key matching.

Not permitted:

- M independently prescribed W0_i histories replaced by one stored random history.
- One private decoder state or one local-zero anchor at a private caller shared across i.
- Identifying P_rec, B and the later retained P_new: these are distinct maps in W29.
- Holding one finite F/H version fixed while refining only its subtractand or zero trajectory.
- A source value reused because it has the same distribution, mean, covariance, query heat, or provider name.

## 5. Exact VALUE census and what is actually enumerable

Let V_F and V_H be the sets of original-gradient VALUE query nodes in the chosen complete F_actual and H_fin evaluations. They include all expanded nested sources, zero/anchor queries and finite iterations. Form their union **modulo certified exact-key aliases**, rather than identifying the two graphs wholesale. Let

    V_E = (V_F union V_H) / exact aliases,
    V_S = {v in V_E : R(v)=empty},
    V_T = V_E minus V_S,
    Q_S=|V_S|,    Q_T=|V_T|.

Then, for M copies of a fixed finite template at the same θ,

    Q_E,all = |V_S union (disjoint union_i V_T,i)|
            = Q_S + M Q_T.                                      (5.1)

This is a literal census identity for that DAG. Its conservative source-level upper bill is

    Q_E,all <= Q_S + M(Q_F,private + Q_H,private),                 (5.2)

with any additional private duplicate calls safely overcounted. Equivalently the unoptimized W29 bill M(Q_F+Q_H) can lose (M−1) times only the truly common captured-query part, plus separately proved within-bank duplicate savings. Nothing in W29 bounds Q_T by a terminal-only cost.

If numerical schedules, heat labels or branch programs differ, use the sum over Q_(T_i); common θ alone is insufficient for identical keys. All actual heat labels are preserved. At a later serial caller θ' the captured query key generally changes and its S must be rebuilt or exactly matched anew.

If slots are admitted compound calls rather than original leaves, expand their actual costs first. W29 prints neither n_tw nor a concrete K for a selected host.

- Each stated old local statistic contains **one cheap-gradient mean, nine residual replay sources, four complete matched mixed reserves and one strong-self reserve**. Each source occurrence expands all its OWN OLD-R13 ancestors. “Four reserves” is a module count, not four primitive gradient queries.

## 6. FIRST, adjoint and discarded-primal replay ledger

For an original gradient node v, VALUE records ∇V(q_v). One forward derivative action at its saved query is the original HVP ∇²V(q_v)·dot(q_v). A reverse sweep aggregates every outgoing cotangent at v and applies the corresponding original transpose action once for that aggregate. There is no derivative convergence assumption and no derivative of an HVP output.

With saved primals and one entering direction, the literal active HVP count is

    Q_FIRST = sum_(v in V_S) 1{required captured action at v}
               + sum_i sum_(v in V_T,i) 1{required private action at v},

at most Q_S+M Q_T for one primitive-leaf sweep, up to the fixed implementation factor in the supplied whole-block interfaces. If all these actions are required, this is the same Q_S+M Q_T census. A full new tangent direction costs a new sweep.

Three cases should not be conflated:

1. **Fresh-W differentiation:** captured S is constant in W, so it has no fresh-W action. Each active T_i remains separately differentiated; cross-bank independent roots do not share a tangent record.
2. **Common θ forward direction:** compute the θ-direction of S once, then propagate it through every T_i together with each bank's actual random query path. This gives no 1/sqrt(M) improvement to the coherent caller derivative. Captured G0 paths are composed with their original physical injection.
3. **Adjoint of one combined output:** collect all contributions from the M banks at the shared S interface, then reverse S once using the aggregate. Reversing each bank separately and failing to add parent contributions is incorrect. Requests for distinct independent output adjoints must be charged as such.

Caching VALUES alone does not store HVPs for arbitrary later directions. If primal intermediate queries or source records were discarded, reconstructing them requires original VALUE replay on the **same** saved input/tape/version. Let R_S and R_T,i be the counts of such replayed original VALUE nodes under the chosen checkpoint schedule. Add

    Q_replay = R_S + sum_i R_T,i

and any needed repeated original actions to the work bill. A random seed can regenerate a tape if the implementation permits it, but it does not regenerate oracle values for free. Streaming can reduce stored private tapes; it cannot turn their replays into S or remove M.

## 7. Complete zero origin versus moving subsource zeros

For a top-level finite DAG

    p_j(W)=g_j(b_j+C_j W+sum_(l<j) M_jl p_l(W)),

execute its complete zero path at the same θ:

    p_j^0=p_j(0),     q_j^0=b_j+sum_(l<j) M_jl p_l^0.

These nodes are θ-measurable. The exact centered graph is

    z_j(W)=g_j(q_j^0+C_jW+sum M_jl z_l(W))−g_j(q_j^0).

Stored g_j(q_j^0) is eligible for S. The nonzero argument remains private. With the same centered differences and zero-start finite twin, every actual zero iterate is zero. That can eliminate repeated queries that are proved identical at the top-level origin; it cannot eliminate the required original zero trajectory that defines q_j^0.

By contrast, if a later subsource is called at y(W), its own centered identity uses q_sub^0(y(W)). This is a moving value computed at the live private caller. It remains in T even though the subsource's internal fresh argument is zero. Replacing it by q_sub^0(y(0)) changes the program. The same warning applies to a compiler vertex's coarse label Q_v and to c=f(b), q=f(b+CW−Sc+Sq), p=q−c: the first node is captured only when b's whole read set is empty.

W29 chooses

    G_fin=(r/beta) C_tw* [p_K(W)−p_K(0)] + B* F0,
    H_fin=B G_fin,       BB*=I.

Hence H_fin(θ;0)=F_actual(θ;0) exactly at the frozen finite version, and E_fin(θ;0)=0. The top-level F0 contribution cancels between F_actual and H_fin if the rewritten finite transcript explicitly uses the corresponding centered actual graph. This can save duplicate top-level readouts and propagate exact zero identities; it does not imply cancellation of the random actual/retained histories or all moving anchors in E.

The origin identity alone does not prove the A^3.9 approximation. The actual centered protected/twin comparison and actual-to-retained origin contract must still be checked. If only an uncentered estimate exists, retain

    e_origin,p = ||F_actual(θ;0)−H_raw,K(θ;0)||_Lp(θ)

and its original computation/derivative paths. Such a shared deterministic-in-W bias receives no averaging improvement.

Exact32's mixed residuals are zero at the all-zero tape for each caller, and its self response is odd with the same property. It keeps the cheap mean, nine replay sources, Gaussian carriers and moving-zero nodes. The older actual-to-retained replay origin equality is still an inherited condition; an Lp mark alone cannot supply it.

## 8. Retained active tapes and protected records

There are two different retained constructions.

### Inside each actual complete E_i

F_actual owns its full W0_i. H_fin owns its padded full master record (W0_i,Z_tw,i) and uses the declared protected projection/twin. One must not delete random OLD-R13 histories, observation/decoder coordinates or the weak twin bank from the actual E_i source. The differences are pointwise on this record. The old actual tail F_actual−F_ret is still included in E_i.

For the CW7 hidden source, the final completed known-center decoder child supplies a fixed portion of the source-zero carrier. Its late protected increment is fresh after all earlier statistic/observation/decoder records, which ignore it. Later paths inside that final child are those charged by the native protected construction. For weak J=2.9:

    v_prot=19/15,  A_prot=A^(34/15),
    epsilon_tw=A^(9/10),  beta approximately A^(9/10),
    P_rec Pi P_rec*=gamma I,  gamma >= A^(19/15)/Lambda.

The old child cutoff 14/3 provides the stated room. This protected increment is a **private part of each complete copied source**, not a pre-caller captured cache. Its unread-prefix property must remain true. Earlier queries may not start reading it through a cached shared object, and different outer banks may not alias it.

### After the empirical signal is deleted in the auxiliary kept Y graph

W29 §§8–9 retains the whole finite Y_H graph, its exact known Gaussian carrier, every original VALUE occurrence, its actual moving anchors and zero paths, plus sqrt(v_E) Z_E. It deletes only the signal M^(-1) sum_i E_fin(W_i).

Only after all retained centers are literally replaced and retained offsets have certified read sets can every W_i become unread in that auxiliary kept graph. The next gradient source may use its exact active selector E_keep, with

    G_pad(W)=E_keep* G(E_keep W),     B_pad=B E_keep.

This is an active-source/tape identity for the kept auxiliary graph. It is not a cache optimization of the actual M evaluations, not a law-only tape compression, and not permission to replace its retained carrier with a newly sampled independent one. Nonlinear H-arm compiler records and any offsets whose paths remain private are still active. Captured data with no fresh read set stays as an external caller label, with its caller derivative intact.

## 9. Why enlarging the cache by a sampled ancestor changes the law

Suppose a source factors E(U,H), with U an ancestor that is currently inside the COMPLETE record. Independent complete copies give, conditional on θ,

    Var(M^(-1) sum_i E(U_i,H_i) | θ)
      = [Var(μ(U)|θ) + E Var(E|U,θ)]/M,

where μ(U)=E[E|U,θ]. Keeping one U and copying only H_i instead gives

    Var(μ(U)|θ) + E Var(E|U,θ)/M.

The extra (1−1/M)Var(μ(U)|θ) is absent from the independent-complete empirical proof. For E=U+H with independent standard scalar U,H, these are 2/M and 1+1/M. The second program may be useful only with a new hierarchical conditional-mean/covariance/cumulant construction and its own work/retention proof. It is not a cache of the existing one.

Conditioning on the shared sampled U and calling it a new caller would also change the target from E[E|θ] to E[E|θ,U]. An outer correction for that random conditional mean remains necessary. The same principle applies when a root reused in multiple factors changes E[B|X]E[B|X]* into E[BB*|X]. Neither equality of source names nor marginal Gaussian approximation repairs the altered joint law.

The W29 empirical law uses complete independence to obtain its 1/M buffered error. Sharing S preserves the exact finite map and therefore preserves that proof; sharing any nontrivial private ancestor does not inherit it.

## 10. Instantiation checklist and final boundary

To turn the symbolic partition into numerical counts, supply one frozen, fully expanded source transcript with:

1. Root caller/captured labels and sampling chronology, explicitly identifying any pre-caller G0.
2. Every original leaf query's exact semantic key, actual heat/width, numerical version and parent edges.
3. Complete owned record identifiers, including old ancestors, decoder children, compiler roots/shields, twin and protected increments.
4. Both the complete top-level zero transcript and every nested moving-zero call at its actual live caller.
5. The selected K, m, source domains, precision/filter schedules and all original-source restoration choices.
6. Exact F/H shared-node aliases; a list of independent replicas that must not be merged.
7. Original VALUE/HVP counts, saved-primal/checkpoint plan, replay counts, Gaussian/linear arithmetic dimensions and storage.
8. Actual-to-retained origin/center contracts and the retained active selector/protected read-set verification.

Then topologically propagate the read sets, form the exact-key quotient, and count (5.1) and the FIRST/replay ledger. Until those fields are supplied, the supported answer is the maximal **rule and literal structural census**, not a numerical saving factor.

**Conclusion:** the allowed optimization is caller-only common-subgraph caching, including complete top-level zero work, plus exact same-record query reuse. It preserves M independent COMPLETE replays with every private ancestor in T. W29 provides no stochastic ancestor cache that removes this multiplicity and no basis for upgrading a distributional sharing proposal into an implementation identity.
