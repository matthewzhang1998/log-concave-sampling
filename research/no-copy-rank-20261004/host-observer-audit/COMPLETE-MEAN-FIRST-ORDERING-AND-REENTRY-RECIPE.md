## 1. The two proofs must remain distinct

### Proof A: conditional law, then actual T-only postprocessing

Freeze the actual captured caller theta, including the already sampled phase G0 and all fixed source versions/parameters. Suppose the COMPLETE executed statistic T has

    W2(Law(T|theta),N(q(theta),sigma^2 I))<=epsilon_T(theta).

This compares its entire output. Its internal fine/source/clock tapes may be integrated. Keep theta fixed in a conditional coupling and then draw the same fresh observation pool on both sides:

    R_i=T+sqrt(a)(E_i-Ebar),
    R_i^ref=T_ref+sqrt(a)(E_i-Ebar).

Use ONE shared T on each side, so all observation differences equal T-T_ref. At sigma^2=a/(k+1), the reference observation covariance is exactly a delta_ij I. This identity would fail if T were resampled separately for different observations.

Run the actual finite mode and fresh completed known-center children on both sides, using their same primitive child records after their respective callers have been exposed. LOW30's finite mode starts b0=y and iterates b_(j+1)=y-aF(b_j); with unit original first,

    L_mode<=sum_(j=0)^J a^j<=1/(1-a)<=2.

The first downstream original-gradient query is F(R_0). It sees the old statistic-private record only through R_0, hence through T.

For the ACTUAL finite completed child, use its stated center bound

    L_child<=1+delta, delta=Lambda sqrt(a),
    lambda=L_child/2.

Under the ordinary guard delta<=1/4, lambda<=5/8. The same-seed state discrepancy obeys

    |Delta X_i|<=lambda |Delta X_(i-1)|+lambda|T-T_ref|.

Consequently

    |Delta X_k|<=[lambda^k L_mode
                 +lambda(1-lambda^k)/(1-lambda)]|T-T_ref|
                <=2|T-T_ref|.                         (1)

Thus the completed actual downstream kernel costs at most 2 epsilon_T in conditional W2. Integrating epsilon_T(theta)^2 over the proved actual caller law gives the unconditional bound. With other literal guards use the displayed geometric factor instead of the numerical 2. The separate posterior-reference contraction 1/2 pays the known-center services' intrinsic law errors; it is not substituted for the actual finite same-seed derivative in (1).

At the terminal original-gradient force, its readout r/sqrt(a) contributes that actual multiplier to a state coupling error. All additional physical/statistic weights are inserted before assigning a local tolerance.

The older CW7 host has this exact separator: its statistic banks feed one T; the observations read T; the finite mode and every child caller read those observations and previous states. No displayed physical decoder query reads an old statistic-private fine variable by a second path.

## 2. A concrete local mean recipe from an actual projected-gradient split

The newly constructed fixture suggests the following sufficient LOCAL producer interface. Suppose on a declared common complete source tape one has a pathwise split

    E(theta;W)=H(theta;W)+R(theta;W),
    H=B G, BB*=I,

where G is an ACTUAL admitted full-gradient VALUE source (or a finite version with its coherent exact-gradient restoration), and R is an ACTUAL near-gradient VALUE source. Its energy, first, curl, caller, source-zero and numerical rows are supplied on that tape. A comparison-law error is not such an R.

At the captured theta, choose fixed numerical positive variances v_H+v_R=v_E. Execute on independent COMPLETE banks:

    Y_H=H0+sqrt(v_H) B N_m((G-G(0))/sqrt(v_H)),
    Y_R=R0+sqrt(v_R) N^v((R-R0)/sqrt(v_R)),
    Y_E=Y_H+Y_R.

Here H0=H(theta;0), R0=R(theta;0); their full caller derivatives and original zero computations remain. N^v is the admitted physical near-gradient VALUE mean, using the recorded coisometry and known Gaussian completion when the square lift is employed. E0=H0+R0 is kept exactly. The actual H inside the R source remains coupled to E on that source's own complete bank; independence is imposed only between the completed mean branches.

The conditional targets add to

    N(E[H|theta]+E[R|theta],v_E I)
        =N(E[E|theta],v_E I).

No joint law with an unused opposite baseline is required for this mean identity. If the baseline is not executed or exported by these programs, it is not an external observer to be retained. A baseline used internally by a proposed packet must still obey that packet's actual retained-variable contract.

For G's full first rho_H, the fixed-order gradient error is the actual

    Lambda_m sqrt(n_G) rho_H^m + epsilon_G,rest/rows/filters.

For R with first A, curl kappa=A^(1+g), actual uncentered fixed-moment profile e_R, and padding mu, its centered law mark is at most e_R. The near-gradient VALUE mean therefore gives, up to fixed variance factors,

    epsilon_R<=Lambda e_R
              {A(A^g+mu)+A^3(1+mu^(-1/2))}
                  +epsilon_R,num.                    (2)

At g=1, mu=A, this is O(A^2 e_R) plus absolute floors. For the origin-anchored execution displayed above, define separately e_R,anch,p=||R-R0||p. Its actual residual after removing R0 and the known Gaussian carrier has energy O(e_R,anch,p), not automatically O(e_R,p). Either prove R0=0, prove ||R0||p<=C e_R,p, or carry this separate anchored profile and origin debt. A Gaussian Lp source bound does not control its deterministic zero value. The actual first is Lambda(A+A^2 mu^(-1/2)), and its caller is Lambda(1+A mu^(-1/2))L_R. The simplified O(A) first holds for mu=A (or mu>=A^2), with the corresponding caller specialization. These are executed-program bounds, not consequences of (2).

For the terminal-flat split, H=H_plus or H_minus has rho_H=O(A^2), and

    e_R,p<=C_p[A+(A^1.9)^(1/p)] e_actual.

The fixture has literal H(0)=R(0)=0, so its anchored and displayed uncentered profiles coincide. Thus this produces a genuine bounded-grade no-copy mean improvement on that fixture, with fresh independent completed branches and no extra baseline observer. It is not a decomposition of the general W29 source.

## 3. Retaining the actual program in the new recipe

A safe candidate kept graph retains the WHOLE gradient-mean Y_H circuit, all its original VALUE leaves, its actual source-zero carrier and anchors, while replacing only Y_R's literal source-dependent residual by its known carrier and R0. This is different from deleting the entire E-repair arm. A gradient mean can have an ordinary retained body larger than the desired deletion tolerance; it may be kept as a full admitted small-source block. Its law comparison does not authorize deleting that body.

The current W29 reconstruction nevertheless explicitly requires its chosen new host's section-8 verification. A source-relative recipe is not a substitute for that enumeration in a general host.

If these graph gates hold, Proof B applies. If they do not, the unresolved item is a source/graph admission, not an arbitrary joint law with private fine records.

## 5. Which old gates disappear and which remain

Disappear as EXTERNAL CW7 law-interface demands:

- a joint Gaussian approximation of a completed mean and its arbitrary original fine-source values;
- retaining an unused opposite baseline through a pair comparison solely because a later decoder exists;
- coupling the Gaussian mean reference to the actual source-zero carrier for purposes of the separate kept-graph proof;
- preventing a complete packet from averaging its own covariance-clock roots before the host is attached, when no remaining packet output separately reads those roots.

- every within-packet shared-passive/shared-root/same-query identity required to establish that packet's law;
- a baseline or other actual packet output reading a fine variable after a pair comparison would integrate it;
- a valid actual projected-gradient/near-gradient source, rather than a formal selected word or coupling error;

The selected host therefore admits a simpler goal: construct a COMPLETE conditional mean with a separate actual retained-source certificate.
