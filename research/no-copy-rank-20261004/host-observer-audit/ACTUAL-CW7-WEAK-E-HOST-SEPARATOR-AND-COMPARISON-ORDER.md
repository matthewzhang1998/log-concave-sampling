## Result

The selected CW7 statistic/observation/decoder host has a genuine sufficient-output boundary. Conditional on the already captured caller theta, its downstream law calculation consumes the whole statistic T and newly drawn observation/child records. It does not separately consume the private source records integrated in proving T's law. The first downstream operation is

    R_i = T + sqrt(a)(E_i-Ebar).

The first downstream original-gradient query is exactly F(R_0)=grad V(R_0): LOW30's finite realization starts b_0=R_0 and performs b_1=R_0-a F(b_0) (lines 13695–13701). Its dependence on a statistic-private fine variable factors entirely through T. Later known-center child queries factor through theta, the shared T, the observation pool, earlier decoder states, and each child's own new record. No first host query with an additional direct read of an integrated statistic-fine variable exists in the displayed selected host.

Therefore an arbitrary joint-fine-observer theorem is NOT an external prerequisite for this host. It is legal to prove the complete repaired statistic's conditional law first, and then append the actual host. This does not permit changing within-packet aliases or appending an internal baseline which reads a variable that a pair theorem already integrated. That separate local obstacle survives exactly where it occurs.

The original full finite graph, all retained labels, moving anchors, carriers and every executed call remain available for the independent energy/first/protected-graph proof. A marginal-law comparison is not a graph simplification, a tape deletion, or a declaration that those labels are unread.

## 2. The minimal law read sets, with no fictitious cache

Let C be the sigma-field generated by theta and the fixed construction data. It includes the once-refreshed phase momentum and previous physical labels already exposed before this call. It does not include any newly sampled OLD-R13 history merely because that history will be expensive.

At one componentwise statistic occurrence, let Omega_l be the COMPLETE private bank of Y_l. Then

    T(theta;Omega) = sigma[h0(theta)
                         + sum_l sqrt(v_l)Y_l(theta;Omega_l)
                         + sqrt(3/4)Z].

Here g_l^0(theta), h0, their actual zero computations, and caller paths are captured computations; a nested zero at a caller depending on Omega_l remains private. The Omega_l are independent only conditional on C. Every old source/proxy/history/observation/decoder alias inside one Omega_l is unchanged.

The following is the minimal displayed downstream read interface:

1. Observation formation: theta, T, and one new shared pool (E_0,...,E_k). It does not read Omega_l separately.
2. Initial finite mode/prox: R_0 and its finite original-gradient iterates. LOW30 starts b_0=R_0, so the first original query is F(R_0); later ones are F(b_j), where b_(j+1)=R_0-a F(b_j). It does not independently read Omega_l. The actual residual center b_J+a F(b_J) and its VALUE error remain charged.
3. Child i caller: y_i=(X_(i-1)+R_i)/2. The completed child is then sampled with a fresh complete record Xi_i after y_i is fixed.
4. Terminal force: original gradient evaluated at the returned hidden state, with its prescribed physical anchor/readout. Its dependence on Omega_l factors through the statistic and decoder values.
5. Subsequent PHYSICAL phase/history continuation: the exported physical state/force and already declared incoming labels are consumed at their actual rows. This statement concerns physical law consumers, not later construction of a new proxy source by reopening the retained graph.

This is a separator for the displayed PHYSICAL LAW CONSUMER, not a universal assertion that every future newly constructed source query factors through T. A future proxy rebuild has the different interface in section 6. This is not a claim that the actual output has no derivative in Omega_l. It plainly does. The assertion is that all routes from Omega_l to a future host law test pass through the exported value T. A host query f(T) is not an extra observer of Omega_l beyond T.

## 3. The legal conditional coupling and the shared observation pool

Suppose a finite statistic has, at its actual captured caller,

    W2(Law(T | C), N(q(theta),sigma^2 I)) <= delta(theta).

Choose a measurable conditional coupling of T and T_ref with squared cost at most delta(theta)^2, allowing an arbitrarily small approximation to the optimum if needed. Keep C identical. Append the SAME fresh observation pool and then couple each known-center transition by its admitted center contract and fresh record construction.

Every observation difference is the SAME vector T-T_ref. One must not independently replace the T in different R_i. With sigma^2=a/(k+1), the reference observation covariance is exactly

    Cov(R_i,R_j | C) = sigma^2 I + a(delta_ij-1/(k+1))I
                    = a delta_ij I.

Thus the reference observations are independent Gaussian vectors. For the ACTUAL finite same-seed postprocessing, use the supplied complete child's center derivative. CW7-ret section 5 permits the conservative bound L_child<=1+delta, delta=Lambda sqrt(a), rather than borrowing the smaller kept-center derivative. Under delta<=1/4, put lambda=L_child/2<=5/8. The finite mode starts at b_0=y and satisfies b_(j+1)=y-aF(b_j), hence

    L_mode <= sum_(j=0)^J a^j <= 1/(1-a) <= 2.

With the same observation pool and the same new child seeds, actual finite outputs obey

    D_k <= [lambda^k L_mode + lambda(1-lambda^k)/(1-lambda)] |T-T_ref|
        <= 2 |T-T_ref|.

Thus the actual T-only postprocessing kernel costs at most 2 delta(theta) in conditional W2 under these guards. More generally use its literal bracket/profile. Integrate the squared conditional cost over the actual caller profile. A bound valid only after integrating caller moments is not promoted to a uniform bound at every caller.

Posterior-reference contraction 1/2 controls the SEPARATE intrinsic child-law errors and convergence-to-target term. The auxiliary KEPT graph separately uses 1/2+O(a). Neither coefficient is substituted for the actual finite child's potentially weaker center derivative in the displayed same-seed inequality.

This is precisely the proof architecture of LOW30's sufficient-statistic decoder and CW7 section 4. It does not require coupling T jointly to its source-zero carrier, fine source values, or retained derivative graph.

## 9. Classification of the earlier open observer gates

The following distinctions narrow the old open statements without reversing any valid local counterexample.

### Removed as external host requirements

- An assertion that a completed E-mean must be jointly close with every old source-fine record simply because a later CW7 decoder or phase will use it. The actual host reads the exported T; its first original query F(R_0) confirms the separator.
- An assertion that the pair law must retain the source-zero carrier or every moving anchor because those are needed for the later retained/protected graph. Those objects stay in the ACTUAL graph proof. They are not outputs of the conditional law comparison.
- An assertion that source-private records from different independent complete repair packets must remain jointly shared. Only captured theta is common between those packets unless the actual packet specification names another retained public. Full within-packet aliases survive.
- The general retained-root observer counterexample is not a no-go for a completed statistic with its roots integrated before host attachment. Its lower bound applies precisely when a distinct root is also observed. The selected host supplies no such observation.

### Conditional on the chosen internal repair, rather than unavoidable host gates

- The old nonlinear midpoint baseline N_h does not occur in CW7's observation interface or W29's two-arm interface. Keeping N_h jointly is an obligation of a repair which actually uses that same-record baseline. It is not a universal requirement on every E-mean algorithm.
- In particular, if one first defines literal sources E_fin=H+R on the same complete source record, then independent whole mean programs for H and R can be added at fixed theta: E[H|theta]+E[R|theta]=E[E_fin|theta]. No joint source-law theorem with an unused N_h is required. R must remain a literal complete VALUE source, not an error vector chosen by a coupling. This observation licenses the ordering, not a mean compiler or energy return for the new R.
- A complete correction packet can integrate its own coarse roots after its correct coefficient/endpoint law is established. If a later packet wants those exact roots, they become declared external labels, and the earlier theorem must really retain them. The builder may instead use an independent complete packet when the target factorization supports that choice.

### Still required where actually used

- The selected pair's incoming passive and declared original external labels, with its conditional error envelope. One cannot integrate them early if a sibling, parent, or baseline still reads them.
- The same fine variable in both original source and proxy inside E_fin, and every fine-bank alias required by a coefficient product, sign response, forward/reverse channel, or nonlinear input constraint. Replacing one factor by an independent copy changes its coefficient in general.
- The first concrete forbidden extra read in the old midpoint architecture is g_t(tbar(cR+vX)+hMV), when its X was integrated by a pair comparison but its baseline is retained. This is an INTERNAL original source query.
- The all-root orientation field and its actual Gaussian-product/calibration identity. A zero-root-plane identity or an average covariance is not that field if the internal coefficient producer still conditions on its root.
- Actual full-gradient/source grammar, finite first/caller and ordinary-energy bounds, whole companion energies, original anchors, numerical floors, protected rows and fixed-order queue/cost enumeration.

The fixture-specific projected-gradient escape remains valid on its documented scope. Its old caveat about a joint nonlinear baseline is necessary for that baseline-sharing endpoint architecture; it should not be carried forward as an extra demand of the independent CW7 statistic host. None of this promotes W29 section 8 to an unconditional new-host certificate.
