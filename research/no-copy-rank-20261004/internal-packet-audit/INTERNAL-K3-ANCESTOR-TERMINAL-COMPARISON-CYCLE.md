# The first internal K3 observer survives complete-bank regeneration

Publication copy: local source paths were sanitized. Original/public SHA-256 values are recorded in `INVENTORY.json`; historical pins identify the original versions.

2026-10-04. A bounded audit of a proposed ancestor-primitive-pair / terminal-primitive-pair producer. The external CW7 host gate has already been removed. This note identifies the remaining internal interface, gives two exact same-potential matrix-target counterexamples, and records a finite query and first-only ledger. It is not an impossibility theorem for another complete mean producer or for every paired K0−K1 cancellation.

## 1. Result and precise scope

The smallest offending original-gradient read is already

    gt(S + a M g0(x + v Zfine)).                         (1)

Here the ancestor pair owns and integrates Zfine, but the following terminal source still uses the actual g0 VALUE at that fine query as its center. The issue occurs inside the six-query K3 source, before any owned-root current or CW7 decoder is attached. Retaining only the ancestor pair's completed output does not retain this original source VALUE.

Fresh COMPLETE packets conditional on the captured caller are legal. They have two different consequences:

- Regenerate the WHOLE coupled six-query word on one new bank: its distribution and all aliases are preserved. The same internal joint-heat problem is present on that new bank.
- Regenerate the terminal branch on a new complete bank while keeping the old ancestor factor: the coefficient becomes a product of conditional means instead of the mean of the same-record product. Sections 5–6 give an exact same-potential Hessian-sandwich witness. The discrepancy persists after the owned covariance root is averaged.

Freezing the old complete fine record as original labels is also legal for a NEW primitive pair comparison. That comparison needs its own positive primitive heat. Its two primitive factors are newly heated at the frozen physical query points; section 7 shows that this changes the matrix target as well. A new joint restoration theorem would be needed. It is not supplied by calling the old fine record a label.

The separate addendum PAIRED-K3-COHERENT-DRIFT-REGENERATION-GAP.md now proves a nonzero PAIRED K0−K1 gap for independent terminal-factor regeneration on an actual same-potential coherent-drift native family, including after shared-root and positive-clock averaging. The exact dependency/comparison-order obstruction applies to both terminal branches. The quantitative expectation lower bounds below test K0 itself. ## 2. One actual finite six-query record

Fix one captured caller theta, one finite original-source version, fixed r,a,epsilon and known M. At a positive whole-input covariance clock let

    W=(S,U,Z)=h R+v V,  h^2+v^2=1,  0<v<1,
    x=cS+sU,  c^2+s^2=1.

R is an owned coarse covariance root, not automatically a captured caller. Conditional on theta,R, V is one COMPLETE fine record. A literal topological order for the native source is

    q1 = gt(S),
    q2 = gt(S+epsilon Z),
    x0 = x,                 x1 = x+a M*(q1-q2),
    q3 = g0(x0),
    q4 = g0(x1),
    t0 = S+a M q3,           t1 = S+a M q4,
    q5 = gt(t0),
    q6 = gt(t1),
    E  = r(q5-q6).                                          (2)

This is six original gradient VALUES before exact query aliases. Every changed source input regenerates this same six-node graph. Its analytical two-factor labels are

    K0(V)=Ht(t0(V)) M H0(x0(V)),
    K1(V)=Ht(t1(V)) M H0(x1(V)).                            (3)

No Hessian in (3) is an executed VALUE.

To isolate the smallest internal path, hold S and the feedback data fixed and write the ancestor query as x+v Zfine. Then (1) is precisely q5. This sub-conditioning only suppresses other read paths; it does not manufacture a new external observer. In the complete record, q5 additionally reads S, and q6 also reads q1,q2 through x1 and q4. These additional paths make the read set larger, not smaller.

The first relevant directed path is

    ancestor-owned Zfine -> q3 -> t0 -> q5.                 (4)

For the second branch the corresponding path is

    ancestor-owned fine record -> (q1,q2,q4) -> t1 -> q6.    (5)

Even if all covariance-root currents are postponed, (4) and (5) are actual source edges needed before those currents.

### Minimal terminal labels already carry the relevant fine information

This claim can be sharpened without asking for arbitrary fine-record observers. Suppose M=I, a,epsilon,s>0, and g0,gt are C1 gradients with a uniform positive Hessian lower bound. Each is a global bijection of R^d: strong monotonicity gives injectivity, and minimizing the coercive strongly convex potential minus p·x gives a unique preimage of every p. The positive Hessian also gives a C1 inverse. The inverse maps below are ANALYTICAL proof identities, never new executed VALUE oracles.

The actual labels already used to construct the two terminal queries are

    L=(S,p0,p1),  p0=g0(x0),  p1=g0(x1).

From this tuple recover, exactly,

    x0=g0^(-1)(p0),             x1=g0^(-1)(p1),
    U=(x0-cS)/s,                Delta=(x1-x0)/a,
    Z=[gt^(-1)(gt(S)-Delta)-S]/epsilon.                 (2a)

Consequently W->(S,p0,p1) is a global bijection, with the displayed measurable inverse. For fixed theta,R and positive v, V=(W-hR)/v, so retaining that terminal-label tuple is information-equivalent to retaining the COMPLETE native fine record V. One branch's labels (S,p0) already determine (S,U); in particular, after S is fixed they determine the active ancestor fine coordinate in x+v Zfine. The second branch's p1 supplies exactly the feedback information needed to recover Z.

Thus the problematic labels are not arbitrary later tests invented to strengthen a marginal theorem. They are the real ancestor VALUES in q5/q6 and the shared S which those queries already read. Retaining only the two terminal points t0,t1 without their construction labels is a different interface; this lemma makes no claim that that smaller tuple determines W.

This information identity does not require an implementation to invert g0 or gt. It also supplies no small-error stability for an approximate tuple: the exact inverse includes the real 1/a,1/epsilon,1/s and 1/v factors. It is used only to identify the information crossing the actual primitive packet cut.

## 3. An explicit finite primitive-pair attempt and the boundary it crosses

This section fixes a concrete finite pair implementation rather than inventing a free Hessian selector. Use LOW30's order-two pair seed, its finite scalar filter, and fresh Gaussian fills. For a primitive gradient source

    f_A(z)=(rho_A/v) g0(x+v z),                            (6)

the source first is at most rho_A. Additive centering at g0(x) is optional for the pair law; when used it is an actual anchor VALUE and its caller path is charged. Its selected matrix target is

    A_A=s0 rho_A E H0(x+v Z),  0<s0<1/4.                  (7)

Let c0=sqrt(1-s0^2), define D_p(u)=f(c0u+s0p)-f(u), and use K+1 distinct Chebyshev nodes b_j in [-1/2,1/2]. Set w_j equal to the derivative at zero of the j-th degree-K Lagrange basis polynomial. Thus sum_j w_j b_j^ell is 1 for ell=1 and zero for ell=0,2,...,K; in particular sum w_j=0. Define the concrete first-coefficient source

    I_K(p,z)=sum_j w_j[f(sqrt(1-b_j^2)z+b_j p)-f(z)].

Its expectation approximates H1(p)=(E Df)p with LOW30's finite first-coefficient error. One legal concrete choice of the otherwise unspecified tail source is

    J(p;U_t,Z_t)=D_p(U_t)-s0 I_K(p,Z_t).

Indeed E J=m(p)-s0(E Df)p plus that finite filter error, exactly the required m_tail. Its private blocks U_t,Z_t are independent, its partial Jacobians stay symmetric, and its complete radius is at most the stated polynomial-logarithmic multiple of the original radius. With this particular J, a fully expanded order-two signal is

    D_p(U_A)-D_p(U_t)
      +s0 sum_j w_j [f(sqrt(1-b_j^2) Z_t+b_j p)-f(Z_t)],   (8)

plus the four independent known Gaussian fills whose positive variance shares sum to one. This is a legal explicit instantiation of LOW30's c_A N2(D/c_A)+c_t N2(-J/c_t)+c_s Z_s+eta Z after choosing the displayed J and writing out the two N2 sources. LOW30 permits other finite tail realizations; the call count below belongs to this one. The seed compares first to N(A_A p,I), then pays the order-rho_A^2 covariance difference to N(A_A p,I-A_A A_A*). It does not execute an unknown matrix square root or give an exact stationary identity at finite order. U_A,U_t,Z_t and the fills are independent, conditional on the original incoming p. The common Z_t is deliberately shared across filter nodes. All queries are finite original VALUES. Choose rho_A and the analogous terminal radius below the stated seed/source guard; no unknown target matrix is evaluated.

The source occurrence f(U_A) in (8) records the actual primitive ancestor value

    pstar=g0(x+v U_A).

The proposed joint-word packet now attempts a following terminal primitive pair, at center

    tstar=S+a M pstar,
    f_T(z)=(rho_T/lambda_T) gt(tstar+lambda_T z),
    A_T(tstar)=s0 rho_T E Ht(tstar+lambda_T Z_T),
    lambda_T>0.                                          (9)

Its incoming selected Gaussian state can be the previous completed output, with the stated known M/J_M adapters and Gaussian completions. This is a perfectly meaningful finite VALUE program. The question is what comparison theorem it has.

The primitive pair contract retains its incoming p and its ORIGINAL external labels. It integrates its source-private records and internal VALUES. The ancestor occurrence pstar in (8) is in that integrated private record. It is not made an external label retroactively by later using it in (9).

The first terminal moving-zero query, when the literal K3 readout (2) is kept, is exactly gt(tstar), equation (1). If a terminal pair seed is executed without this zero query, its first heated query gt(tstar+lambda_T z) has the same forbidden private ancestor read. The zero query's presence is guaranteed in the intact six-query K3 source/mark; the extra terminal innovation does not cure the path.

Crucially, the pair's selected output Y_A is not pstar. Replacing gt(S+a M pstar) by gt(S+a M Y_A) is a different source. No theorem in the cited pair interface identifies those VALUES or gives an inverse reconstruction of pstar from Y_A.

The same discussion applies to a finite higher-order pair: (8) is replaced by its complete finite transcript, and any of its private primitive ancestor occurrences still requires the same retained-variable check. No completed pair output is differentiated as an original gradient source.

## 4. The finite comparison-order cycle

There is no runtime directed cycle in (2): its evaluation is acyclic. The cycle is in the prerequisites for TWO SEPARATE primitive law replacements.

A. Ancestor-first comparison. To replace the ancestor pair by its selected stationary Gaussian kernel and discard its private fine record, every remaining operation must depend on that record only through the retained incoming labels and the exported pair output. q5 violates this criterion through pstar. Thus the terminal observer must first be removed or jointly compared.

T. Terminal-first comparison. Condition on pstar and the rest of the old fine record and compare the terminal pair using fresh terminal-private tapes. This is legal as a conditional terminal comparison. But the reference kernel still has the matrix A_T(tstar(pstar)) from (9), acting on the old ancestor pair's output. Consequently the old fine record remains an internal coefficient observer. To make that terminal reference an allowed postprocessing of the ancestor pair alone, one still needs to average its dependence jointly with the ancestor factor. That is the joint word E[Ht(t0) M H0(x0)] under audit, not an ordinary terminal marginal comparison.

There is also an incoming-error guard at this terminal-first step. The terminal pair's pointwise envelope makes its comparison at the ACTUAL incoming Y_A meaningful, with cost e_T(Y_A,pstar,other labels). But the advertised small integrated prior bounds e_T(G,labels) for a standard Gaussian G conditional on ALL those terminal labels. The ancestor pair theorem supplies its reference only conditional on its earlier retained labels; it does not supply a standard Gaussian incoming state conditional on its discarded pstar. Thus terminal-first cannot silently inherit the small integrated terminal prior. It must price the actual incoming/coupling through the pointwise envelope and its Lipschitz bound, or provide the same missing joint-label comparison. This error-budget issue is in addition to the persistent coefficient observer, and is part of the literal chronological-composition premise.

The blocked prerequisite graph is therefore

    eliminate ancestor-private dependence to close T
       -> need the joint A/T comparison,
    compare A using only its existing marginal pair theorem
       -> first close T's ancestor-private observer.

Equivalently, for the proposed independent replacement order, A needs T's old-fine observer discharged, while discharging T needs information the marginal A theorem has discarded. Swapping the two comparisons never supplies that information. Postponing a later owned-root current changes neither prerequisite.

This is a finite two-site cycle in the actual native q3->q5 path, with q4->q6 as its second branch. It is not an arbitrary-observer impossibility argument. It can be escaped by a proved complete-packet comparison, a genuine joint constrained-law kernel, or another producer that never exports this private label across the primitive boundary.

## 5. Exact SAME-potential positive-clock witness for regeneration

Use d=2, M=I, c=3/5, s=4/5, a=r=1/16, epsilon=a^(9/10), and

    T=[[1/2,1/10],[1/10,1/2]],  N=e1 e1*,  delta=1/10,
    v=1/2, h=sqrt(3)/2, k=1/v=2,
    g0(y)=gt(y)=g(y)=T y+(delta/k) sin(k y1)e1.

The single potential is

    Vpot(y)=y*T y/2 -delta cos(k y1)/k^2.

It is smooth, g(0)=0, and

    H(y)=T+delta cos(k y1)N,
    .3 I <= H(y) <= .7 I                               (10)

globally. The bound follows from .4 I<=T<=.6 I and -delta I<=delta cos(.)N<=delta I. Thus the original primitive is anchored, strongly convex and unit-first. Individual matrices in this family need not commute.

Take the coarse root R=0 in the complete positive clock W=hR+vV. VS,VU,VZ are independent standard two-vectors. Define independent standard two-vectors

    X=c VS+s VU,  Y=s VS-c VU,  VS=cX+sY.

Then x=vX, and for the ACTUAL native t0=S+a g(x),

    C0=cos(k x1)=cos X1,
    Ct=cos(k t0,1)
      =cos(q X1+s Y1+a b X2+beta sin X1),
    q=c+a/2=0.63125,  b=1/10,  beta=a delta=0.00625.

All six nodes in (2), including feedback using VZ, remain defined on this exact record and source. K0 happens not to use Z, r or epsilon; this does not remove them from the complete K3 program. No independently chosen terminal potential is substituted.

Let V' be a complete independent fine bank conditional on theta,R. Original and split-bank branch targets are

    B0(R)=E_V[H(t0(V)) H(x(V))],
    Bsplit(R)=E_(V,V')[H(t0(V')) H(x(V))].               (11)

Expanding H gives EXACTLY

    B0(0)-Bsplit(0)=delta^2 Cov(Ct,C0) N.                (12)

Set L=exp[-(s^2+(ab)^2)/2]. Integrating Y1,X2 first gives

    Cov(Ct,C0)=L Cov(cos(qX+beta sin X),cos X).

At beta=0 the scalar covariance is

    Cbase=exp[-(q^2+1)/2](cosh q-1).

Since |cos(u+beta sin X)-cos u|<=beta,

    Cov(Ct,C0)>=L[Cbase-beta(1+exp(-1/2))]
               =0.0670260701... >0.05.                 (13)

Thus the (1,1) entry of (12) is greater than 5e-4, with the stronger displayed bound 0.0006702607. This is an analytic Gaussian calculation, not an empirical small-sample covariance. Any declared pair attenuation s0^2 rho_A rho_T, the physical ra, and endpoint-adapter norms multiply the mismatch exactly; they do not make the two target identities equal.

A full regenerate of BOTH factors using V' gives E K0(V')=E K0(V), of course. That legal operation reintroduces their shared pstar inside the new record. Equation (12) applies only when the terminal half is regenerated independently of the ancestor half whose selected factor is retained.

## 6. Averaging owned roots later does not repair the split-bank word

The preceding counterexample is already a failure of a conditional field identity on a positive clock. It also has a direct integrated-root version, so postponing and then averaging R cannot remove this example.

Let R,V,V' be independent standard COMPLETE triples and retain the same h=sqrt(3)/2,v=1/2. Compare

    E_R E_V [Ht(t0(hR+vV)) H0(x(hR+vV))]

with

    E_R [E_V Ht(t0(hR+vV))]
          [E_V' H0(x(hR+vV'))].                        (14)

Their difference is delta^2 E_R Cov(Ct,C0 | R) N.

Remove only the nonlinear terminal phase beta sin(kx1) for a calculation, keeping the original nonlinear model as the object of comparison. Let Q=k[S1+a(Tx)1] and B=kx1, evaluated on the standard marginal W=hR+vV. Their variances and covariance are

    A=Var Q=k^2[1+2a(1/2)c+a^2((1/2)^2+b^2)]=4.1540625,
    Bvar=Var B=k^2=4,
    D=Cov(Q,B)=k^2(c+a/2)=2.525.

The exact Gaussian conditional covariance identity is

    E_R Cov(cos Q,cos B | R)
       =exp[-(A+Bvar)/2] [cosh D-cosh(h^2 D)]
       =0.0489757113....                                 (15)

Restoring the bounded phase beta sin B changes each of the two expectations defining this integrated conditional covariance by at most beta. Therefore

    E_R Cov(Ct,C0 | R)>=0.0489757113...-2beta
                      =0.0364757113...>0.03.             (16)

The (1,1) difference in (14) is thus greater than 3e-4, with bound 0.0003647571. Both banks in (14) are independent COMPLETE records conditional on the SAME coarse root; no external host or baseline participates. The mismatch survives the very owned-root averaging suggested as an ordering fix.

If instead complete banks are regenerated independent even of R conditional only on theta, the scalar covariance admits the same formula as (13) with frequency multiplier 2. Its lower bound is 0.0876569552, so the discrepancy also survives that stronger independence. It targets yet another bank coupling; it is not the same-root field in (14).

## 7. Legal freezing is different from illegal averaging

Freeze the COMPLETE original W as original labels of new primitive channels. Their private innovations eta_t,eta_0 are now fresh, independent standard two-vectors. At positive physical primitive widths lambda_t,lambda_0 the legal factors are

    Hbar_t(W)=E_eta Ht(t0(W)+lambda_t eta),
    Hbar_0(W)=E_eta H0(x(W)+lambda_0 eta).

For the same potential,

    Hbar_t=T+alpha_t delta Ct N,
    Hbar_0=T+alpha_0 delta C0 N,
    alpha_j=exp(-k^2 lambda_j^2/2).                      (17)

This is the product selected by the newly heated fixed-location primitive sources. It is not the original product Ht(t0(W))H0(x(W)). Freezing makes these NEW pair comparisons legal; it does not set alpha_j=1.

At R=0 choose lambda_t=lambda_0=v=1/2, so alpha=exp(-1/2). Since (H(t0)H(x))12=.1+.01 Ct, the original minus newly heated averaged product has

    gap12=.01(1-alpha) E Ct,
    E Ct>=L[exp(-q^2/2)-beta]=0.5904236146...,
    gap12>=0.0023231359... >0.002.                       (18)

This is an exact same-potential failure of target preservation under a finite legal frozen-label construction. It concerns independent ADDITIVE heat in the physical primitive query arguments. Heating the entire W and recomputing nonlinear t0 is a different operation and must not be assigned (17).

For this one smooth fixed source, the new heat can tend to zero and restore the target. That limiting observation is not a finite uniform restoration theorem over the admitted bounded-Hessian class. In particular, at frozen x=t0=0, (17) gives exactly (T+alpha_t delta N)(T+alpha_0 delta N) instead of (T+delta N)^2. For any positive externally selected width, increasing k while retaining (10) can keep k lambda large. Bounded Hessians supply no uniform modulus for this factorwise restoration. This is the pointwise primitive interface limitation, not a claimed lower bound against all integrated weak restoration schemes.

To select E H at width lambda with a small primitive radius rho, the actual source is (rho/lambda)g(y+lambda z), or its centered version. The first in z is <=rho, but the known VALUE and caller readout is rho/lambda. All finite floors, anchors and caller paths therefore retain this amplification. Zero-width primitive heat is not a gratis admitted pair call.

### A known weak restoration escape, and the specific K3 premises still needed

There IS an existing weak comparison which restores an additionally smoothed ancestor Hessian without any pointwise Hessian modulus. The source-qualified results in p-shear-heat/HIDDEN-GRADIENT-ANCESTOR-VARIANCE-PRESERVING-HEAT.md and r-shear-consumer/HIDDEN-VECTOR-ANCESTOR-SOURCE-CURRENT-AND-RETENTION.md treat an independent full terminal Gaussian Y and an independent ancestor z entering the terminal only through a U g0(z). The native K3 geometry does not automatically supply those same premises. For K0 at a zero whole-clock root, condition on x=cS+sU. Then S=cx+s vY with Y independent standard, and

    t0=cx+s vY+a M g0(x).

There is a full terminal Gaussian, but the derivative of its center in x contains cI+a M H0(x), with an order-one cI term. The cited derivative cut no longer retains its small-a gain unchanged. For K1 the transformed ancestor is x1=cS+sU+a M*Delta(S,Z). For M=I and fixed Z at a zero clock root, its actual conditional S density is proportional to

    exp[-|S|^2/(2v^2)
        -|x1-cS-a Delta(S,Z)|^2/(2s^2 v^2)].

This follows from U=(x1-cS-a Delta(S,Z))/s; the U-to-x1 Jacobian is the constant s^d. In general this is a nonlinear shear law, not the independent terminal Gaussian in the cited source. One must establish the law and its legitimate weak derivative/current, rather than reuse the independent-source coefficient estimate by chronology alone.

A sufficient new rule would construct an actual finite original-VALUE selected skew source Ctilde on the common roots, prove its exact heat law, and control its difference from the actual K3 Clead AFTER contraction with the intact whole-source mark. For example the positive clock sum of that marked discrepancy must have an explicitly paid bound smaller than kappa e at the desired grade, together with the real input-to-matrix-Hilbert root derivative frame used by the ensuing Price/Riesz current. All primitive widths, center/anchor firsts, known fills, source restorations, and source-zero/retained rows are then charged. A weak comparison can legitimately provide this without a Hessian modulus. The two primitive marginal pair theorems and a decision to average roots later do not themselves provide it.

## 8. Queries, first-only execution, and honest finite cost

Raw source cost. Each generic complete (2) occurrence costs six original gradient VALUES. Each changed heat/sign/filter/finite-version record rebuilds those six sites. A paired target evaluates both branches in this same record. Two independent complete word banks cost twelve original VALUES before certified exact aliases, not six terminals pasted onto one cached random ancestor. Fixed caller-only zero/anchor nodes may be shared by exact semantic keys.

Explicit order-two packet. The literal unoptimized list (8) has two D differences (four primitive source calls), K+1 filter-node calls and one shared filter baseline: K+6 primitive gradient VALUES for one uncentered affine primitive source. If the zero baseline cancels algebraically under sum w_j=0, that exact query can be omitted. Centering adds only genuinely required moving-zero nodes, with identical anchors cached at that one caller. A terminal order-two seed has its own K_T+6 calls and its own private tapes. For an attempted A-then-T packet, the unoptimized primitive bill is (K_A+6)+(K_T+6), plus every separately executed intact K3 mark/source and its anchors. The marked six-query (2) occurrence can share pstar only if it is literally the recorded ancestor node with the same point, tape and source version. That exact sharing creates the observer edge; it does not legalize its comparison.

General finite source bill. If the source interface is the complete six-query K3 rather than one affine primitive leaf, charge six original calls per complete source occurrence in the fixed finite pair/filter transcript. At fixed order, let n_A,n_T and n_joint be its ACTUAL complete occurrence counts. A conservative bill is Q_captured+6(n_A+n_T+n_joint)+Q_separate_anchors, with exact same-record aliases removed only after checking complete keys. LOW30's polynomial-logarithmic occurrence count is an admitted finite count, not an inverse-width empirical bank count. There is no numerical total for an unspecified all-rank queue.

FIRST and adjoints. Save every original primal query. One forward direction of (2) uses one original HVP at each of the six active sites. One reverse sweep aggregates cotangents at shared sites before one transpose action per required site. The selected Hessian matrices in this note are analytical proof labels only. No HVP output is used to manufacture a new VALUE source, and no derivative of an HVP is used. A fresh direction costs its real sweep; discarded primals incur same-tape VALUE replay. The primitive-pair sources (6),(9) have first <=rho_A,rho_T; their center/caller firsts include their real inverse-width readouts and all q3/q4 paths.

Native first and mark. The original source retains |E|<=ra^2 epsilon |Z|, full first <=3r and the stated leading curl <=5ra under the pinned native bounds. The present matrix-target discrepancy is not represented as an executed E-marked residual. Neither a coupling discrepancy nor a wrong matrix target is an executable original source. Any proposed correction must supply its own actual energy, zero/anchor, complete first/caller and retained-graph certificate.

Widths and floors. Every finite rho/v and rho/lambda normalization, positive variance share, known M/J_M/L endpoint action, and later physical ra insertion is part of the error budget. An absolute numerical floor is not absorbed into a vanishing E mark. Known fills are retained. Postponing owned-root currents is allowed, but their eventual same-record source and incoming-first bill remains; it does not change (12),(16) or (18).
