# Retain the mixed covariance field; do not replace the endpoint by its mean

New bounded extraction and obstruction, 2026-10-04. This is progress on Candidate A, not an all-rank no-copy theorem.

## Result first

The existing small-full-gradient mixed program already has a useful stronger intermediate interface: before its owned-root mixture step, it is a positive **root-retained conditional covariance reserve**. For a literal finite clock list its selected field is

    S_J(R)=sum_j w_j Sym[J_E,j(R) J_H,j(R)*],

and the reference covariance is v_out I-S_J(R), with the SAME retained roots R. Its one-energy comparison error is

    Lambda A^(3+5g/2)e + N_ret,2,

its physical residual energy is Lambda A^(1+3g/4)e+N_energy,p, its complete physical residual first is Lambda A^(2+3g/4), and its canonical physical caller is Lambda A^(3/2+3g/4). Retained identity outputs have first one; they are not included in the small residual radius. Its extra original-query/first-sweep exponent is exactly **b=0** at fixed target, for a supplied clock with fixed-order polynomial-logarithmic cardinality and inverse output shares, and the admitted gradient compiler.

This extraction is valid under an observer of the retained roots, because the coupling keeps the roots identical. Replacing S_J(R) by its expectation first is not valid under that observer. The companion note gives an explicit smooth admissible-descendant example with joint defect Theta(kappa e), even though the unobserved marginal defect is O(A kappa^2 e).

The complete K3 endpoint remains open. A new exact SAME-potential witness below has T0=T1 and A0!=A1, so the common auxiliary mode is identically zero at its actual selected record while the full leading matrix word is nonzero and nonsymmetric. The new conditional reserve cannot be substituted for that missing word without proving that the actual ambient endpoint current is its exact same-root S_J field. No such identification is supplied by the existing pins.

## 2. A reusable retained-field rule

### Inputs and ownership

Use the exact source type of the small-full-gradient mixed return:

    E:R^n -> R^d, full first <=Lambda A,
    recorded square-lift curl <=Lambda kappa,
    actual centered Lp energy e_p,
    kappa=A^(1+g), 0<g<=1;
    H=B G, BB*=I, G an actual genuine-gradient VALUE source,
    full first(G)<=Lambda kappa,
    actual original caller(G)<=Lambda kappa/sqrt(A).

E and G are evaluated on the same complete padded Gaussian source tape. P, the marked source's physical coisometry, need not equal B. Neither is inferred from a physical-row bound alone.

Fix a literal finite positive clock list (w_j,c_j,v_j), c_j^2+v_j^2=1. All coarse roots and originally external labels are exposed BEFORE fresh private banks. Fine banks used in a product remain independent conditional on the retained roots. One may use a supplied common root across clock nodes, or a supplied tuple of independent coarse roots; the selected field records whichever is actually used. Conditional execution banks at different nodes are independent. Every coarse-root alias remains in the returned tuple. It is not valid to retain fresh fine records which the pair theorem integrated out.

Assume the usual admitted finite clock sums and output allocations, in particular sum w_j <=Lambda, sum w_j/v_j<=Lambda, and the weighted first/error sums from the original source theorem. For a new clock list, these are input gates to verify, not free normalization. To claim b=0, additionally require clock cardinality and inverse positive output shares to be fixed-order polynomials in the actual public logarithms. Arbitrary finite lists whose sizes scale like A^(-k) do not satisfy this cost gate.

### Literal program

Put

    nu=g/4, rho=A^nu, alpha=rho/kappa=A^(-1-3g/4), mu=A.

At node j use the exact eta=1 child

    g_j(z)=alpha/(s0 v_j) [G(c_j R_j+v_j z)-G(c_j R_j)].

Execute the admitted fixed-order genuine-gradient pair with its known B lift and Gaussian completion. Its selected coefficient is alpha J_H,j(R_j)*. This is an analytical reference; no Hessian action is used as a VALUE.

    f_j(z)= -w_j/(2 b_j c_out,j alpha s0 v_j)
                P* [E(c_j R_j+v_j z)-E(c_j R_j)].

Return the same literal positive two-pair sum

    Y_j=b_j P0,j+c_out,j P P^v(f_j;Q_j)+zeta_j Z_j.

The positive output shares omega_j=b_j^2+c_out,j^2+zeta_j^2 are fixed numerical allocations by the number of nodes, not inverse small w_j or v_j. Add their outputs and the same fixed untouched Gaussian reserve. Return (R,Y). **Do not perform the final owned-root covariance-to-expectation comparison.**

The child comparison is applied through the parent's ACTUAL incoming first. The parent comparison retains its input, and the coupling is extended to P0,j by its exact conditional Gaussian reverse law. This is the same finite circuit and proof sequence as the pinned theorem. At each node the two self-products cancel in the ideal joint pair covariance. Thus the joint conditional reference is

    (R, Z_R),    Law(Z_R | R)=N(0,v_out I-S_J(R)),
    S_J(R)=sum_j w_j Sym[J_E,j(R_j) J_H,j(R_j)*].

The reference square root is analytical only. The executing circuit never forms the random S_J or its square root. Since ||S_J||op<=Lambda A kappa and v_out contains a fixed numerical gap, the conditional reference is positive for the existing small-A guard.

If exact centering is required, use two independent COMPLETE private executions conditional on the SAME exposed R and take their difference divided by sqrt(2), with the corresponding numerical output allocation. This preserves the conditional covariance and costs a factor two. Do not independently redraw R and then claim the old root has been retained.

### The marked mixed-residual branch has a genuine fixed-scale rank gain

The preceding conservative re-admission gives a bounded typed closure for THIS offspring, which should not be confused with closure of the ambient graph.     e_(m+1),p <= Lambda_m A^(1+3g/4) e_m,p+N_m,p.

    T_(m+1)(A) <= p_m(L) T_m(A)+q_m(L) Q_H,m(A),

This closes the marked mixed-residual TYPE and its energy-grade descendants. It does not say that a law-comparison discrepancy is an executable residual VALUE or that applying this iteration cancels the original endpoint defect: the residual is a particular returned program, whereas the remaining comparison errors and covariance-current identities retain their distinct roles. ### An exact conditional-Gaussian consumer, when actually supplied

### Literal finite field versus a continuum endpoint

Equation (1) targets exactly the finite supplied S_J. It does not silently upgrade a quadrature theorem for E S_J into an approximation theorem under every retained-root observer. To use a continuous-field endpoint, additionally prove its error in the appropriate retained-field norm and charge it in N_ret.

A useful necessary cutoff adjustment can be stated without such an upgrade. For a common Gaussian coarse root, Bessel gives ||J_E(t,R)||_(L2;HS)<=e/sqrt(1-t), and ||J_H||op<=kappa. Therefore the final continuous-clock strip 1-t<v_min^2 has L2 field norm at most 2 kappa e v_min. To fit the sharper retained grade in (1), choose

    v_min <= C A^(2+3g/2),

rather than the coarser A kappa=A^(2+g) sufficient for the owned-root grade. This prices the tail only; every finite quadrature/interpolation error under the retained observer still has to be supplied. A dyadic clock uses only logarithmically many width scales, but the actual small widths remain in first, precision and domain ledgers.

    chi(u)=exp(1-1/(1-u^2)) for |u|<1, and 0 otherwise,

## 5. The remaining graph and all currently exposed offspring

The smallest current graph still has the following honest gates:

1. E -> ambient G on Gamma. Six VALUES and bounded ambient first; Gamma is nonlinear. Pulling the full gradient back introduces HVP-valued companions and uncontrolled next jets, so it is not an admitted closure edge.
2. Common auxiliary heat -> E-marked terminal-difference channel. Valid, but the terminal-flat witness proves it does not cover the terminal-sum/ancestor-difference word.
3. Opposite heat -> unmarked baseline B0 plus common mode. At Z=0, its baseline has energy at least 2mra sigma ||M||HS. It cannot be deleted with an actual-E factor. Literal subtraction gives F_minus-B0=F_plus(-V), losing the new word. Its joint current with the ancestor difference must remain.
4. Variance-preserving rotation -> known Gaussian defects and G_lambda. G_lambda is a valid small full-gradient source. The rule in section 2 can return its EXACT finite-clock mixed field while preserving its coarse roots. The whole-source energy and all numerical floors remain.
5. Ambient endpoint -> the fields needed to cancel its actual nonlinear same-root current. This identification has NOT been proved. Constant cross-covariance bounds cannot supply it; the independent observer lower bound is quantitative.
6. The completed mixed reserve -> a small physical VALUE residual with energy gain 1/alpha and curl at most 2 Lambda ell. At unchanged declared A,kappa this satisfies the old near-gradient envelopes because ell/kappa=A^(1-g/4); no inverse-heat copies are needed to re-evaluate it at fixed rank. It is not an original gradient child. Deletion still requires actual energy plus floor to fit the caller allowance, and reusing it inside the same observed endpoint still requires its literal retained-root/ownership contract.
7. Numerical/clock/source errors -> absolute additive floors, each selected before execution at its actual width and propagated through actual incoming first. An unchanged absolute floor is not a positive rank gain.

In particular the state "unmarked opposite baseline with nonlinear ancestor constraint" returns to the same constrained-source type when lifted again. No decreasing auxiliary counter or strict rank gain has been proved for that return. Merely appending the valid lambda reserve does not remove this self-cycle. It is therefore not legitimate to put a finite M_Q into the Candidate A recurrence on the strength of the present modules.

The next sufficient rule must either (a) express and cancel the complete ambient endpoint current as exact root-retained finite covariance/selected-word fields, including the opposite baseline and all companions, or (b) admit that baseline/constraint body with a genuinely improving complete returned-source contract. The present note gives the reusable conditional mixed field for (a), and exact tests which any proposed completion must pass. It does not claim an impossibility theorem against all such completions.
