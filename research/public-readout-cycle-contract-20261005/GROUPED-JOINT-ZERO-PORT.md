# A retained-bank grouped zero port, and its non-exportable physical roots

2026-10-05. A new architectural input type, relative to the sealed fixed-order original-VALUE native contracts. This is not a stronger interpretation of an already completed marginal comparison.

## Result

There is a concrete positive joint zero-subtree port in the permanent-leaf family. It preserves the complete external record E, including the original outer caller y, every pre-existing physical public, and the literal coefficient bank B. It uses a fresh group of native dynamic tapes, and exports one grouped physical readout. Conditional on E, its output can be made Gaussian to any fixed power alpha^P, with strictly increasing surplus in its correction queue. No derivative or Gaussian Riesz operation in B is used in this law proof.

An actual instance starts with two copies of the sealed eight-force C2/cubic-core producer, on independent new complete dynamic tapes, with opposite root signs and the same literal B and original clocks. The main alpha^8 coefficient cancels pointwise in B. The first remaining conditional coefficient has order alpha^12 and surplus eight. Its native repairs have d'=h d, h>=2. Every original old root remains in the executed record and is retained jointly through E.

The restriction is substantive: the group's own marked physical publics and pair-private/side tapes are INTERNAL, consumed through their declared grouped readout. They are not individually exported after being averaged. A recursive caller that reads one of them does not satisfy this input type. A source-valid retained-public counterexample below proves that an independent fresh packet and a Gaussian change of variables cannot automatically fix that stronger contract.

## 7. Exact boundary of the improvement

This construction is suitable for a newly grouped terminal assembly whose internal marked publics have no downstream individual use. It retains all PRE-EXISTING external publics and coefficient labels jointly. It does not by itself upgrade the old six-packet to an all-order normal form: that packet has same-grade decorated public-tilt partners outside the pure permanent-leaf family. It also does not turn the sealed eight-force counterpacket into an all-order joint repair of an already exposed old physical mark.

In a recursive compiler, the required new type rule is: expose the grouped output and declared external E; never later promote an integrated internal root to a fresh coefficient caller. If a history needs that root later, declare it external BEFORE deriving the local current and prove the larger conditional current family. Fresh banks alone cannot change that required joint target. The companion obstruction proves this is a mathematical limitation of the proposed transform, not bookkeeping pessimism.
