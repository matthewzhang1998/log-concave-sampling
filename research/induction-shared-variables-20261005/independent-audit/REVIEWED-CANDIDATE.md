## 3. Exact degree-eight tensor, including both rank-six terms

    lambda = alpha^4 ell,       ell = known factor * u w^2/t_c^4,
    t_c^2 = sigma_c^2 + h^2 m/32.

    V0 = lambda F_B(theta,theta; P+a theta,Q+b theta,S,T).

Since S,T are independent and centered, E V0=0. Therefore the COMPLETE degree-eight coefficient is

    C8(theta) = (lambda^2/2) E[V0^2/lambda^2].

Equivalently, in two copies of the four-force tensor, contract the S slots and T slots by identity, and contract the two P and two Q slots respectively with

    I + a^2 theta theta*,       I + b^2 theta theta*.

The four terms are:

2. T10, rank six: a^2 theta theta* in the P slots, identity in Q.
3. T01, rank six: identity in P, b^2 theta theta* in Q.
4. T11, rank eight: both theta theta* terms, with coefficient a^2 b^2.

The complete physical tensor is symmetrized only after this labelled contraction. Different histories are combined only under an explicit multiplicity convention. In the scalar unit-tensor specialization this reads

Why no other degree-eight term is missing: the first cumulant requires BOTH side shifts, hence starts at alpha^6 and is exactly the intended six-mark current. In the second cumulant, replacing a side pairing by its two shifts costs alpha^2; subtracting the first cumulant squared costs alpha^12. Three or more four-force spine copies have at least alpha^12. Thus the next possible intrinsic degree after the displayed alpha^8 terms is alpha^10. Native finite means and source/pair errors are separate numerical floors, not parity cancellations.

## 10. Exact unresolved port

The theorem enlarges conditional closure from unmarked centers to all direct-public decorations of those centers, and repairs the complete first tight rank-four/six/eight degree-eight family. It does not remove unrelated degree-eight families from other old histories or previously retained degree-nine old-bank remainders.
