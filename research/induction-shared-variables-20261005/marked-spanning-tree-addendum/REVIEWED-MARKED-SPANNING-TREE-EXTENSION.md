# Marked-spanning-tree extension: tensor cuts, native producers, and conditional surplus

2026-10-05. New extension submitted for separate audit after the decorated alpha-ten theorem was sealed. The algebraic and graph statements below have explicit proofs. The full old-bank heat/observer induction is still not claimed.

## 1. A broader force-graph invariant

Let G be a finite connected loopless force multigraph. Every vertex v is a literal normalized privately heated original-gradient coefficient of total valence k_v+2, hence executable by a finite C_(k_v) selected source after choosing two selected slots. External legs are physical theta slots; an internal edge contracts two distinct force occurrences. All original labels, private heats, scalar clocks and affine coefficient callers are retained.

Assume G has a specified spanning tree T such that EVERY graph-theoretic leaf of T has at least one external physical leg. Call (G,T) a marked-spanning-tree certificate. A single-vertex graph is permitted separately when it has at least two external legs. Vertices may have physical marks and additional edges simultaneously. In particular a formerly C0 physical leaf may become a marked C1 vertex incident to a bank bridge.

This condition is broader than one protected C0 leaf per center. It does not require every force vertex to carry a physical mark. Self-loops are excluded.

## 2. Global proper-cut theorem by an acyclic tensor circuit

Suppose every proper cut of the local normalized tensor T_v has operator norm at most c_v. Then EVERY proper cut of the full contracted tensor T_G has operator norm at most product_v c_v.

Proof. Fix any nontrivial partition of the external legs into inputs and outputs. Assign each input a strictly positive source mass and each output a strictly positive sink mass, with equal total masses. The masses can be chosen so that the signed total mass on either component of every tree edge is nonzero: the forbidden equalities are finitely many proper hyperplanes of the positive balanced mass cone.

Here is why none is the whole cone. Each component of T after removing an edge contains at least one graph-theoretic tree leaf, hence an external leg. Thus the component's external-leg subset is neither empty nor the entire external set. Its signed-mass functional cannot be a scalar multiple of the one total-balance relation. A generic point of the positive cone avoids all the hyperplanes.

The unique tree flow with those boundary sources/sinks has nonzero flow on every tree edge. Orient each edge in its positive-flow direction. At every force vertex conservation holds when physical input/output flows are included. Consequently there is at least one incoming and at least one outgoing index at that vertex. Its resulting tensor flattening is therefore a PROPER local cut.

A directed tree is acyclic. Choose any topological order of these oriented tree edges. Orient EVERY non-tree edge from the earlier vertex to the later vertex in this same order. Parallel edges cause no problem. These additions preserve acyclicity and preserve each vertex's already established incoming and outgoing index. Self-loops would fail this step, which is why they are excluded.

Regard every local tensor as the operator from all its incoming to all its outgoing indices. The full tensor contraction is now an acyclic tensor circuit from the chosen physical inputs to the chosen physical outputs. Execute vertices in topological order, tensoring each local operator with identities on the other live wires. Each step has operator norm at most c_v; wire permutations have norm one. Their composition has norm at most product_v c_v. This is exactly the desired global flattening. No self-trace or hidden Hilbert factor appears. QED.

The earlier worry that a long selected spine can contain both endpoints of an opened edge is therefore not, by itself, a proper-cut obstruction for a marked-spanning-tree graph. One must orient the WHOLE tensor circuit for the chosen cut, rather than bound that self-trace in isolation.

## 3. One-Hilbert theorem

Suppose one chosen local tensor has Hilbert norm at most h_v sqrt(D), and all other local proper cuts have the c_w bounds. Root T at a marked tree leaf. Start with that root tensor in Hilbert norm. Attach force vertices in a parent-before-child order, contracting ALL edges to the already built set when a new vertex is added.

The contracted-index set is nonempty because its tree parent is already present. The remaining-index set is also nonempty: an internal tree vertex has a not-yet-added tree child, and a tree leaf has a physical external leg. Thus every attachment uses a proper local cut and costs its operator norm, not another Hilbert factor. The finished graph has Hilbert norm at most sqrt(D) h_root product_(v != root) c_v.

This proof does not need a special two-force chunk. The root can be any marked leaf whose local full Hilbert bound is available. The normalized original-gradient C_k source bounds provide it at every fixed k.

## 4. Native VALUE realization

Choose a marked tree leaf as the genuine root. Select one path from it to another marked tree leaf as the root spine. At each off-spine branch choose a downward path to a marked tree leaf as a side spine, continuing recursively. This finite decomposition partitions all force vertices into selected spines.

Every spine terminates at a physical slot. At a branching vertex the other tree children occupy external source-probe ports. Every nontree edge gets its own independent D-Gaussian color, occurring once at each endpoint. Every physical leg not used as a spine endpoint gets its own independent physical public and its positive readout. A marked vertex that is internal to T can thus have a direct physical probe as well as selected tree slots; it is not forcibly kept as a selected endpoint.

At each vertex two slots are selected, leaving exactly k_v external ports. The original tensor is symmetric in its derivative/output slots because it is a privately heated gradient tensor. Choosing different selected slots is therefore the same native C_(k_v) tensor target with its exact scalar adapter normalization. It is not rerooting a completed LAW or reading a formerly integrated input: the complete original-gradient VALUE graph is freshly built with this selected-slot choice.

Execute all side subtrees on independent complete private tapes in the admitted chronological comparison order. Cut-edge Gaussians and external old coefficients are retained until their prescribed averaging. Use the fixed finite C_k VALUE adapter, filters, calibrated native pair, and positive covariance gap at every source. No tensor entry, higher original derivative or conditional-expectation oracle is queried.

Conditional on all old coefficients and all cut colors, an unshifted reverse side output is standard Gaussian and centered. Hence the first root cluster must insert each side spine once. Direct physical colors occur once and contribute their theta shifts. Each cut color occurs twice globally and has its unique first-cluster pairing. The first coefficient is exactly the target force graph.

## 5. Complete conditional offspring preserve the certificate

For any fixed connected nonmain cumulant, take all copies of the root spine and the side spines actually inserted by the finite reverse recursion. Before Gaussian pairings, each root-spine copy plus its inserted sides is a TREE of force occurrences. Every leaf of that tree is a selected spine endpoint and retains its physical theta slot. A missing side subtree contributes no force vertex; it cannot create an unmarked tree leaf because the parent remains on its selected path.

Gaussian pairing only ADDS edges between existing force occurrences. Direct physical ports can be consumed, but they are not the protected selected endpoints at those tree leaves. A local cut/public color occurred at most once at each original force vertex, so no self-loop on one force occurrence is generated. Repeated source labels in different copies are distinct occurrences.

Connected cumulants retain graphs whose root-spine argument trees are connected after the added Wick edges. Contract each argument tree to a node, choose a spanning tree of the resulting connected quotient, and then restore each entire argument tree. The union is a spanning tree of the offspring force graph. Its graph-theoretic leaves were leaves of an argument tree and still have their physical marks. Internal Wick edges and unused quotient edges remain nontree edges.

Thus the marked-spanning-tree certificate survives every complete conditional/auxiliary/public cumulant. No assumption that a complete old side subtree survives is used.

## 6. General root-heavy surplus

Let the target scalar coefficient be

    alpha^A H product_v z_v,       A=beta N+d,       d>0,

where N is the number of original force occurrences, beta>0, and z_v are the exact known scalar clock/shield factors assigned to their own occurrences. Put amplitude alpha^beta z_v at every nonroot force and alpha^(d+beta) H z_root at the genuine root. The product is exactly the target coefficient. Signs and inverse readout normalizations belong in H.

If the root spine has ell_0 forces and its nonmain cluster uses h root-spine copies, while the inserted side spines contribute a total of J force occurrences, then

    N' = h ell_0 + J,
    A' = h(beta ell_0+d)+beta J = beta N'+h d.

Hence d'=h d>=2d for h>=2, independently of tree topology, spine length and derivative orders. The center/leaf specialization takes beta=1/2, N=2n and reproduces alpha^(n+d).

For a declared private heat floor tau=alpha^gamma and max k_v <= K, suppose actual nodewise factors satisfy |z_v|<=Lambda tau^(-k_v) and their required one-hit/clock sums are separately verified. If delta=beta-gamma K>0, every nonroot source has positive radius degree at least delta. The effective coefficient degree is at least delta N+d. A fixed output cutoff P bounds both N and d; the root surplus doubles at each repair, so the conditional repair queue is finite.

This is a source/queue statement for already specified heated currents. It does not silently restore an unsmoothed coefficient target or assert that choosing a heat floor solves its target mismatch.

## 8. What this removes and what still blocks the full old bank

A Gaussian bank-covariance bridge connecting two SEPARATE certified graphs adds one derivative-hit slot at each chosen original force and joins those slots by an edge. Keep both old spanning trees and this new bridge edge. The result is a marked spanning tree, even when a hit was at a protected C0 leaf. Such a hit changes its local source to C1 but does not remove its physical mark. Repeating bridges according to a spanning tree of separate copies gives the same conclusion. Thus marked-leaf bank bridges are NOT an intrinsic topology/all-cut dead end under this larger invariant.

This does not yet prove a complete old-bank iterative sampler. Still required are:

1. Exact positive simultaneous Gaussian disintegration for each higher shared-bank cumulant/derivative history, preserving original joint heat and every old readout.
2. Actual clock and one-hit bounds for the resulting higher C_k factors; or a finite smoothing-target restoration at the required grade. Finite derivative existence alone does not pay those errors.
3. A source-qualified finite census showing that ALL mixed old/new and physical-tilt currents have such loopless certificates and no amplitude-free observer defect. A nonzero physical readout in an integrated coefficient bank has the previously proved same-grade infinite-rank obstruction.
4. A well-founded combined queue. Pure conditional returns double surplus; mixed old/new returns may have one new root copy, so they require an additional strictly positive old contribution proved from the actual source graph. It cannot be inferred from a terminal LAW coefficient.
5. The true target's remaining mean, covariance, normalization and endpoint composition debts, with complete finite costs.

The new theorem supplies a genuine broad native graph and conditional-induction port. It is not a claim that the five remaining analytical/observer obligations are automatic.
