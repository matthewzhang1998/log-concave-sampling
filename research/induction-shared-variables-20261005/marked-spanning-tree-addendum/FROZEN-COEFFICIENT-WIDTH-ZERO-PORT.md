5. Finite native/value/filter/gate/mean/root errors are retained as separate propagated floors, or are chosen small enough at the actual requested grade. A fixed previously executed approximation is not automatically upgraded.

## 3. Precision qualification

A side-source approximation with fixed error delta_side can contribute r*delta_side through its strict root ancestor. It is not intrinsically O(r^2). Likewise a finite mean or first-chaos filtering bias can leave a linear current. These are retained floors and must be chosen so their propagated total meets the intended target.

If nonroot amplitudes merely stay bounded away from zero while r tends to zero, a fixed pair order need not achieve an r^2 floor. The requested native order may then have to increase. In the proposed rank-eight allocation every nonroot amplitude is alpha^(1/14), so an appropriately high fixed order and separately assigned absolute floors suffice at any fixed requested alpha grade. The exact prior remains

    Lambda sqrt(D) sum_v rho_v^b product_(strict ancestors of v) rho_a.

No previous execution is reused at a stronger floor without checking its source version.

The executed original VALUE precision has its real division by A*t_v. Choosing the oracle floor proportionally smaller pays that division. It affects the explicit precision/replay bill and must not be hidden, but is not an extra intrinsic conditional-return width exponent.
