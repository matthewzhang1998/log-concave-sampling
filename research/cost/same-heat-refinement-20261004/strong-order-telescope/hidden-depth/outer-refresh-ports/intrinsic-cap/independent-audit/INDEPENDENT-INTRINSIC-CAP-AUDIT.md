There is a robust endpoint proof which does not require such an assertion. First form the COMPLETE native forward polynomial comparator, including all self/orientation terms and all native same-endpoint cancellations.     ||QT||_HS <= sqrt(d) ||QT||_(output | remaining indices).

The strong root Taylor remainder can be handled directly at the same endpoint. Conditional on its saved matrices, its completion Gaussian is independent; for any remainder operator R(M),
