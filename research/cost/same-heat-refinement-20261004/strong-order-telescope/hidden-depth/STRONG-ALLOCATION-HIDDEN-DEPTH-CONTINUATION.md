## Finding and admission boundary

It uses ordinary strong Monte Carlo accuracy, not the earlier buffered 1/N marginal improvement. No Gaussian covariance correction is needed.

For each fixed maximum order index J and fixed finite hidden depth D, including D proportional to J, the resulting relative source family has

    state law order R_j=j+1/2, j>=1,
    complete adjacent force-pair Lp energy <=Lambda_(J,D,p) sqrt(Dim) A^j,
    complete query exponent <=2(j-1)_+ = (2R_j-3)_+.

The same exponent bounds actual Gaussian dimension after factoring out Dim. The constants, logarithmic degrees, and common small-A guard depend on J,D. This is not a uniform-in-growing-J constant claim, a sublinear-order result, or an admission for every unspecified outer coefficient port.

The construction is relative to the reviewed known-center CW7/quarter/short source, its genuine-gradient-reference and finite-restoration ports, and the **complete causal history port** specified in Section 7. It preserves original aliases and random internal callers. It does not replace a correlated history by independent force terms. An arbitrary legacy history that has not supplied that port is not admitted by this note merely because its terminal law is accurate.

The all-level base should be the cheap refreshed seed described below. Plain CW7 is enough for the weaker first/retained induction, but its stated I+O(sqrt(A)) caller estimate does not alone prove an actual g=1 return at level zero.
