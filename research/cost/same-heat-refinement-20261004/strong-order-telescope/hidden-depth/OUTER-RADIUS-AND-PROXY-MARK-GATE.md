# Outer radius and protected-proxy mark gate

2026-10-04. A concrete next-interface boundary for the slope-two weak-source family. This does not change its emitted sampler, source-query recurrence, or pinned mark2.9 theorem.

Let the terminal physical force radius be r=A^s. Choose a short refresh time h=A^(v/2) and a twin amplitude epsilon=A^e. The known proofs give

    protected variance gamma comparable to A^v,
    projection error <=Lambda sqrt(Dim) r A^(1+3v/2),
    twin error <=Lambda sqrt(Dim) r A^(2+e),
    beta comparable to A^max(v/2,e),
    full gradient radius rho<=Lambda A^[s-max(v/2,e)].

For a requested relative mark J, a sufficient positive-power shrinking-radius condition is therefore

    v >= 2(J-1)/3,
    e >= J-2,
    max(v/2,e) < s.

At the minimal exponents this is

    J < min(1+3s,2+s).

The inequalities are strict for a fixed-order heat-power prior error. At equality the radius has no positive heat power; increasing a fixed correction order does not turn a mere public-logarithmic radius into a prescribed positive heat power.

For the canonical source s=1, mark J=2.9 is allowed. The pinned construction uses v=19/15 and e=.9, giving radius A^.1.

For the old outer accurate/covariance normalization s=3/4, the maximum mark under this tradeoff is strictly below11/4=2.75. The pinned J=2.9 fails because its twin exponent e=.9 is already larger than3/4. Changing the short-refresh v alone cannot fix this.

A lowered proxy mark CAN be supplied within the same slope-two sampler. Keep its existing v=19/15, hence v/2=19/30<3/4 and protected projection grade2.9, but choose epsilon=A^(J-2) with 2<J<2.75. Then

    rho <=Lambda A^[3/4-max(19/30,J-2)]

has a positive heat exponent. For example J=2.6 uses e=.6, has twin grade2.6, and gives full radius A^(7/60). At J=2.7 the radius is A^.05. This merely changes the subsequently constructed proxy/twin and fixed prior order; it adds no inverse-heat source-query exponent and does not alter any empirical bank or posterior sampler. The original finite precision, caller, and common-origin gates are still required.

Whether a particular outer coefficient consumer can work with this lowered mark is its own theorem. Its required approximation/curl/energy grades cannot simply be renamed.

To KEEP mark2.9 at radius A^(3/4), one would instead need to improve the structural tradeoff. For example, if the terminal predecessor row were A^u, its squared twin price would be epsilon A^(2u); choosing e<3/4 and maintaining2u+e>=2.9 would require u>1.075. The current family has only u=1. Another option would be a genuine-gradient embedding with a smaller physical error than A^2 epsilon at the same protected inverse-width price. Neither improvement is proved by the slope-two source construction.

This is the precise next radius/mark interface, alongside the separate growing-order covariance/cumulant and chronological outer-error closure. It is not a claim that the new source-query exponent is the total outer sampling exponent.
