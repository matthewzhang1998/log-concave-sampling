# All-layer martingale currents and the retained path gap

2026-10-04. This is a source/current audit, not a posterior-law lower bound.

## Result

Recursive conditioning does not keep a randomized nonlinear flow unbiased. It gives an exact **paired rank-one feedback / rank-two covariance current**. The first is regenerated by the next original force; the second can remain dimension-coherent even for a quadratic potential. Correcting only the scalar final-layer conditional variance misses both interfaces.

There is also a positive-gap obstruction to a blanket joint-path repair: a finite Gaussian harmonic path has a low-rank carrier. A centered quadrature impulse can inject covariance transverse to that carrier. An unread final Gaussian keep does not create a reserve in earlier retained path coordinates. Thus one cannot subtract the entire path-error covariance from the existing path Gram while retaining all those observers.

These facts isolate the actual reusable gate. They do not rule out a new observer-qualified endpoint compiler that corrects the paired currents and preserves the true posterior law.

## 1. Exact finite-DAG all-layer telescope

Choose one intermediate Picard layer. Freeze the entering caller, every earlier record, every later clock bank, and every other independent primitive read by the remaining graph (including a final K, or average K separately). These frozen primitives are independent of this layer's fresh clock bank conditional on its entering records. Only finitely many path values are read by the remaining graph; gather these into a finite vector. The remaining F_R must have no direct dependency on the selected clock bank beyond that vector. Any raw current-clock observer must be included as an additional compared coordinate/current; it cannot be silently frozen into R while retaining E[D|R]=0.

Write that layer's path vector as q+D, where q is its conditional integral mean and

    E[D|R]=0.

R contains all the frozen earlier and later records, including actual shared labels. The actual remaining VALUE program is a C1 map F_R, made from its finite original-gradient queries, known filters and saved records. Put

    Y_t=F_R(q+tD),  0<=t<=1.

The conditional law error has the exact identity

    E[phi(Y_1)-phi(Y_0)|R]
      = E integral_0^1 V_t dot grad phi(Y_t) dt
        + E integral_0^1 (1-s) Q_s : Hess phi(Y_s) ds,  (1)

where

    V_t=[DF_R(q+tD)-DF_R(q)]D,
    Q_s=(DF_R(q)D) tensor (DF_R(q+sD)D).               (2)

Only the symmetric part of Q_s contributes against the test Hessian. The two vector factors and all source points retain their actual joint record.

### Proof without original third derivatives

Differentiate phi(F_R(q+tD)) once in t. Add and subtract DF_R(q)D, and use

    E[DF_R(q)D dot grad phi(Y_0)|R]=0.

The remaining difference of test gradients is

    grad phi(Y_t)-grad phi(Y_0)
      =integral_0^t Hess phi(Y_s) DF_R(q+sD)D ds.

Changing the order of the two scalar integrations gives (1). This proof uses only C1 of F_R. It does not differentiate DF_R, and it does not treat an original HVP action as a new differentiable VALUE oracle.

For a fully implemented counterpacket, the differences of firsts in (2) still need their own native frozen-child/source contracts. The analytical identity alone does not return such a port.

## 2. What conditional centering does and does not remove

For one subsequent scalar force, F(q)=B-w b(q),

    V_t=-w[b'(q+tD)-b'(q)]D.

C2 bounds b' but supplies no power modulus for its difference. Thus the uniform conditional VALUE estimate is O(|w| a E|D|), not automatically O(E D^2). This is the earlier-layer term in the scalar randomized ledger.

The exact conditional mean defect is

    E[b(q+D)-b(q)|R]
      =E integral_0^1 [b'(q+tD)-b'(q)]D dt.            (3)

### Smooth fixed-caller test

Take q=0, D uniform on [-epsilon,epsilon], and

    b(x)=a[x/2+c(sqrt(x^2+eta^2)-eta)],  0<c<1/4.

Its original Hessian ratio b'(x)/a lies between 1/2-c and 1/2+c, so it is a strict C2 Hessian-sandwich example. As eta/epsilon tends to zero,

    E[b(D)-b(0)]/(a epsilon) -> c/2.

The fresh clock can be realized smoothly as D=epsilon[2 Phi(G)-1], with a bounded first. Thus this is not a discontinuous-sign trick. The conditional rank-one current in (3) is genuinely present despite exact centering of D.

This test is at a fixed caller. It does not show the same lower bound after integrating a nondegenerate Gaussian q against the actual posterior genealogy. Such integration may produce additional cancellation. To use that cancellation, a proposed compiler must retain the actual joint q,D law and every later observer rather than replace (3) by zero.

## 3. Covariance does not become dimension safe by conditioning alone

When F_R is linear, V_t vanishes, but Q_s need not be harmless. A common random quadrature clock in a quadratic problem produces a random scalar covariance shared by all physical coordinates. In high dimension the output radius reveals that random scale, so a scalar conditional N^-3 weak estimate cannot be promoted to a sqrt(Dim) theorem merely by integrating out the clocks.

The exact quadratic Gaussian-mixture formula and radial test appear in `SCALAR-RANDOMIZED-QUARTER-FLOW-AND-ALL-LAYER-DEBTS.md`, Section 7. This already rules out a general inference of the form

    centered clock noise + small coordinate first => dimension-safe weak gain

without an actual covariance/current repair. It is not a lower bound for all calibrated randomized quadratures.

## 4. A zero-Gram direction in a retained path

Consider scalar harmonic positions at three intermediate times t_1<t_2<t_3<T. The base path is

    q(t)=cos(t) X+sin(t) Z,

where X,Z are independent centered Gaussians. Its three-coordinate carrier matrix has rank two.

Now execute one positive randomized impulse with S uniform on [s_-,s_+] contained in (t_1,t_2), selected independently of X,Z. Realize it as S=s_-+(s_+-s_-) Phi(G) on a fresh standard Gaussian clock. For a quadratic force and weight h>0, the emitted path is

    q_S(t_i)=cos(t_i)X+sin(t_i)Z
             -a lambda h sin_+(t_i-S)[cos(S)X+sin(S)Z]. (4)

Here sin_+(r)=sin(r) for r>=0 and zero otherwise. No query is at its kink; a specified known C1 smoothing would leave this finite fixture unchanged. The impulse is an original linear-gradient VALUE. It has actual finite VALUE and first actions with no original-third-derivative issue. Its clock first is proportional to |X|+|Z|; that conditional/Lp row is explicit, and a native uniform global-first admission is not asserted for this unguarded fixture.

Let bar A,bar B be the two coefficient vectors obtained by averaging (4) over S. Its exact conditional mean path is

    q_bar=bar A X+bar B Z.

The centered clock error D=q_S-q_bar satisfies E[D|X,Z]=0 exactly. Choose a unit vector w perpendicular to both bar A and bar B. Then

    Var(w dot q_bar)=0,
    E[(w dot q_S)^2]=E[(w dot D)^2]>0                 (5)

for generic specified times. The associated baseline Gram G has w*G w=0, while the clock-error covariance Sigma_D has w*Sigma_D w>0. Consequently G-Sigma_D is not positive semidefinite. There is no negative covariance reserve inside this baseline carrier for that retained direction.

The joint retained-path W2 distance is at least sqrt(E[(w dot D)^2]), because the target is supported on the plane w dot x=0. This is the strong error scale, despite centering of the quadrature error. The final unread Gaussian K is not observed in any of these intermediate coordinates and therefore supplies no variance in (5).

This example is a finite centered quadrature-source test. Its reference is the exact conditional clock mean of the displayed single impulse, not a claim that this finite mean equals the exact nonlinear posterior flow. It rules out a blanket joint-path Gaussianization step using only the existing harmonic Gram. A tailored final endpoint may discard the offending observer; alternatively a new full-rank path reserve may be introduced, with its law and cost explicitly paid.

## 5. What would close the all-layer gate

A useful next theorem must consume the paired currents (1)--(2), with their same-root Gaussian genealogy and actual future observer. It must return:

- an actual positive endpoint correction, including every regenerated rank-one feedback and covariance/cumulant row;
- a strict positive gain after caller, low-clock, root-observer and inverse-gap losses;
- one-energy sqrt(Dim) bounds, rather than a path-magnitude-square factor;
- a valid positive reserve in every correction direction that is kept observable;
- finite original VALUE/first/adjoint execution without differentiating an HVP child;
- the actual retained/caller/zero/protected ports and literal old-source and clock-query count.

Correcting only conditional means, only scalar final covariances, or only independent final clock banks does not establish this theorem. Conversely, (1) gives a precise common source identity on which to build it. It is a more useful target than asserting that recursive martingale conditioning already cancels nonlinear feedback.
