# Fixed-seed rank corrections and query-sharing audit

Publication copy: nonmathematical provenance wording and the executable input scope were sanitized. Original/public hashes are in `INVENTORY.json`; historical pins identify original source versions.

Date: 2026-10-04. New mathematical audit, not a reconstruction of an old note.

## Result first

The best immediate research target is a **complete fixed-target no-copy local compiler rebuilt from the cheap CW7 seed**. Finite rank-dependent duplication is acceptable; removing every duplication is unnecessary. If the compiler uses only a fixed-target finite number or a fixed-target polynomial-logarithmic number of complete old calls, it has no heat exponent. Starting at c_7=0, even the existing finer-heat terminal preserves c_P=0. There is no separate terminal-cost obstacle in this case.

A second, different target is a **same-heat additive posterior-defect correction**. It could improve an already positive-cost family because its exponent recurrence is c_new=max(c_old,b_P), rather than a heat-dilated recurrence. It must correct bias relative to the true posterior, not only Gaussianize an old source more accurately.

A third idea, **amortizing an expensive common ancestor across empirical banks**, fails as a stand-alone sublinear-cost mechanism even if its statistical validity is granted for free: keeping the current empirical counts gives an optimistic certificate c_P=P−5.4, hence c_P/P→1. Literal stochastic sharing is generally invalid as well. The existing exact-zero and identical-query caches are valid, but do not erase the empirical multiplicity.

No new complete all-order source construction is proved here. The positive conclusions are conditional cost implications. The sharing and signed-averaging counterexamples are exact algebraic obstructions to the specified shortcuts, not lower bounds on arbitrary algorithms.

## 2. Candidate A: fixed-seed finite rank/defect compiler

### Concrete construction target

Freeze a desired local accuracy Q before execution. Build a finite typed correction graph from original VALUE/first-action records of a fixed cheap source, using native common-ancestor, scalar true-Gram, skew-word and constraint-descendant modules only where their actual input contracts apply. Each node has:

1. A rank/defect grade and source type.
2. The actual endpoint/caller, retained labels and independent-record ownership.
3. The complete emitted successors, including bias, coherent self, covariance mixture, true orientation, low-clock tail and numerical calibration.
4. All source heat labels and actual widths/gaps.

A rank is not merely the number of Taylor terms: the grade must increase for every surviving source-qualified row. The type-weight criterion from the reconstructed acceptance note is the correct finite test. For an edge t→u, prove

    q_u+w_u ≥ q_t+w_t+δ,  δ>0,                          (2)

after every real width and caller loss. A genuine finite decreasing auxiliary counter can be included in the type. An unchanged absolute tail is not a positive-gain edge.

Once the graph is closed, its depth M_Q is finite at fixed Q. It may depend very badly on Q. That dependence is allowed in the slow-diagonal goal.

### Exact changed work recurrence

An adequate rank recurrence at unchanged source heat is

    T_(m+1)(A) ≤ p_m(L) T_m(A)+q_m(L) Q_seed(A),        (3)

with computable fixed-target polynomials and no A^(−κ) count. More generally, a finite sum of differently heat-labelled old calls is allowed if those labels are recorded. Expanding (3) for the fixed M_Q gives

    T_Q(A) ≤ C_Q L^(d_Q) Q_seed(A).                    (4)

Even a full tree with nine children per rank only contributes 9^(M_Q), an order-dependent constant. It does not create a heat exponent. A graph with M_Q proportional to log(1/A), on the other hand, would create one; M_Q must be selected from Q and the fixed grade witness, not from A-dependent stochastic convergence.

If a complete no-copy local port replaces the empirical arm in every stage, the existing outer construction has a bound of the form

    Q_R(a) ≤ Λ_R(L) sum_j Q_P(a^(s_j)),
    c_R ≤ S_P c_P,                                    (5)

where S_P is the actual largest inherited exponent in that complete stage. For the first seed stage one may keep its full mode cutoff S_7=5; after the actual g=1 join one may use S_P=R/P. The distinction does not matter for an exact zero exponent:

    c_7=0  ⇒  c_P=0 at every rebuilt fixed order.       (6)

Thus this route does NOT require an independent same-heat terminal theorem. Attaching it only after the present positive-cost family would instead preserve a positive inherited ratio, so the rebuild matters.

### Selectable tiny heat penalties are enough

Suppose the complete local compiler at each fixed target can achieve any selected κ_i>0, with arbitrary computable target/κ-dependent constants. The safe reconstructed bound is

    c_7.5/7.5 ≤ (2/3)κ_0,
    c_(P_i+.5)/(P_i+.5) ≤ c_(P_i)/P_i+κ_i/P_i.        (7)

For any final budget ε>0, take

    κ_0=3ε/4,
    κ_i=ε P_i/2^(i+1),  i≥1.

For any finite selected path,

    c_final/P_final ≤ ε/2+ε sum_(i≥1) 2^(−i−1)<ε.     (8)

Rebuild the selected final orders with ε tending to zero. No claim is made that merely appending lower-cost stages to one already positive-cost family dilutes its old ratio.

### Proved versus open

Proved here: (3)–(8) as cost implications, including the harmlessness of fixed-target whole-program duplication and the special first-stage factor. Proved in the inputs: bounded native modules, zero specialization, matched source/caller contracts where explicitly stated.

Open: a complete generic matrix rank compiler with (2), all successor source contracts, positive variance reserve, and the literal work premise (3). Scalar K3 and a bounded common-ancestor/constraint lift are not that theorem. The matrix same-query jet producer and the constrained endpoint return must remain actual gates.

### Minimal falsification tests

First, extend the scalar nine-bank module by one true even-rank correction. Plain signed mixing cannot do it. If E_i are iid and independent of a Gaussian fill, with real coefficients satisfying sum a_i=1, then

    κ_4(sum a_i E_i+Gaussian)=κ_4(E) sum a_i^4,
    sum a_i^4 ≥ m^(−3).                               (9)

The existing eight coefficients 1/6 and ninth coefficient −1/3 give sum a_i^3=0, but sum a_i^4=1/54. For the smooth scalar test E=A sin G,

    E sin²G=(1−e^(−2))/2,
    E sin⁴G=(3−4e^(−2)+e^(−8))/8,
    κ_4(sin G)=−0.2533595131… .                        (10)

Even grant the exact covariance Gaussian reserve. Its fourth cumulant is still the nonzero A^4 multiple in (9). With uniform sixth moments, the elementary fourth-moment/W2 inequality converts this to a same-order obstruction. To suppress this coefficient by an additional A^δ through sample count alone requires m≳A^(−δ/3). A fixed finite weighted iid average cannot replace an actual fourth-rank counterpacket. This does not rule out dependent nonlinear counterpackets or new sources. If a claimed local port covers every source with the current small-energy/first envelopes, the variant E=A^3.9 sin(G/A^2.9) has energy O(A^3.9), first at most A, and fourth cumulant asymptotic to −(3/8)A^15.6. It therefore gives the same eventual arbitrary-order obstruction inside those envelopes. It is not asserted to be the exact native K3 remainder; a port restricted to that precise source must be tested on its own finite record.

Second, before claiming the generic matrix port, run the exact d=2 same-potential oscillatory K3 witness from MATRIX-K3-SAME-QUERY-TWO-SIDED-WORD-CONTRACT.md. It has a genuine order-ra skew in the length-two terminal/ancestor word. Any proposed rule that only prices hidden-column squares, replaces BB* by B², or independently heats the two original queries fails this test already at the smallest noncommuting word.

## 3. Candidate B: same-heat additive posterior-defect refinement

### Concrete construction target

Keep a complete incumbent R_P at the SAME requested heat a and add a rank-indexed correction program rooted in original source records. The desired full producer is

    R_R(a,y)=Refine(R_P(a,y), original fixed-source packets),

with an actual coupled VALUE realization, preserved old origin/carrier/retained graph, and a proof relative to the true Q_(a,y) at order R. The correction may be implemented as a finite graph; it need not literally be a scalar sum. It must return the complete caller and source interfaces needed by the next use.

The target bill is

    Q_R(a) ≤ Λ_P(L)[Q_P(a)+a^(−b_P)],                 (11)

or, allowing a polynomial-log number of complete incumbent calls,

    Q_R(a) ≤ Λ_P(L)[poly(L) Q_P(a)+a^(−b_P)].

The same-heat restriction means every old call is at a heat comparable to a up to public logarithms. If instead an old call uses a^(η_P), replace c_P by η_P c_P below; that heat is not hidden in Λ_P.

### Exact changed recurrence

From (11),

    c_R ≤ max(c_P,b_P),
    c_(P_n) ≤ max(c_(P_0), sup_(i<n) b_(P_i)).        (12)

Thus bounded b_P gives c_P=O(1), and a sublinear running envelope of b_P gives c_P/P→0, even when the incumbent initially has c_(P_0)>0. This is a genuine way to repair a positive-cost family without rebuilding it from zero. If all packets come from a fixed cheap core with polylogarithmic work, b_P=0.

A finite shared-query implementation is attractive here because an identical fixed source record may feed several correction readouts. Yet independent banks required inside products remain separate. The target does not require unlawful sharing; fixed-target independent duplication is harmless by Candidate A's accounting.

### Proved versus open

Proved: the exponent recurrence (12). Open: the same-heat posterior correction theorem itself. The current native covariance/mean modules target means or covariance actions of actual sources. They do not, by themselves, correct the intrinsic posterior bias of that source family.

The reconstructed growth note mentions an earlier fixed-library posterior Stein-defect proposal. Its original source bytes are not present in the files inspected here. The historical report explicitly says its grouped defect-energy/caller/gain gate was open and that ordinary L2 insertion contraction failed. This audit does not promote that historical proposal to an available module.

### Minimal falsification test

Use the scalar quadratic posterior

    Q_(a,y)=N(y/(1+λa), a/(1+λa)),  λ>0.             (13)

A fixed core whose mean uses t_L(x)=sum_(j=0)^L(−x)^j has the exact error

    t_L(x)−1/(1+x)=−(−x)^(L+1)/(1+x), x=λa.        (14)

Consequently its W2 error is at least

    |y| (λa)^(L+1)/(1+λa).                          (15)

An arbitrarily accurate Gaussian-mean compiler that preserves this core's mean leaves (15) unchanged. The candidate must explicitly correct both the intended mean and the covariance/carrier of (13) at the same heat. This toy case is easy enough to verify exactly; success there is necessary but far from sufficient for the bounded-Hessian nonlinear class.

For the nonlinear extension, test the whole coupled query genealogy on the K3 two-Hessian fixture and keep all caller derivatives. A formal expansion containing unavailable high derivatives under a merely C2 potential hypothesis is not an executable source theorem.

## 4. Candidate C: amortized common-ancestor empirical banks

This is a useful screening calculation, not a recommended stand-alone route.

### The maximally generous recurrence

Grant, without yet proving its law, that a whole old core costs Q_P(A) only once per exposed caller, while each of the N_R(A) residual evaluations costs A^(−b), b fixed. Also grant zero cost for all statistical corrections needed to justify sharing. The optimistic local ledger becomes

    Q_P(A)+N_R(A) A^(−b).                            (16)

Over the suffix interval, the complete exponent is

    c_R ≤ max{(R/P)c_P,
               b+R−β,
               (R/P)b+[R−β(R/P)]_+}.                (17)

For P≥7.5 and 0≤b≤β, the third term is no larger than the second: their difference is (b−β)/(2P). Hence

    c_R ≤ max{(R/P)c_P, b+P−4.9}.                    (18)

For the strongest possible case b=0, start at the first-generation c_7.5=2.1. The selected certificate solves exactly to

    c_P=P−5.4,  P≥7.5,
    c_P/P=1−5.4/P→1.                                (19)

This improves quadratic growth to linear in the optimistic ledger. It still fails c_P/P→0. There remains a linear-in-target empirical count even after the old program has been amortized perfectly. With a positive incumbent ratio already above 1, the inherited ratio floor is still larger.

These are sufficient-certificate recurrences under hypothetical (16), not necessary lower bounds on arbitrary common-ancestor algorithms. They show that *this proposed accounting change* cannot establish the requested asymptotic target. It must additionally replace the empirical law/error envelope or prove an actual rank-dependent residual gain.

### Why naive sharing is not even the granted law

Let E(U,H) have conditional mean μ(U). Independent fine records H_i sharing one ancestor U give

    Cov(N^(−1) sum_i E(U,H_i))
      = Cov μ(U)+(1/N) E Cov(E(U,H)|U).               (20)

Independent COMPLETE records instead give

    (1/N)[Cov μ(U)+E Cov(E(U,H)|U)].                 (21)

The missing term is (1−1/N)Cov μ(U). For E(U,H)=U+H, (20) is 1+1/N and (21) is 2/N. It does not disappear merely because the ancestor's instructions were already executed.

An even simpler buffered example is E=αG. Aliasing the same G in all N slots produces N(0,v+α²), whereas independent records produce N(0,v+α²/N). Their W2 distances to N(0,v) are exactly

    sqrt(v+α²)−sqrt(v),
    sqrt(v+α²/N)−sqrt(v),                            (22)

respectively. The first does not have the required 1/N gain. One may take α=A^3.9 to respect the small-energy envelope; the counterexample is about invalid N scaling, not saturation of the old first-radius majorant.

At covariance-word nodes, the same issue appears conditionally on the coarse root X. Two independent complete fine banks produce E[B|X] E[B|X]*. Reusing a bank produces E[BB*|X]; their difference is the conditional covariance. Even in a scalar smooth example B=1+δ cos G it is nonzero:

    Var(B)=δ²(1−e^(−1))²/2.                         (23)

Finally, caller chronology can fail before covariance is considered. If the exposed caller is θ=tanh G from an earlier computation, reusing that G in F_θ(W)=α θ tanh W has positive mean α E[tanh²G]. A fresh conditional record W independent of θ gives conditional mean zero. Thus the new random record must be sampled after its caller is exposed, or a new joint contract must explicitly account for the dependency.

### What would make conditional sharing legitimate

A proposed new interface could retain U, represent μ(U) by an ACTUAL admitted source, Gaussianize/correct its outer law separately, and correct all joint products via total covariance/cumulance. Then (20) is the correct starting identity. That is a new hierarchical source construction with its own price, not a cache optimization. At minimum it must return μ(U)'s bias, energy, caller, covariance, higher defects and retained chronology. Candidate A or B may eventually provide such a construction.

## 5. Exact reuse that is legal now

A deterministic query may be reused when its full semantic key matches: original provider/version; heat; actual point expression; owned primitive-record identities; captured labels/caller environment; finite numerical precision/version; and the requested first/adjoint action. This includes:

- The same original query shared by multiple readouts on one actual source record.
- The literal F/H common active record in E=F−H when the required version and labels are identical.
- Canonical zero-trajectory evaluations and fixed coefficients.
- A shared deterministic prefix before any branch that was not required to be independent.

The finite VALUE graph and its law are then unchanged. For adjoints, accumulate all cotangents at a shared node before its reverse sweep; for different HVP directions, charge the distinct legal directional actions unless the interface already supplies an appropriately priced combined action. Do not replace many HVPs by an unpriced full Hessian reconstruction.

An empirical bank's distinct primitive-record identities make its keys different, even at the same exposed caller. Different serial/decoder callers likewise produce different keys. Collapsing these is a change in the random program. Protected late records cannot be reused by earlier queries that must ignore them. Rejection/conditioning and privately resampled records also remain under their original ownership rules.

No exact-key reuse found in the reconstructed contracts removes the N_R(A) factor. The existing zero-origin specialization and active-retained-tape savings already remove important duplication but do not cheapen a complete random old-source occurrence.

## 6. Slow diagonal and what this audit does not prove

If a complete outer theorem ultimately yields

    Work_P(D) ≤ C_P (log(D+2))^(d_P) D^(γ_P),
    γ_P→0,

with effective constants and effective fixed-order domain/numerical guards, a slow diagonal can absorb the fixed-order constants. Choose increasing computable thresholds D_n so the chosen order P_n is admitted, γ_(P_n)≤1/n, and

    log C_(P_n)+d_(P_n) log log(D+2) ≤ (log D)/n

for every D≥D_n. Selecting the largest admitted n gives a subpolynomial bound. The threshold must also enforce all heat/radius/moment/precision guards. If a=D^(−1/(2P)), the inner factor a^(−c_P) contributes γ_P=c_P/(2P), but all other outer dimension powers must also be included before claiming the complete theorem.

One cannot hide A^(−κ(P)) in C_P: it depends on the execution heat. By contrast 9^(M_P), enormous computable moments, and fixed-P logarithmic degrees can be absorbed by the slow diagonal once the actual all-order source theorem exists. This is why a finite rank compiler is a meaningful target even without uniform polynomial dependence on rank.

## 7. Recommended next checkpoint

Candidate B is a separate higher-risk path if a same-heat posterior defect producer can be established; its clean max recurrence would also rescue positive-cost incumbents.
