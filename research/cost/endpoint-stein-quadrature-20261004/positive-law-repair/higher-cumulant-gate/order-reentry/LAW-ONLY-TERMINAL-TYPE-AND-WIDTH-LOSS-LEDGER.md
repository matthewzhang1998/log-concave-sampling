# Law-only terminal type and the inverse-width loss ledger

2026-10-04. Alternative typed interface, pending independent review. This is not a new all-order construction and does not weaken the actual raw-source requirements of the audited re-entry lemma.

## 1. Two output types must remain distinct

RAW(theta;W) exports an actual differentiable original-VALUE graph, known carrier/zero/anchors, native source/gradient or curl qualification, and the actual first/caller/retained ports required by its next source compiler.

LAW(theta;W) exports a complete finite positive output kernel at a captured caller theta, with the exact same-input conditional target and full law/current error, finite moment/absolute numerical profiles, complete original query bill, and explicit primitive-record ownership. It may have a large but finite and computable executed caller derivative. A uniform O(A) derivative is not required merely to compare its law through exact reference kernels.

A LAW output cannot be relabeled RAW from a small W2 error. In particular, composing g(LAW) gives a valid original-gradient VALUE and an A-Lipschitz law pushforward, but feeding that composite into a first/curl-sensitive mean compiler requests a new RAW certificate. It is not justified by the LAW target alone.

## 2. Correct law-only suffix comparison

Let K(theta,x) be the exact reference kernel and Khat the executed LAW kernel, with

    W2(Khat(theta,x),K(theta,x))<=e(theta,x),
    W2(K(theta,x),K(theta,x'))<=L(theta)|x-x'|.

At actual entering law mu and comparison law nu,

    W2(mu Khat,nu K)<=||e||_(L2(mu))+L W2(mu,nu).

The local error is evaluated at the ACTUAL input distribution. This is proved by replacing the output at that same input first, then coupling through the exact reference kernel. It requires no derivative of Khat. It does not preserve private records integrated by its local LAW comparison.

Thus a non-reentering accurate terminal correction can have caller first A^(-b_p) Lambda_p without invalidating this comparison. Actual moments and numerical caller profiles must still close at the actual entering laws. An exact-kernel contraction never becomes a claim about the derivative of Khat.

## 3. When an inverse-width factor is only a precision cost

Suppose a known finite caller/row factor is

    B(A,sigma,L)<=C_p A^(-b_p) Lambda_p(L)

on the declared finite clock domain, with fixed-p computable b_p. If it multiplies only an assignable absolute prior/filter/oracle or known-arithmetic error delta, selecting

    delta <= A^(p+b_p)/(C_p Lambda_p)

makes its propagated error at most A^p. Its bit/clock precision logarithm is O_p(log(1/A)+L). If the imported VALUE service achieves that absolute tolerance with public-log work at its admitted radius, this factor adds no inverse-A query exponent. Its actual higher order and all guards must be recorded, not called free.

Examples where such a factor can legitimately be numerical:

- A separately admitted approximate prior pair perturbs a LAW kernel caller; couple at that cut and pay the kernel's actual finite B times the prior error.
- A fixed encoded covariance/root/readout changes a primitive input; propagate the absolute encoding floor through the executed first program.
- A finite mode or source anchor has an already assigned absolute restoration profile, with its correct physical readout and original caller dependence.

A source compiler whose own sample/copy count grows like 1/delta does NOT satisfy the public-log premise; its replication must be charged. A radius below an imported threshold must be checked at the actual normalized source, independently of numerical accuracy.

## 4. What cannot be moved into precision

An intrinsic averaged-law current is not a numerical floor. In particular a bound of the form

    sum_nodes w_node A^m sigma_node^(-r) sqrt(D)

cannot be made A^p by choosing smaller oracle error if that is the actual non-Gaussian/current remainder. If the clock sum is algebraically divergent at the required cutoff, it remains a new source/current correction obligation.

Similarly, large owned-coarse caller derivatives used INSIDE a Gaussian-Riesz/decoupling proof can multiply the actual coefficient energy. That product is an intrinsic law term. Merely changing the output's label from RAW to LAW does not remove it.

This distinction applies to the first rank-six feedback bridge. Its vertexwise private-gradient source radii can remain small while its owned-coarse caller sum loses an additional inverse shield. RAW re-entry is then not supplied. A LAW-only treatment may ignore that derivative for later exact-kernel composition, but still needs a valid grouped Riesz/current estimate for the bridge itself and its descendants. No fake independent vertex shielding is permitted.

## 5. The fixed-raw-core alternative grammar

A legitimate alternative to upgrading every accurate output as a raw source would use:

1. A fixed cheap RAW coefficient core with its genuine original-query first/gradient/curl/retained interface.
2. Positive LAW-only accurate kernels used for main posterior draws, fresh conditional decoder children, and final lifts.
3. A completed current constructor in which EVERY derivative/coefficient request goes either to that fixed raw core or to another explicitly admitted raw primitive. Accurate kernels may appear only at their proved conditional LAW/current ports.
4. Full current-level descendant returns for any operation that marks an accurate coefficient kernel or joins two such kernels. A marginal W2 error by itself does not permit cancellation or reuse of the underlying coefficient.
5. The complete replay recursion, actual heat labels, assignable floors and independent-bank chronology.

The construction must enumerate all graph reads. A single hidden derivative of the accurate output, natural pullback through it, or source-sensitive observer of an integrated private Gaussian invalidates the claimed law-only separation.

Under these premises, a large finite first of an accurate correction may be harmless, and known inverse widths can be absorbed into absolute tolerances as Section 3 specifies. None of those premises is supplied solely by the current cubic packet. The grouped common-Gaussian bridge and same-carrier nonlinear posterior targets remain actual unresolved ports.
