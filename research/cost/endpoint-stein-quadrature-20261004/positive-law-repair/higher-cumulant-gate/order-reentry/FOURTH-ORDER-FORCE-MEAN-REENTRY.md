## 8. Why this does not repeat indefinitely

Suppose hypothetically an endpoint of order p>=3 had exactly the same only-known native bounds: residual first O(Lambda A), residual energy O(Lambda A sqrt(D)). Its force target bias would improve to A^(p+1), but (3.1)-(3.3) would still give curl O(Lambda A^2), near-gradient energy O(Lambda A^2 sqrt(D)), and hence the EXISTING near-gradient compiler would still have an O(Lambda A^4 sqrt(D)) allowance. The supported mean accuracy would be min(p+1,4), not p+1 uniformly in p.

This is a ceiling of this proved interface and estimate, not an impossibility theorem for the source. Choosing smaller padding alone cannot remove its term e_E*kappa_E, which already has order A^4. Claiming curl O(A^(p+1)) from the endpoint law is invalid under C2.

Even at p=3, (5.3) does not resolve the endpoint law current. The prior cubic Gaussianization leaves a one-energy O(A^3 sqrt(D)) current. A next law constructor must actually produce/cancel its connected third-rank contribution, restore every induced lower-rank mean/covariance and sampler-feedback term, preserve a positive independent reserve, and supply a full-law remainder. A formal cumulant, a tensor oracle, or a better Gaussian mean does not satisfy that port.

Thus (7.1) is a complete bounded re-entry cost identity. No c(P)/P->0 conclusion follows from it. The exact first remaining challenge is a source-qualified positive third-rank law packet, followed by a genuinely repeatable improvement of the near-gradient and covariance services.
