If a scalar type weight is needed, the inequality is imposed on effective order plus that type weight, not on a selected leading term. The type list must cover every emitted descendant. This is a proposed sufficient ordering, not proof that all current constructors satisfy it. The required finite-source admission is separate from the combinatorics of a formally connected tree.
