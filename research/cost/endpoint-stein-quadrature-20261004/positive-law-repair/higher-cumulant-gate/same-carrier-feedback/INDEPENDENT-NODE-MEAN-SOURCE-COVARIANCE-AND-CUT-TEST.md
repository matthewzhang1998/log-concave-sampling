# Independent-node mean source: changed covariance, cost, and conditioning test

2026-10-05. Bounded new-program analysis, independent review required before admission. This variant preserves the resummed terminal force. It is not a completed m3 current compiler and does not inherit the common-H covariance receipts.

## 1. New literal source

For a positive interior rule (v_j,tau_j), sum v_j=1 and sum v_j tau_j=1/2, let c_j=sqrt(1−tau_j2). At captured x draw INDEPENDENT D-dimensional H_j and execute

    I_ind(x,H_1,...,H_N)=sum_j v_j g(tau_j x+c_j H_j),
    J_ind(x,H)=g(x−I_ind(x,H)).                       (1)

The shifted terminal g is retained exactly. No conditional mean, covariance, Hessian, or formal coefficient is queried. This is a distinct source from the old common-H packet.

Its conditional mean is still exactly v_Q(x)=sum_j v_j P_(tau_j)g(x). Its actual ports are

    0<=D_x I_ind<=A I/2,
    ||D_H I_ind||op<=A beta_2,
    beta_2²=sum_j v_j² c_j²<=1,
    ||I_ind||_(Lp|x)<=C_p A(|x|+sqrt(D)),             (2)
    ||D_x J_ind||op<=A(1+A/2),
    ||D_H J_ind||op<=A² beta_2.

The moment bound uses positive weights and one physical Gaussian marginal per site; it does not replace the actual N D private dimension by D. All N roots remain in the finite record. At x=H=0 the saved-anchor zero is literal. At fixed nonzero x, I_ind(x,0) and J_ind(x,0) are actual executable origins.

The old conditional decoupling lemma applies with the same x-first bound and the displayed full-private bound. It therefore still gives the admitted THIRD-order mean comparison after outer R1, subject to the same genuine Hermite multiplier tolerance. It does not improve that order or establish a fourth-order law.

## 2. The covariance changes completely

Conditional independence of the H_j gives the exact target

    C_ind(x)=sum_j v_j²
        [P_(tau_j)(g g*)(x)−P_(tau_j)g(x) P_(tau_j)g(x)*].       (3)

There are no off-diagonal clock covariances in (3). The old common-H packet instead has all cross-node covariances, so its receipt cannot be imported. Gaussian Poincare implies

    0<=C_ind(x)<=A² beta_2² I,
    ||C_ind(x)||HS<=A² beta_2² sqrt(D).              (4)

For g(x)=B x with B symmetric,

    C_ind=beta_2² B²,
    C_common=(sum_j v_j c_j)² B²,
    C_true=B²/4.                                    (5)

Thus independent nodes do not repair the covariance by themselves. Even if beta_2 becomes small as a rule gains nodes, the true Markov covariance remains B²/4. Any resummed mean correction must use the NEW difference C_true−C_ind, with its correct own mean and live ancestors.

The aggregate I_ind is generally not a genuine gradient on its full N D private tape under a single output coisometry. Each separate node is a genuine gradient in H_j, and their covariances may be compiled separately with the v_j² weights, but no full-gradient claim for the sum is made.

## 3. Outer finite mean source and complete raw counts

For a positive outer rule (w_i,r_i), put x_i=r_i Z+d_i G and use the SAME N-root bank (H_j)_j across outer nodes:

    F_ind(Z,G,H)=sum_i w_i J_ind(x_i,H),
    B(Z,G)=sum_i w_i g(x_i), E=F_ind−B.              (6)

This sharing preserves the one-variable outer mean identity: E_(G,H)F_ind=sum_i w_i P_(r_i)[E_H J_ind](Z). A new complete mean-compiler occurrence has its own whole bank; it does not share H_j across distinct banks or changed source arguments.

B is a genuine gradient in G. For E, the difference of the two terminal Dg terms in the G block is symmetric; the remaining chain through D_x I_ind is O(A²). Its H_j blocks have norms at most A² v_j c_j. Consequently

    first(E)<=C A, curl(P_G*E)<=C A²,
    captured-Z first(E)<=C A,
    ||E||_(Lp|Z)<=C_p A²(|Z|+sqrt(D)).              (7)

The actual raw private dimension is (N+1)D. Its safe original VALUE counts are

    Q_F=N_out(N+1), Q_B=N_out, Q_E=N_out(N+2).       (8)

A complete gradient/near-gradient OWN-mean compiler has the same fixed-grade source-class admission as before, but with this actual private dimension and these full occurrence counts. Its bill is Q_captured+N_B Q_B+N_E Q_E plus known/numerical/replay work. The fixed-Z origins in (6) are full original-VALUE executions at all private zeros. An inner anchor depending on outer G remains private and cannot be globally cached.

Original HVPs appear only in requested first/adjoint sweeps at every recorded VALUE site. Positive sums preserve numerical absolute floors with total weights one; the terminal feedback multiplies an inner floor by at most A. Any future response widths, inverse heat coefficients or Gaussian covariance encodings have their own absolute precision budget. All versions, rules, origins and tolerances are frozen before differentiation.

## 4. Exact Gaussian direction test for a marked subset

For one outer clock, expose Z=z and write x=r z+q G_0, q>0. Its inner sites are

    Y_j=tau_j x+c_j H_j.

For a subset S of marked indices, the Gaussian directional operator

    A_a=q^(−1)partial_(G0,a)−sum_(j in S)(tau_j/c_j)partial_(Hj,a)

annihilates each MARKED Y_j and changes x by a. Its score is

    S_S=G_0/q−sum_(j in S)(tau_j/c_j)H_j,
    Cov(S_S)=sigma_S² I,
    sigma_S²=q^(−2)+sum_(j in S)tau_j²/c_j².          (9)

At fixed z, this score is Gaussian and independent of the marked site vectors, hence of their original g values/Jacobians. This is a real extra direction that the two-root common-H geometry generally lacks when distinct ratios tau_j/c_j must be preserved simultaneously.

However it does NOT preserve all unmarked force ancestors:

    A_a Y_j=tau_j a for j not in S,
    D_A I_ind=sum_(j not in S) v_j tau_j Dg(Y_j),
    ||D_A I_ind||op<=A/2.                            (10)

Thus the terminal g(x−I_ind) must be replayed through those paths. The resulting feedback cannot be dropped just because it has a bounded first. Formula (9) alone is not a native current-consumer theorem.

## 5. Conditioning on the COMPLETE ancestor record

Two distinctions are essential.

First, if x and all site vectors Y_j are captured, each H_j=(Y_j−tau_j x)/c_j is determined. There are no remaining independent original Gaussian marked directions. For a strictly monotone g, capturing all original force VALUES is already enough to recover the same sites; noninvertibility cannot be used as a uniform Gaussian independence premise.

Second, if only Z and all Y_j are captured but x is left unread, one D-dimensional Gaussian innovation of x does remain. Its exact conditional covariance is

    Var(x|Z,Y_1,...,Y_N)=sigma_all^(−2) I,
    sigma_all²=q^(−2)+sum_j tau_j²/c_j².              (11)

Indeed this is the Gaussian posterior precision for independent observations Y_j=tau_j x+c_j H_j. The all-site score in (9) is sigma_all² times that residual innovation and is independent of every Y_j. It preserves I_ind exactly.

This remaining direction is not uniformly cheap. The closest endpoint node can make c_j² algebraically small in A; (11) has no quadrature weight v_j in its precision sum. Thus preserving ALL force ancestors can introduce an inverse-power score/response factor even though the node count is only polylogarithmic. No public-log first/radius or current bound follows from (11) without an additional weighted cut/cancellation proof.

Before terminal evaluation, at captured x and the UNmarked sites, the marked H_j remain genuinely independent Gaussian directions. A legitimate native response can use that cut only if it executes the complete marked force descendants and the shifted terminal g afterward. Capturing the terminal output first imposes a further nonlinear constraint and does not leave those same Gaussians independent. No completed conditional-law comparison may append the integrated marked roots as observers later.

## Verdict

The independent-node variant is a valid finite VALUE mean source with explicitly changed covariance and complete root/query counts. It supplies positive Gaussian room at selected sites BEFORE their descendants are evaluated. It does not preserve fresh Gaussian marks after the full ancestor/terminal record is captured, and an all-site preservation score has a potentially algebraic precision cost. The resummed mean-current gate remains open; an admissible construction must specify its actual cuts, replay the complete terminal ancestor graph, and prove all resulting current and inverse-width bounds.
