# Tight grade-seven bridge: amplitude ledger and an exact reroot gate

2026-10-05. Bounded candidate analysis, not a closure theorem. All grades below are effective powers of A; original force counts are stated separately. No external tensor or derivative oracle is used or proposed.

## Result first

The six-force/six-mark Price tree has a legal pointwise leading normalization. The tight center-center history has scalar weight beta = w^2 / sigma_2^4, root amplitude proportional to delta^2 A beta, and five A-amplitude nonroots. Its first conditional feedback has **eight forces at effective grade ten**, not twelve forces or two complete six-force colors. Raising the two off-spine side amplitudes from A to A/delta, while compensating the root, raises this eight-force feedback to effective grade twelve. It does not change the main coefficient's own whole-coarse covariance.

There are two distinct remaining facts:

1. The generic one-Hilbert Price certificate for that covariance costs A^14 beta^2 / sigma_2^2. Repeated whole-coarse connections have the ledger A^(7h) beta^h sigma_2^(-2(h-1)). The original endpoint mass is tight and supplies no missing powers. This is an explicit failure of this certificate/pointwise compiler, not proof that the actual current has this worst size.
2. The attractive alternative of promoting the new common-shift probe to a Gaussian channel terminal by literal channel reversal is false. A bounded scalar Gaussian channel gives an exact conditional covariance defect at the square of its amplitude. For an unnormalized cubic difference with delta=A^(1/2), that defect already has grade seven. It survives perfect pair/filter accuracy. A Gaussian channel terminal transposition is valid; a terminal/external-probe permutation is not.

Either port therefore needs a genuinely new grouped conditional-current certificate. LAW-only precision can absorb large executed caller factors multiplying numerical floors, but neither of these intrinsic currents is a numerical floor.

## 1. Exact target and the complete coarse bank

    q = x+sG,

    A^6 delta^2 beta_(v,v'),
    beta_(v,v') = w^2 /(sigma_2^2 sigma_v sigma_v').

For center-center hits, beta = w^2/sigma_2^4. Both old centers now have degree three, one physical mark, and original derivative order three. Their adapters are the two-probe C2 adapters. The ordinary fixed-rank C2 adapter needs its own frozen finite calibration; it is not a request to query D^3g.

The finite Price-clock quadrature and every prior/filter error need separate absolute budgets. No original sigma is enlarged by the common bridge heat.

## 2. Literal pointwise normalization and amplitude redistribution

Use the center-center tree

    L-C-C'-L', with side leaves R at C and R' at C'.

Choose L as output root. The longest root spine has four vertices. All six vertices are physically marked. With positive fixed/logarithmic readout shares, let R6 be their known product. Set

    rho_L = -delta^2 A beta/R6,
    rho_v = A  for the other five vertices.

The one-cluster coefficient is the desired negative A^6 delta^2 beta times the normalized six-tree. Every probability and output variance share is positive. The sign is carried by the actual root source amplitude. On each endpoint panel Delta_2 <= C sigma_2^2, so beta is bounded up to the other positive weights. Its node sum is logarithmic. Thus the private source guard passes in the usual small-A/public-log window.

More generally rescale selected nonroots by delta^-1. Let K be their total number and K_I their number on the four-vertex root spine. Compensate with

    rho_L = -A delta^(2+K) beta/R6.

Every rescaled nonroot still has radius A^(1-p), and every unscaled one has radius A. The main amplitude remains A^(6+2p) beta. The four-force spine amplitude is

    lambda = Lambda beta A^4 delta^(2+K-K_I)
           = Lambda beta A^[4+2p+p K_offspine].

Only a rescaled force OMITTED from that spine improves its feedback scale. Rescaling a force on the spine cancels against the root compensation in lambda. For p=1/2:

- no rescaled side leaves: spine grade 5, first squared-spine grade 10;
- one rescaled side leaf: spine grade 11/2, first squared-spine grade 11;
- both rescaled side leaves: spine grade 6, first squared-spine grade 12.

These first squared-spine terms have eight original forces in all three cases. Calling the last one a twelve-force tree would be wrong.

## 3. Exact conditional offspring: four probes, not an intact-color rule

Condition on the complete old/Price coarse bank. Chronologically execute the two side-leaf channels, then the four-force spine, retaining every external physical public during the appropriate replacements. The ideal polynomial-target reverse symbol has the form

    V(theta)=lambda B(theta)[P+a theta, Q+b theta,
                              S+u(theta), T+v(theta)],

where B is multilinear in its four displayed slots and quadratic in the two endpoint physical theta slots. P,Q,S,T are four independent standard probe banks before their reverse shifts. The shifts a theta,b theta come from the two internal physical marks and carry no force amplitude. The shifts u,v carry the side-leaf amplitudes. This is an exact finite-coefficient identity for the calibrated multilinear polynomial target, not for the literal bounded finite-filter field and not an unbounded producer. The bounded reverse-channel recursion is exact with its actual filtered fields. Replacing those fields by the displayed multilinear target requires separately assigned fixed-order Sobolev/Lp calibration floors, propagated through every product at its actual caller. A finite coefficient filter is not exactly multilinear.

The polynomial target's first cumulant is exactly the intended six-mark main. Its second cumulant contains a nonzero zero-shift term. The scalar specialization is

    V=lambda theta^2 P Q S T,
    (1/2) E[V^2] = lambda^2 theta^4/2.

Thus even with both side crosses zero and before any physical tilts, the eight-force/four-mark current remains. It is a connected graph of two four-force spines joined by four Gaussian contractions, with eight vertices and ten edges: its cycle rank is three. This differs from the single-cycle quartic-repair descendant. Reversing the root sign does not reverse this even current.

The polynomial target's full conditional finite return is generated by Wick's cumulant formula for the displayed multilinear symbol. The actual bounded program adds the separately calibrated fixed-order floors just specified; they are not included in the Wick parity enumeration. For n copies, let k,k' be the numbers of uncontracted side-probe occurrences, hence the numbers of side-force shifts. Their exponents obey

    effective grade = n [4+2p+p K_offspine] + k e_R + k' e_R',
    force count = 4n+k+k',
    k,k' are nonnegative and have parity n.

The internal physical-probe shifts alter rank, not force grade. For n>=2 every such coefficient has grade at least twice the spine grade; for odd n, at least one shift of each side color is necessary. This proves a conditional effective-grade gain without claiming either intact six-force color survives.

Each probe color occurs only once in an initial root-spine block. Therefore no Wick edge self-contracts inside that initial block. Every block retains its two endpoint physical slots. Cap leaves also retain their physical slots. For each connected Wick graph, merge groups contracting all intervening edges at once. The restricted block-contraction lemma gives all proper cuts Lambda and one HS factor Lambda sqrt(D), times beta^n and the exact A powers above. This argument proves the conditional coefficient bound; producing and then closing every resulting multi-cycle packet is a separate task. The auxiliary-edge construction can open these cycles, but its new source/current ledger must be checked, because the present beta has no clock slack.

## 4. Actual firsts, absolute floors and complete count

For the default allocation, root fresh/private scale is Lambda A^2 beta. Its own coarse derivative is at a leaf and costs sigma_1^-1, which is summable with the squared original leaf-clock masses. The first tight center-caller path is

    Lambda beta A^3 / sigma_2;

the other center lies one strict ancestor farther away. With both side leaves rescaled, the compensated root has grade three, the center source still grade one, and this path becomes

    Lambda beta A^4 / sigma_2.

Their endpoint sums can grow respectively like A^3/sigma_min and A^4/sigma_min. An unchanged Lambda A RAW caller contract is therefore not proved at arbitrarily fine inherited cutoffs. The complete LAW graph is still finite on its recorded domain. If sigma_min=A^b_P, its caller factor is a computable fixed-P power of A^-1. That factor may be included when selecting all absolute prior/filter/clipping/encoding budgets.

For example, if the smallest source exponent is a_min=1/2 and the total numerical amplification is bounded by Lambda A^-B_P, a native prior order k with a_min k >= P+B_P, and separately chosen finite filter tolerances, prices those floors. k is fixed by P, not by A. No floor is divided by actual source energy. Correlated callers use uniform frozen-label higher-Lp calibration, with Holder when products share roots.

For each fixed derivative-hit/injection and Price-clock history, the leading candidate costs six COMPLETE selected-pair calls: four C0 and two two-probe C2 calls. Each includes all original g VALUES, exact anchors, finite scalar coefficient filters, pair/calibration replicas and discarded-primal replay. All old query centers and Price roots are part of the complete bank. Inverse positive shares and quadrature/filter counts are public-log factors. Gaussian dimension/D has the analogous fixed-order/public-log count. Original HVPs occur only in requested first/adjoint sweeps at recorded original VALUE sites.

This is a count for the finite leading candidate and its assigned numerical floors. It is not the recurrence for a closed correction grammar.

## 5. The exact tight own-coarse ledger

Let J(q,q',t):theta^6 denote the normalized six-tree coefficient. Its known main scale at p=1/2 is

    C = Lambda A^7 beta J.

Amplitude redistribution leaves C unchanged, including its dependence on the whole coarse bank. The ordinary native derivative-cut certificate gives a center hit cost sigma_2^-1. Applying the dimension-safe Price contraction to the main's own coarse covariance therefore gives the explicit ledger

    proper cuts <= Lambda A^14 beta^2 sigma_2^-2,
    HS <= Lambda A^14 beta^2 sigma_2^-2 sqrt(D).

At the original center endpoint,

    beta^2 sigma_2^-2 = w^4/sigma_2^10,
    Delta_2^4/sigma_2^10 is of order sigma_2^-2.

Hence the sum of this positive bound is of order sigma_min^-2, not merely logarithmic. There is no spare endpoint mass from raising beta to a power. All-center-hit connected h-fold coarse histories have the generic derivative ledger

    A^(7h) beta^h sigma_2^(-2(h-1)).

At sigma_min=A^b_P this becomes

    A^[(7-2b_P)h+2b_P].

If b_P>=7/2, these particular bounds do not increase their effective order with h. They do not justify a finite force cutoff. For b_P>=7, even the two-copy normalized pointwise scalar A^14 sigma_min^-2 need not be small; a direct all-small-amplitude source factorization of that literal normalization cannot pass the native gap without an additional grouping/cancellation or explicitly charged replication.

This is a precise failure of the *available bound and direct pointwise constructor*, not a lower bound on the actual current. Oscillatory cancellation, unexpanded common-heat energy, or a more informative native port might improve it. None is furnished by a marginal law certificate or the original all-cut estimate alone. A proof claiming this worst ledger is attained uniformly would need a separate source-qualified example, which is not asserted here.

In particular the first-eight-force auxiliary cycle note has w^4/sigma_2^4 and real clock slack; the present beta^2=w^4/sigma_2^8 does not inherit that estimate. An auxiliary edge only changes the execution of an index contraction, not these original heat powers.

## 6. Positive bounded terminal-difference channel

Here is the useful positive sublemma, stated separately from its unavailable reroot extension. Let an old terminal channel, conditional on its old public P and the entire frozen coarse caller, have ideal jointly Gaussian input/output (R,Y) with standard marginals and matrix cross M. Its two calls at q_+,q_- have crosses M_+,M_-. The old channel can be the complete cubic path before any physical readout.

Retain an actual original standard Gaussian input R. Draw one fresh standard Z0 and split

    R_+=(R+Z0)/sqrt(2), R_-=(R-Z0)/sqrt(2).

These are independent standard Gaussians conditional on all frozen callers. Execute two independent COMPLETE old-channel copies, using R_+,R_- as their inputs and the prescribed q_+,q_- as callers, with the same captured old public P. Return

    Y=(Y_+-Y_-)/sqrt(2).

The pair (R,Y), conditional on all those callers, is exactly jointly Gaussian in the ideal reference, with

    Cov(Y)=I, Cov(R)=I, Cov(Y,R)=(M_+-M_-)/2.

For example write Y_+=M_+R_++S_+Z_+ and similarly for the other channel, with S_+S_+^*=I-M_+M_+^*. Then direct substitution proves the cross, while independence of the two complete calls gives Cov(Y)=I. This is an actual positive program; it does not request the difference cross or its square root as an oracle.

For finite-law replacement, condition on P and every fixed coarse label first. The independent split inputs permit each complete old-channel comparison while retaining its selected incoming input and the other independent input. Keep both split inputs through both comparisons; their known inverse linear transform retains the required original R (and may also retain Z0). Then apply the final known norm-one difference row. No source-private tape integrated by either comparison is appended afterward. Pair/filter priors and calibration floors receive these actual linear readouts and any later executed caller factors. Comparisons that also require a further physical R observer retain R throughout.

The full bill is Q_diff=Q_old,+ + Q_old,- plus known splitting/readout work, one additional D-dimensional Gaussian Z0, and each old copy's complete root/filter/calibration bank. Every changed caller replays its full old query ancestry. Finite fixed-order source rescaling is allowed only with its actual new native radii, firsts, orders and tolerances recorded. With unchanged old calls, a safe actual first/caller bound is the sum of their bounds; the small difference of ideal crosses does not by itself prove a delta gain of the sampled program's derivative.

At original source zero both old zero channels supply their recorded caller-independent Gaussian outputs, so their difference is a recorded caller-independent Gaussian. At delta=0 with a NONZERO original source, the ideal cross vanishes and the pair law is independent Gaussian, but the executed map can still depend on the callers through its noise rotation. That distinct statement must not be relabeled as a pointwise source-zero identity.

Ordinary terminal transposition of a Gaussian joint law is legal. The next section shows precisely why exchanging an external argument of M with a terminal is a different operation.

## 7. A bounded exact counterexample to external-probe rerooting

The following does not involve any small-width estimate. Let G,R,Z be independent standard scalar Gaussians and let m be a bounded odd function with |m|<1. Execute the exact compensated conditional channel

    Y = m(G) R + sqrt(1-m(G)^2) Z.

Conditional on the captured external probe G, (R,Y) is jointly Gaussian with standard marginals and cross m(G). Its source-zero Gaussian is Z, pointwise independent of G. For instance take

    m(G)=epsilon sin(delta G),  0<epsilon<1/2.

This has bounded actual caller derivative epsilon delta. It has a source-qualified realization inside the old cubic channel. Take the anchored scalar original gradient

    g(x)=A[kappa x+eta(1-cos x)],
    0<eta<kappa, kappa+eta<1.

Then 0<Dg<A. The center C1 coefficient is a known multiple of cos(q_2) times its center-public probe. To make this an exactly bounded selected field, retain the native finite odd-in-probe scalar filter: for the sinusoidal source its selected matrix has the form cos(q_2) h(P), where h is a bounded finite sine combination. It is nonzero at some fixed captured P=p0 whenever the C1 calibration is nontrivial. Its polynomial target is used only for calibration, not for execution.

Choose the center phase q_2=pi/2. Use the old center's independent coarse block W2 as the new scalar probe direction, while all other coarse coordinates remain captured. W2 changes the center query and does not change the two leaf centers. Consequently the two scalar leaf-channel factors remain fixed and nonzero, and the symmetric center shift gives

    cos(pi/2+dG)-cos(pi/2-dG) = -2 sin(dG).

Here d is delta times the actual known W2-to-center row, including sigma_2 and any fixed outer s factor. Fix this original clock away from its endpoint, so that this row is a nonzero constant independent of A. The two complete old-channel calls in Section 6 therefore have difference cross epsilon sin(dG), with epsilon of the cubic amplitude scale A^3. This does not assume a general whole-bank shift leaves the leaf queries unchanged: it singles out the actual W2 block and captures every other coordinate.

The bounded-field ideal reference is thus literal for this original-gradient source. Finite native pair execution only contributes its separately assigned absolute floor. It cannot remove the strictly positive square-amplitude covariance defect. Replacing d by delta in the formulas merely absorbs the fixed known row constant; its heat grade is unchanged.

Now attempt to promote G to the selected input while treating the old input R=r as an external caller. The exact same joint law has

    E[Y|R=r]=0,
    E[Y^2|R=r]=1+(r^2-1) v,
    v=E[m(G)^2]>0.

For the sinusoid,

    v=epsilon^2 (1-exp(-2delta^2))/2.

Except at the accidental values r^2=1, the new output is not standard Gaussian. It is therefore not an admitted Gaussian channel conditional on r. Its conditional mean given G,r is r m(G), also non-linear in G; a matrix-terminal transposition does not fix this.

The entire finite conditional expansion is explicit. With H_n the probabilists' Hermite polynomials,

    E[exp(tY)|R=r]
      = exp(t^2/2) sum_(n>=0) H_n(r) t^n E[m(G)^n]/n!.

In particular the leading conditional log defect is

    (v/2)(r^2-1)t^2.

It is intrinsic and remains when every numerical tolerance vanishes. If epsilon is of cubic force scale A^3 and delta=A^(1/2), then v is of scale A^7. If the channel response is normalized by multiplying epsilon by delta^-1 using one small A/delta nonroot, its defect is instead of scale A^6 before any separately declared downstream attenuation. The complete actual downstream readout must be restored; no missing delta may be presumed.

Reading aR+cY makes the same issue visible without conditional language:

    log E exp(theta(aR+cY))
      = (a^2+c^2)theta^2/2 + log E exp(ac m(G)theta^2),

so the first intrinsic current is +(ac)^2 v theta^4/2. A root sign change leaves it unchanged. If m also carries an old center public P, its physical tilt supplies the additional rank-six component as well. Merely averaging the newly declared caller again therefore does not erase the full-current obligation.

The valid orthogonal input-split construction is Section 6. The failure occurs at the NEXT step, when an external argument of its cross is exchanged with a terminal index. That exchange is not Gaussian-channel reversal and has the exact defect just computed. This is not a claim that no higher conditional-current surgery can repair the defect.

## 8. Physical-R tilts of a joint rotated bank remain separate

For the fractional B pair, the physical variable is the original input R and the generated P has cross M=JB/delta. The complete rotated callers are

    q_+=x+s[sqrt(1-delta^2)Q+delta P],
    q_-=x+s[sqrt(1-delta^2)Q-delta P].

A physical tilt shifts P by bM theta. It shifts the two q callers by opposite b s JB theta. The symmetric and antisymmetric joint-bank coordinates have respective Gaussian widths sqrt(1-delta^2) and delta. Consequently the full joint-bank Cameron-Martin/Hermite score is M=JB/delta, not JB. A j-mark score costs, conservatively,

    Lambda ||B/delta||^j = Lambda A^[j(1-p)].

For p=1/2 a grade-N joint coefficient therefore has score grades N+j/2, if its required score-energy certificate is available. In contrast, a function of only one marginal q_+ or q_- can use its full marginal heat and the cancellation delta M=JB. That marginal argument cannot be applied backward to an arbitrary function of the joint bank.

A full-HS Hermite/Bessel estimate can in appropriate cases preserve one Hilbert factor. It does not automatically bound every proper cut needed by a new source or establish its VALUE realization. The coefficient's entire joint bank, actual B-pair source, and derivative/mark history must remain in the return. This port remains OPEN here. In particular neither the auxiliary cycle's owned-q bridge count nor Section 3's conditional Wick proof claims all higher physical-R tilt descendants.

## 9. Narrow conclusion

A positive leading grade-seven Price tree and a conditional force/grade-return ledger are available. Off-spine inverse-delta rescaling gives a genuine conditional gain, without any intact-colored-subtree assertion. The complete tight whole-coarse current compiler is still unproved.

The minimal new port must either preserve the grouped common-heat gain through its actual positive source and every later connected current, or supply a conditional external-probe-to-terminal conversion with the complete covariance and higher-current correction just exposed. A terminal Gaussian channel, its transpose, its small law error, and a larger LAW-only numerical budget do not by themselves provide that port.
