## 3. Exact law marking and its descendants

Write H_s C(y)=E C(y+sG). For a formal physical Laplace/current variable theta, Gaussian tilting in R gives, at the leading epsilon coefficient,

    E exp(b theta.JR) C(rho z+s[sqrt(1-delta^2)Q +/- delta R])
      =exp(b^2|J*theta|^2/2)
         (H_s C)(rho z +/- s delta b J*theta).

This identity applies to the whole coefficient with its common root bank. It is not a derivative of an output mean and not three separate heat replacements. The two signs therefore give the exact leading-current expression

    epsilon alpha^3 [H_s C(rho z+s delta b J*theta)
                     -H_s C(rho z-s delta b J*theta)]:theta^3.       (1)

Its first marked term is

    2 epsilon alpha^3 s delta b
        D_(J*theta)(H_s C)(rho z):theta^3.                         (2)

All odd higher derivatives are also present. For example the next term is

    (epsilon alpha^3/3)(s delta b)^3
        D_(J*theta)^3(H_s C)(rho z):theta^3,                       (3)

a rank-six current. With delta=A and the cubic epsilon of grade three, (2) has grade four and (3) has grade six before their true heat/clock factors. The remaining odd terms can only be truncated after a separately proved finite heat-current remainder estimate. Equation (1) is an exact coefficient identity; it does not query H_s C or any derivative.

The old quadratic descendant is, conditional on Q,R,

    epsilon^2 alpha^(its physical rank)
       [D_2(x,q_+;theta)+D_2(x,q_-;theta)],                       (4)

with the appropriate rankwise readout factors. Reversing epsilon does not reverse (4). Upon integrating Q,R, additional connected products of the two leading coefficients appear. They must be generated at the same endpoint and retained with their literal labels. In particular no delta or new B factor may be attached to an inherited descendant unless it is actually present in that descendant's graph.

For the linear native reference, the first two conditional symbol terms are the cubic cab q A_Q:theta^3 and quartic (ca q)^2|A_Q^sharp:theta^2|^2/2. The conditional quartic term (4) adds and remains grade six. Its pure physical rank-six homogeneous part uses the UNTILTED variance; the theta-dependence of the tilted measure produces higher ranks. The whole signed difference is zero at delta=0, and an O(delta^2) estimate requires the appropriate grouped derivative/energy bounds. It must not be counted as two uncancelled self variances. These are finite-coefficient analytical identities; the unbounded linear matrix field is not used as a Gaussian covariance sampler.

## 5. Why the grade-six coarse bridge is still missing

The current to be realized is the original cubic coarse covariance

    Cov_Q(C(Q):theta^3,C(Q):theta^3)
      = E <R_Q C, D_Q C>:theta^6,                                 (5)

with the Riesz convention fixed by the inherited hierarchy. It has two intact cubic subtrees and grade six. A Gaussian bridge clock is common to each entire subtree.

The leading conditional coefficient of an unrenormalized marked response is proportional to epsilon delta. Coupling two such responses through their Gaussian mark consequently produces epsilon_1 epsilon_2 delta_1 delta_2. For delta_1=delta_2=A this is grade eight. It does not equal (5), which was already grade six.

Dividing both responses by delta is not an innocuous scalar edit to the proven native port. If the two old A-amplitude child vertices are retained, the old root amplitude proportional to A must be multiplied by 1/delta; at delta=A it is order one, outside the conservative Lambda A source/actual-first return. Redistributing the amplitude among vertices also changes those first/source guards. Such a renormalized port would need a new proof, and cannot import the old return unchanged. This is a failure of unchanged-scale raw reuse, not an impossibility for a law-only service: a service used only at fixed callers might tolerate a larger explicitly charged first and tighter absolute calibration. That alternative still owes the full grouped current/all-cut certificate and an admissible law consumer.

Taking delta of constant size avoids the grade loss but does not isolate the first Gaussian chaos. Every odd chaos of the complete coefficient remains at the same A grade. A finite public-chaos filter can isolate a prescribed finite jet, or approximate first-chaos projection to a separately controlled absolute heat-remainder floor, with signed weights placed in source amplitudes. It cannot exactly project every smooth coefficient using finitely many nonzero correlation samples. Its nonlinear descendants are weighted quadratically and remain. It is a leading-current approximation, not a full conditional-law filter.

An exact correlated-bank interpolation is positive and does preserve all queries: run complete kernels at Q_1=G, Q_2=tG+sqrt(1-t^2)H. Its joint leading cubic covariance is Cov(C(Q_1),C(Q_2)). The difference of the t=1 and t=0 log laws isolates (5), but that difference is not itself an executed positive kernel. Differentiating this interpolation gives a legitimate analytical Price/Riesz identity, not a new VALUE producer.

### Restricted sign obstruction

Consider specifically sums of independent, conditionally cubic native kernels with common coefficient roots, no direct readout of those roots in the source-zero physical carrier, and no conditional epsilon-squared rank-six term. In this restricted class the pure rank-six coefficient at order epsilon squared is one half the variance of the total leading cubic scalar coefficient. It is nonnegative on every diagonal test theta:

    [rank 6, epsilon^2] log E exp(epsilon sum_j a_j C_j:theta^3
                                 +epsilon^2 rank-4 terms)
      = (1/2) Var(sum_j a_j C_j:theta^3) >= 0.                     (6)

Signed source amplitudes a_j can make individual cross terms negative but also retain their self terms. They do not implement an arbitrary negative coarse-variance counterterm. Equation (6) is only a limitation of this specified composition class, not of the full marked kernel above. With a public-root Gaussian readout, tilting can turn a root-dependent conditional rank-four term into higher physical ranks, so that case needs its complete symbol instead of (6). A new sign-programmable native six-current kernel, or a stronger joint kernel surgery port, could also escape it.

## 6. Two contracts that cannot be inferred from the old export

### 6.1 Marginal law plus known zero does not license carrier subtraction

There are positive, finite, smooth Gaussian-preserving reparameterizations with the same conditional marginal law, exact source-zero Gaussian, and O(A) complete residual/caller first, but different retained-carrier responses.

In dimension two, let J be the quarter-turn matrix, choose a smooth radial cutoff chi supported in 1<|u|<2, and set

    T_a(q,u)=exp(a f(q) chi(|u|^2) J) u,

where f and Df are bounded. Radius is unchanged, and on each circle this is a rotation, so T_a(q,U) is exactly standard Gaussian for every q,a. T_0(q,U)=U. Compact support gives uniformly O(|a|) residual first and caller first. Its conditional output mean and every non-Gaussian conditional current are zero.

Nevertheless T_a(q,U)-U has a nonzero order-a response relative to the executed carrier U. Therefore no rule can reconstruct a carrier-retained response from only the old marginal-current export, known source-zero output and actual first bounds. This is an interface counterexample, not a claim that the pinned native implementation secretly has this reparameterization. A new joint theorem about that specific implementation would be required for subtraction.

### 6.2 Proper cuts of C do not alone give every grouped derivative cut

For q in R^m with m>=1, let

    C(q)=sin(q_1) sum_(i=1)^D e_i tensor e_i tensor e_i.

Every proper matrix cut of C is at most one. But the derivative cut q -> all three physical slots has norm sqrt(D)|cos(q_1)|. Common Gaussian heat of variance u multiplies this by exp(-u/2), and does not turn it into a dimension-free bound. The covariance of C has a three-versus-three cut with norm D Var(sin Q_1).

Thus generic coefficient proper cuts, or a generic nongradient kernel first, do not imply the native one-energy all-cut return for the grouped bridge. Native original-gradient structure may supply an additional estimate; that estimate must be proved with the grouped shared bank. Scoring one common Gaussian supplies bounds involving a Hilbert coefficient norm in the root-versus-all-physical cut, and cannot silently replace it by a proper-cut constant.
