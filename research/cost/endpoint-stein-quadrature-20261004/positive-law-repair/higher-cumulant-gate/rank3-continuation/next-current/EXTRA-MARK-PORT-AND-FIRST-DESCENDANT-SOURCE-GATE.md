# Conditional kernel extra mark and the first descendant source gate

2026-10-04. Proposed closure interface with a verified coefficient identity and explicit source/read-set obligations. This is not a proved arbitrary-order recurrence. It preserves the inherited error/current record instead of renaming it as an error quadratic in the new coefficient.

## 1. Required input: a conditional current kernel, not just a marginal law

The input is an actual finite VALUE kernel K_C(y;Omega), with external caller y, independent complete private bank Omega, known caller-independent Gaussian carrier, and actual residual/caller first <=Lambda A. Its current certificate must identify a leading rank-m conditional tensor C(y), programmable leading sign/amplitude, and every retained higher connected current together with its original query labels, clocks, source graph, energy and derivative frames. Arbitrarily small fixed-target filter/pair/numerical floors are separate.

A marginal W2 error bound does not supply this input. In particular, the cubic packet's grade-six feedback remains grade six when its caller is marked; a new rank-four kernel does not automatically have grade-eight error.

The original outer clock must be exposed as a real program cut. If

    kappa_m(y)=R_m C_inner(y)
              =integral_0^1 r^(m-1) P_r C_inner(y) dr,

the new operation uses the existing complete inner kernel conditional on its actual outer coarse caller. It does not query a derivative of the completed kappa_m output.

## 2. Exact public-mark coefficient identity

Fix r, put s=sqrt(1-r^2), and form the actual caller

    Y'=rY+s[aP+sqrt(1-a^2)G], |a|<=1/2.

For fixed P, each complete inner kernel is run on a fresh bank conditional on this Y'. Read an independent public bP together with alpha times the kernel. At its leading rank-m coefficient, Gaussian integration in P gives the exact formal coefficient identity

    E[e^(b theta.P) C_inner(Y')]
      =e^(b^2|theta|^2/2)
         P_r[exp(a b s theta.D) C_inner](Y).           (1)

The derivative in (1) acts on C_inner before P_r. It is not D_Y P_r; no spurious inverse-r factor is introduced. The identity is first justified for bounded smooth coefficients, then for the actual positive-heat coefficients and fixed finite characteristic/current expansions.

The part first order in a has coefficient

    alpha^m b s/m!  P_r(D C_inner):theta^(tensor(m+1)).

Thus its new cumulant is

    (m+1) alpha^m b s Sym P_r(D C_inner).             (2)

A finite separate-first-chaos filter in a realizes this leading coefficient using only changes of the caller Y'. Every signed filter coefficient is placed in the programmable old-kernel source amplitude, not in a probability weight. Allocate positive Gaussian shares to the independent complete kernel copies; their caller-independent baseline variances add. The same P is retained across the filter copies, as required for the coefficient identity. All changed callers replay complete original ancestors.

The finite filter uses |a| bounded away from one and its smallest node only inverse-polylogarithmic. It is an analytic Gaussian coefficient filter for the leading current; it is not a finite-difference claim about a merely C2 Hessian.

Since

    D kappa_m=integral_0^1 r^m P_r(D C_inner) dr,

the root coefficient restoring the old outer clock is proportional to its known positive weight times r^m/s. The integrals with 1/s are finite, and the extra caller/owned-root sums with 1/s^2 are logarithmic at the recorded cutoff. No new heat is shrunk to zero to imitate an unsmoothed derivative.

## 3. Attach the original-gradient B mark

For the hierarchy term B D kappa_m, prepare a native genuine-gradient selected pair (R,P), conditional on the same captured Y, whose cross matrix is M_B(Y)=s0 B(Y). The original B source is the already legal anchored mean-Jacobian source made from original g VALUES. Its output P is standard in the ideal conditional reference, and R is retained.

Use this P in the caller Y' above, and read bR instead of bP. The Gaussian shift in (1) becomes a b s M_B(Y)theta. Hence (2) becomes

    (m+1) alpha^m b s Sym[M_B P_r(D C_inner)].         (3)

For m=3, the cumulant hierarchy requires the rank-four term

    8 R4 Sym[B D kappa_3],

besides the separate 6 R4 Sym[(D C2)(D C2)] term. Thus the known source/readout scalar, the pair's s0 and the clock factor r^3/s fully determine the factor needed to match (3). No unknown tensor coefficient is evaluated.

The order of law comparisons is mandatory. First replace the actual complete B pair by its exact Gaussian pair while retaining R; its error is transported through the inner kernel's ACTUAL Lambda A caller first. Then compare the inner complete kernel banks conditionally on the now-exact Gaussian inputs, retaining R and the captured Y. The same complete child output P is used at every prescribed filter occurrence. Its private tape is not appended after the pair comparison.

This is an ordinary VALUE-kernel composition, not admission of K_C as a new gradient. Under the declared actual path bounds, its residual/caller first remains Lambda A: the child-to-inner path gains the actual kernel caller factor, and the public R readout belongs to the known carrier. Original HVPs still occur only in first/adjoint sweeps at recorded original-gradient VALUE sites.

## 4. Error/current return must remain inherited

The old kernel's conditional remainder currents remain in the new record, with their literal clock, readout, source-amplitude and 1/s factors. They are not multiplied by the new B factor unless that factor is present in their actual graph. Pair and finite first-chaos errors are assigned their own absolute floors through actual downstream firsts.

In particular, coupling a cubic kernel through a B pair creates an intended four-force rank-four term, but its shared-public mixture can still produce a six-force feedback from TWO OLD cubic subtrees, with no B vertices. It need not contain two copies of the new four-force main. Its grade remains six, rather than eight.

Equation (1) is a leading-coefficient identity, not by itself a full current-level closure theorem. The input's higher current records must be propagated, and the new shared-public and owned-root connected terms must be generated at the same endpoint before a fixed-target recurrence is claimed.

## 5. The first exact cubic feedback descendants are native trees

For the cubic native path with an exactly linear center target M_Q(P)=A_Q[P], collapse the compensated Gaussian channels and integrate their reverse innovations. Conditional on Q, its exact finite-coefficient symbol is

    exp( baseline Gaussian term
          +cab q A_Q:theta^3
          +(ca q)^2 |A_Q^sharp:theta^2|^2/2 ).         (4)

The finite bounded field and analytic clipping/filter errors remain separately budgeted; the polynomial target in (4) is used only for finite coefficient identification.

The first q^2 term is rank four. It glues two intact three-force paths along their center-public marks. Each center loses that physical mark and gains the joining edge, so its derivative order is unchanged. The result is a six-force, four-mark tree with legal native vertices. There is no trace cycle.

A second q^2 term comes from coarse-Q covariance of the cubic coefficient. An unexpanded Gaussian Riesz bridge joins two three-force trees, preserving all six physical marks. Expanding only after assigning the finite Riesz clock increments the degree and derivative order together at the two hit original vertices. This is a six-force, six-mark native tree. Again no derivative-valued producer is needed at the vertex level.

One must use (4), not the separately dropped M^T M covariance-root term in a W2 proof. That separate term appears to create a closed trace when publics are averaged; its compensation is already built into the exact reverse symbol.

History-wise strict spine also needs care. If a new B leaf is attached at v of an old tree T, its diameter is max(diam(T),ecc(v)+1). Joining two copies at that derivative-hit vertex has diameter 2ecc(v)+1, which is strictly larger. This requires the derivative-hit history to stay explicit. Pooling all hits and then claiming a largest-spine improvement is not justified by this argument.

## 6. Exact derivative count, including unmarked degree-two vertices

For a legal native vertex, degree d_v and physical mark m_v in {0,1} give derivative order

    j_v=d_v+m_v-1>=1.

Every leaf is marked; an unmarked vertex has degree at least two. Its additional heat loss relative to one bounded original Jacobian is j_v-1=d_v+m_v-2. For a tree with M marks,

    sum_v(j_v-1)=M-2.                                (5)

There is no negative-loss credit: an unmarked degree-two vertex has j_v=1 and zero extra heat. The tempting unmarked degree-one j_v=0 exception is outside the admitted native class.

A true VALUE-energy Bessel mark may require one further inverse shield beyond (5). The bounded-Jacobian A sqrt(D) mark does not. The source-qualified PSD/dilated-clock argument in the audited adapter can retain the actual integrated H energy, but that argument must be carried through the new clock family instead of silently identifying a VALUE energy with a Jacobian HS norm.

## 7. First remaining actual-source port: common bridge heat

The q^2 rank-six bridge has an analytical smoothing gain on the Riesz-averaged side. A pointwise source realization cannot simply assign that common heat independently to every original vertex.

The obstruction to that PARTICULAR reshielding is elementary. In a near-coincident three-query subtree, the conditional Gaussian Gram has the form

    sigma^2 I_3+u 1 1^T.

Its two transverse eigenvalues are still sigma^2. Therefore no Gaussian decomposition preserving those three query laws can give all three vertices independent shields of variance sigma^2+u. Even the conditional variance of one query given the other two is

    sigma^2(sigma^2+3u)/(sigma^2+2u)<=3sigma^2/2.

Thus the analytical common-shift derivative gain cannot be converted into a vertexwise caller-first gain merely by relabeling roots. The actual common bridge Gaussian must remain shared by the complete old subtree.

A successful continuation needs either (a) a proved whole-subtree common-heat native source construction with its actual caller/owned-root first and complete replay bill, or (b) the conditional kernel-valued response port above, carrying the complete connected current record. The existing finite primitive vertex types are admitted; the grouped common-heat source/clock/first return is the first unresolved port. The marginal cubic packet W2 theorem does not provide it.

For n intact cubic subtrees, the proposed M-2 heat count and one gain per unexpanded Riesz bridge suggest a summable all-fixed-rank rule, but it remains conditional on this exact grouped-source port. This note does not promote that analytical heuristic to an executed recurrence.
