## 2. Nonlinear separator for that particular amplifier

## 3. Conditional OU-velocity law service

`conditional-ou-velocity/THIRD-ORDER-CONDITIONAL-OU-VELOCITY-LAW-SERVICE.md`

The actual conditional posterior-force source splits into a genuine-gradient baseline and a shared-root near-gradient chord with energy `O(A^2 s^3 sqrt(D))`, first `O(As)`, curl `O(A^2 s^3)`, and exact zero. This matches the admitted positive gradient / near-gradient mean consumers.

The source declares the actual normalized-radius, covariance-gap and public-log guards, full caller profiles, root read sets and complete replay counts. The unit Gaussian buffer is retained in its target; this is not a strong mean oracle.

Independent scoped PASS: `conditional-ou-velocity/independent-audit/INDEPENDENT-CONDITIONAL-OU-VELOCITY-AUDIT.md`, 1,685 assertions.
