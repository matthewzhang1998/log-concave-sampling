## 2. The terminal floor survives improved local replication

Suppose an improved local arm has top-heat penalty k_P>=0 and retains the same adaptive1/N error envelope. For delta=R-P the general certificate is

    c_R<=max(c_P+k_P,
               (R/P)[c_P+(k_P-delta)_+]).          (A)

The positive part retains N>=1 and the complete old terminal cost(R/P)c_P. The shorter expression without it cannot be extrapolated to k=0.

Setting k=0 makes the selected inherited ratio stay constant:

    c_R=(R/P)c_P, r_R=r_P.

Thus merely adding cheaper later upgrades to a positive-cost family does not make r vanish. For a constant k<=delta, r cannot decrease and is driven toward at least k/delta if it starts below that threshold. For fixed k>delta the eventual ratio growth is logarithmic. Saying k=o(P) is not sufficient.

For a general replacement bill c_R<=alpha_P c_P+b_P, define

    theta_P=alpha_P P/R, epsilon_P=b_P/R.

A sufficient dilution condition is theta_P<=1-d_P, sum d_P=infinity, epsilon_P=o(d_P). A bigger accuracy jump with alpha_P=R/P still has theta=1. One must alter the normalized inherited factor or restart with sufficiently cheap predecessors, as well as control the additive forcing.

## 3. Main asymptotic target: rebuilt arbitrarily-small-power local compilers

The existing native-repair research can target this precise port:

For each required fixed local grade Q and each selected kappa>0, return a complete finite local program with

    Q_local(A)<=Lambda_(Q,kappa) A^(-kappa) Q_old(A),

plus the actual law, original-source bias target, first/caller, retained graph, origin, protected rows and computable numerical/domain guards. It must cover the WHOLE mean/repair, not one private current. The full literal source call list must obey the stated bill.

Rebuild each selected finite high-order family FROM c_7=0. The first CW7 stage does not yet have actual g=1, so with only a uniform local A^(-kappa_0) bill its full mode cutoff gives

    c_7.5<=5 kappa_0, r_7.5<=(2/3)kappa_0.

After that, the safe uniform-local-cost old-suffix bound gives

    r_next<=r_current+kappa_i/P_i.

For final ratio epsilon, choose the finitely many stage penalties with

    (2/3)kappa_0+sum_(i>=1)kappa_i/P_i<=epsilon.    (B)

Arbitrarily small selectable penalties at every required fixed grade suffice: distribute epsilon over these finite known weights. Constants may depend badly on Q,kappa. Their effective computability and guards, not uniform polynomial-in-Q growth, are required. Let epsilon tend to0 across regenerated higher-order families.

Exact polynomial-log local work is the stronger case kappa_i=0; c_P=0 then remains0 even through the terminal ratio. Under the specific adaptive envelope(A), the simpler invariant k_i<=epsilon delta gives c_i<=epsilon P_i. Do not use that invariant for a compiler without the same precision law; use(B) instead.

The concrete first checkpoint is one whole g=1 upgrade from the cheap seed with zero or arbitrarily small heat exponent, including all residual mean and source ports. A partial private-row gain is useful progress but is not that checkpoint.

## 4. Structural alternative: same-heat additive posterior refinement

A genuine source-level refinement can improve an already positive-cost family if it has

    Q_R(a)<=Lambda_P[Q_P(a)+a^(-b_P)]              (C)

and returns a higher accuracy order relative to the true Q_(a,y), with complete old calls only at heats comparable to a up to public logs. It must preserve the full actual/retained/caller and observed-label interface.

Because work in(C) is additive,

    c_R<=max(c_P,b_P).

Bounded b_P keeps c bounded while order grows, so c(P)/P tends to0. More generally the running envelope max_(i<=n)b_(P_i)=o(P_n) suffices. A polynomial-log number of complete old calls at the same heat is allowed.

This requires an actual coupled or defect-correction program. It cannot be obtained merely by approximating E F_P more accurately, caching one sample, or reusing the terminal child across differing exposed callers. Current empirical banks require independent private records; hidden decoders have different callers and fresh Gaussian shares. Their count is already additive and logarithmic. Reusing a draw changes their joint law and carrier unless a new joint interface proves otherwise.

Native corrections with same-source polylog work are promising local ingredients, but an improved local covariance row is not yet a same-heat POSTERIOR order improvement of type(C).

## 5. Cheap-library resets and jumps

A reset could directly provide a new complete order-R source with total c_reset(R)=o(R), including both force/history and terminal services. A diluting jump could satisfy the theta/epsilon criterion above. Neither follows from a cheaper covariance stage alone.

A fixed cheap F_7 library has a bias floor. More precise Gaussianization of E F_7, a larger gradient-prior order B, or more deterministic Picard position updates does not remove that bias. The old intrinsic known-center insertion has physical floor A^8; its uniform next hidden family is limited to index7.5. A fixed-library arbitrary-order constructor needs actual bias/defect corrections using original sources.

A cheap terminal alone also does not suffice while expensive F_P calls continue along smaller-heat prefixes. A fixed order-L terminal needs heat a^(R/L), exposing an inherited source ratio c_P/L. Both channels must be charged.

If the current empirical numerator A^5.4 and the old suffix are retained in a hypothetical larger jump, k=R-5.4 and the dominant ratio increment is1-5.4/P, independent of jump size. Fewer generations reduce the number of increments, but do not make them vanish. The current source proof does not authorize arbitrary jumps anyway.
