## 0. Result and precise boundary

Fix an integer j>=2. Put P=2j-1. Relative to the completed same-heat selected hidden source family described below, there is a finite positive outer sampler with arbitrary fixed accumulated order P and complete per-phase original-query heat exponent

    c_outer(P)=2j-2=P-1.

All source calls use the current heat A or an order/public-log-comparable heat. Hidden depth j-1 is fixed after j is chosen. No covariance, cumulant, marked mean/pair, proxy, retained-current, pure-even or root-reserve consumer is executed in this new outer layer. Every fluctuation is paid in ordinary L2 coupling. Signed multilevel averages are vectors inside a Gaussian pushforward, not signed probabilities.

The force producer is an executable recursion already supplied by the complete source proof, rather than an increased accuracy declaration for a fixed coefficient program. Its normalized direct grades are exactly

    (I,P_*,F_*)=(2j-1,2j-1,2j+1)=(P,P,P+2).

The first two grades hold before future smoothing. The proof below uses a simpler uniform-step chronological epoch, so it does not need a future-rank assertion at all. The existing dyadic schedule could also consume the same direct grades.

With the same source/caller/domain and numerical assumptions, choose H as below. The full original-query count, including all nested history banks, is

    Q <= Lambda_j kappa H^{-(4j-3)} = Lambda_j kappa H^{-(2P-1)}.

When the source domain and fixed small-heat guards admit the selected H, this gives

    Q <= Lambda_j {kappa^(2P) + kappa^(3-1/P) (D/epsilon^2)^(1-1/(2P))}.

This is a LINEAR-COST all-order outer theorem. It is neither a sublinear-cost result nor a dimension-subpolynomial bound: its dimension/accuracy exponent tends to 1, and it is worse than the existing fixed-order specialized sampler. Source-relative domain restrictions must be imposed honestly if that source version is used. The separate companion ELEMENTARY-SEED-AND-COMPLETE-SOURCE.md supplies a quarter-only source with an elementary finite-mode Gaussian seed and no algebraic physical-dimension guard; Section10 states the resulting standalone specialization. ## 2. Executed force mean, its target, and its complete work

For each requested (d,i) in a phase, condition only on theta=(x,v^-) and construct independent complete banks

    N_0(j)=ceil(C_j A^-2(j-1)),
    N_l(j)=ceil(C_j A^-2(j-l)), 1<=l<=j,
    M_(d,j,i)=average_N0 F_(d,0,i)
                   +sum_(l=1)^j average_Nl Delta_(d,l,i),
    ghat_(d,j,i)=M_(d,j,i)/sqrt(A).

Independence is between complete banks conditional on theta. Each paired occurrence includes its fine/coarse side, all complete lower-depth banks, known-center grids, observations, original zeros, mode iterations, caller queries and captured aliases. Coefficient-node count is polynomial in public logarithms and is absorbed in Lambda_j.

The finite telescope gives E[M|theta]=E[F_(d,j,i)|theta] exactly. L2 variance plus the finitely many bank levels gives

    ||M-EF_(d,j,i)||_2 <= Lambda_j sqrt(D) A^j.

Since grad V is 1-Lipschitz, the conditional source law comparison separately gives

    |E F_(d,j,i)-sqrt(A)m_A(y_i^[d])|
         <= Lambda_j sqrt(D) A^(j+1) + e_num.

Consequently

    ||ghat_(d,j,i)-m_A(y_i^[d])||_2
         <= Lambda_j sqrt(D) A^(j-1/2) + e_num/sqrt(A).  (M)

This is an actual strong comparison of an executable vector with a deterministic conditional target. Neither expectation is queried. The two terms have distinct origins, sampling and law bias; only the former is divided by sample counts.

Every positive-level bank pays

    2(j-l)+2(l-1)=2(j-1),

and the level-zero bank has the same total exponent. Summing the j levels does not change that exponent. The hidden recurrence has the identical bound at each of the fixed d levels, because each lower complete source is charged inside this bank. Thus the cost of M itself is Lambda_j A^-2(j-1), rather than that cost multiplied by another A-dependent sample count at every hidden depth. This is the completed strong-telescope fixed point. Requesting an original directional first/adjoint action can be charged by the corresponding complete sweep; the new outer sampler does not need such actions.

## 9. What this repairs and what remains open

The prior fixed covariance route has a genuine tuning ceiling; raising source law accuracy leaves its current parent-energy rows unchanged, and raising packet cutoff eventually violates the old widths. This construction repairs that obstruction by changing the producer: it pays the entire force fluctuation directly using a complete multilevel bank. It does not assert a new sharper covariance coefficient lemma.

It supplies an all-order OUTER reference, executable matching VALUE producer, complete direct comparison census, actual-prefix closure, reset/lift and full query exponent, all at same/comparable heat. A useful next optimization would reduce the sampling exponent while preserving this complete graph, not merely increase its force-law order. No sublinear conclusion follows here.
