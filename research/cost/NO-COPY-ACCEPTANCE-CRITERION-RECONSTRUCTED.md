## 1. Required decision

Accept an all-order low-cost repair only after a CLOSED finite producer/defect ledger proves positive net progress for every surviving row, stable actual source admissibility, and fully expanded work without unbudgeted inverse-A replication or inherited heat changes.

Two private-stage errors gaining one A power is insufficient. In particular a law error is not automatically an executed marked VALUE source or an improved input energy.

## 4. Minimal finite correction recurrence

For a fixed target Q, enumerate finite defect/source types t. A repair of type t at grade q must emit a COMPLETE finite list of successors u. Give proved lower grades

    q_u>=q+a_(t,u)+b_(t,u)g+sum_j d_(t,u,j)tau_j,   (1)

where g is the actual available host/curl margin and widths are h_j=A^(tau_j). Coefficients d include both genuine padding gains and every inverse-width/readout/observer loss. A minimum creates separate rows; none may be dropped.

Find rational type weights w_t and delta>0 such that each reachable successor satisfies

    a_(t,u)+b_(t,u)g+sum d_(t,u,j)tau_j+w_u-w_t
                                                    >=delta.       (2)

Then q+w_t increases along each repair path. A zero or negative total-gain directed cycle cannot be fixed by changing weights. An unchanged mean-bias or orientation self-loop must be repaired or shown already above the target stopping grade.

Zero-grade operations with a proved finite decreasing auxiliary counter, such as visible test degree, may be compressed into one macrostep or included in the finite type/weight ledger. A well-founded finite descent is not an unproved zero-grade loop. This reconstruction makes that permissible encoding explicit.

Equation(1) must be relative to the ACTUAL input defect. A fixed absolute tail A^b e reappearing unchanged after every application is a zero-gain row after the first application, not a repeated gain b.

Every successor also needs the actual source and work checks below. Only THEN may the aggregate error obey a recurrence such as

    E_(m+1)<=Lambda_m A^delta E_m+epsilon_num,m.

This follows from the typed construction. Iterating a W2 number as though it were a source is invalid.

If e<=Lambda sqrt(D) A^nu, stop when all unweighted q reach Q-nu plus margins. With bounded type weights, the depth is a computable finite M_Q, of order(required gain+weight range)/delta when delta is uniform. It is selected before A and source tapes. It may grow arbitrarily fast with Q, but is fixed at fixed Q.

## 5. Width and source stability gates

On every actual composed path prove:

- admissible positive fresh/caller host margins;
- the claimed next input curl;
- positive shrinking-radius exponents, or a separately checked fixed-order radius guard;
- retained physical outgoing mass, origin, terminal/protected rows;

These are derivatives and energies of the finite program, not derivatives of comparison errors. Mean bias and captured caller derivatives do not get an independent-copy gain.

### Canonical reset versus cumulative losses

At a NEW completed host, an original terminal force still has physical-center caller r/sqrt(A), because the FULL posterior derivative I+O(A^g) is bounded. The weaker g affects its recorded curl. Freshly expanded ORIGINAL-force repairs can therefore reset the caller margin; the old g need not be subtracted from1-2tau.

That is not permission to feed the last emitted counterpacket as a new original source. Nested packet caller losses may multiply. If only

    g_m>=g_0-2 sum tau_j,
    total gain<=2 sum tau_j

is known, a positive source-margin budget bounds total achievable gain. Splitting tau into smaller order-dependent pieces does not remove the cap.

Ways out require a source theorem: a canonical reset, actual ancestor/output attenuation paying the internal width losses, or a fixed intrinsic gain surviving summably small width losses. If each gain is bounded by a fixed multiple of accumulated width loss, type weights cannot turn a bounded budget into arbitrary accuracy.

This is a certificate limitation, not an algorithmic lower bound. ## 6. Clock support, gaps and true inherited heats

Check ACTUAL weighted clock sums. A small minimum width is neither automatically an inverse-A work cost nor automatically harmless. For illustration, covariance t written in v=sqrt(1-t) has dt=2v dv. A bound v^(-s) is bounded for s<2, logarithmic at s=2, and costs A^[-tau(s-2)] for s>2 with v_min=A^tau. Use each module's real measure and finite weights.

The original tail A e v_min can be moved from A^3 e to A^4 e by v_min=A^3 if all weighted source/current bounds survive. The H/sandwich clock products then become A^6/A^5 before other floors. This is a full numerical/source recheck, not automatically an empirical copy bill.

Preserve a known positive untouched Gaussian allocation after signed readouts. Target-dependent numerical shares or inverse-public-log shares have no heat power. A gap A^z does: its inverse powers belong in grades, source guards and restoration. Unknown covariance positivity or an unknown matrix root is not an executed primitive.

Old f calls inside new modules retain their actual source heat. Mehler argument changes are not heat changes. If a new node invokes the old program at A^eta, inherited work is A^(-eta c_old), not a free same-source factor.

## 7. Work and effective numerical stopping

A sufficient no-power-copy recurrence at fixed Q is

    N_(m+1)(A)<=p_m(L)N_m(A)+q_m(L),
    L=1+log(D+2)+log(1/A)+log(1/epsilon_num),       (3)

with computable polynomials, fixed-target finite M_Q, and unchanged inherited heat labels. Fixed nine-bank factors, finite branching and huge Q-dependent constants are allowed. The product over m<=M_Q remains polynomial in public logarithms.

Branching depth proportional to log(1/A), or independent count A^(-k), creates a real heat exponent. A linear-work Picard loop of logarithmic length is different and may remain polynomial-logarithmic.

Compute complete source amplification and scales before choosing precision. It suffices that at fixed Q,

    log V_total and log(1/min_scale)
                         <= a computable polynomial in public logs.

Allocate coherent leaf errors below the final grade budget. For a prior of radius A^rho and actual dimension at most Lambda D A^(-kappa_tape), choose finite B with

    rho B-kappa_tape/2 > target grade+all assigned power losses.

Require rho>0 or separately verify its actual numerical radius guard. Increasing B does not repair a radius that grows as A shrinks. Deterministic-zero restoration gets its own actual finite convergence bound, not a Gaussian Lp inference.

Moments, source orders, clock/interpolation degrees, variance shares, stopping depths and numerical budgets must be computable and fixed before execution. Very small tolerances are cheap only under a provider with the stated logarithmic precision response.

If some modules have selectable heat penalties kappa_j>0, the fully expanded path/max-plus ledger, including every copy and changed inherited heat, must be below the requested local kappa. Width selection alone does not prove that inequality.
