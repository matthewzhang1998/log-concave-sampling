## 4. Limited repeatability and exact unresolved boundary

Equations (2)-(3) give an explicit regularity recurrence for another such direct current update:

    L_new<=L_old+L_correction,
    kappa_new<=kappa_old+2L_correction,
    E_new<=(1+e)E_old+epsilon_clip.

Thus bounded sums of correction firsts and bounded products of (1+e) preserve these declared source envelopes. This is not a statement that repeated current errors may be used under arbitrary nonlinear mark-dependent callbacks. Linear current propagation through a later bounded kernel is permitted only while the marker is used linearly, the stated shared labels remain available, and every new carrier has the required conditional independence. Additional marked insertions, nonlinear reentry and the positive unweighted endpoint law need their own joint proof.

The original source work at each update remains charged. This note does not prove cost independent of the number of actual source updates, does not eliminate the missing base/singleton supplier, and does not resolve unrestricted-D history comparison. Its useful result is that the locally resummed current need not lose the small-energy/near-gradient RAW marker ports merely because a marginal covariance LAW would consume its bank.
