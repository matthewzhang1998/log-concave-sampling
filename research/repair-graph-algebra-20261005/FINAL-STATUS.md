## Limits that remain explicit

The formal trace relation does not gain reserve, prove a finite heat remainder, or make its negative cross-heat factor positive. Same-graph edge insertion is not automatically the existing inter-packet bank-tree native port. Arbitrary retained observers and same-vertex trace classes need their own image/current certificates. Native-to-ideal quadratic norm floors cannot become exact higher current jets. Formal finite generation does not establish original-target closure or c(P)=o(P).

The audited owned-clock extension is compatible with the framework and supplies finite heat reentry; it does not supply an indefinitely renewable resource. The separately audited carrier-stable port adds positive common-carrier superposition only within its ownership boundary and below its native quadratic floor.
