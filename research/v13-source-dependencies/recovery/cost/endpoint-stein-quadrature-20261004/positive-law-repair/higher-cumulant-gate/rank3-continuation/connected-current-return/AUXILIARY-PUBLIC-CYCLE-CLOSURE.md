## 7. Reusable operation and remaining induction test

The reusable operation is **auxiliary Gaussian edge closure**: cut chosen coefficient-graph edges into external slots of a literal native VALUE forest, assign the two ends of each edge the same fresh Gaussian caller, give those callers zero physical readout, and condition on them for complete prior replacement. It yields a positive packet whose leading coefficient is the Gaussian average of the opened forest. Coefficient normalization, covariance fills, actual derivative paths and all auxiliary connected descendants are computed from the actual program.

It is admitted only after checking:

1. Every resulting C_k source is still a legal original-gradient private block, with exact anchors and actual small radius.
2. Every conditional selected input is independent of its current source's private/probe banks given the captured auxiliary roots.
3. All partial Wick traces needed through the cutoff have a one-Hilbert/all-cut certificate. A sufficient check, proved here, partitions the graph into open native chunks that each keep a physical slot and contain at most one occurrence of each paired auxiliary Gaussian color.
4. Every derivative-hit/mark-consuming history retains those conditions, or receives a separately admitted type. A mean-current contraction that removes a chunk's last physical slot does not pass automatically.
5. The actual longest-spine/force-grade return and every width, caller, clock, floor and complete copy cost are recorded.

For this eight-force input these checks hold, with explicit six-force root spines and all nonleading conditional grades at least twelve. For a generic later branching graph, a child feedback can omit side subtrees, so total force count alone is not a termination proof. Its actual history-specific root-spine statistic must be retained and rechecked. Fixed-target combinatorial growth is acceptable; inverse-heat-dependent recursion depth is not.

The newly raised six-force/six-mark covariance current still needs a sign-programmable whole-bank coefficient bridge with its tight width/current return. This note does not prove that port, the nonlinear same-carrier mean/covariance ports, or a global closed queue. Therefore c(P)/P -> 0 remains unproved. It does remove the concrete eight-force cycle as an automatic dead end and supplies a literal operation that an eventual induction can reuse under its explicit preservation checks.
