## 4. Exact zero-subtree failure, not a numerical floor

Set both side crosses to zero. Then u=v=0, but the ideal side outputs S,T remain standard Gaussian external probes. Already in scalar dimension,

    V = lambda theta^2 S T,
    log E exp(V) = -1/2 log(1-lambda^2 theta^4)

## 5. Grouped contraction lemma: the cycle does not force a D loss

Let A and C be tensors whose external individual physical slots have dimension D. Suppose each has at least one uncontracted external slot and all proper matrix cuts bounded by K_A,K_C. Contract an arbitrary nonempty shared multi-index, treating that entire multi-index as one summation index. For any nontrivial external cut, write the contraction as sum_i A_i tensor C_i. Operator Cauchy-Schwarz bounds its operator norm by

    ||sum_i A_i A_i^*||^(1/2) ||sum_i C_i^* C_i||^(1/2),

or the reversed orientation. At least one orientation has a nonempty local row cut in both factors: if one factor has all its external slots on one side, use the orientation that leaves those slots as rows. Thus both factors are proper flattenings, and the output cut is at most K_A K_C.

For a connected graph of blocks with NO self-contracted edge inside an initial block, each block retaining at least one physical slot throughout, successively merge two connected groups, contracting **all edges between those groups at once**. Every group retains a physical slot, so induction applies even when a merge closes graph cycles. Final proper cuts are bounded by the product of block cuts. Isolating any one D-dimensional physical index yields full HS at most sqrt(D) times that product. Never estimate a free scalar trace by D and then multiply another Hilbert factor. These hypotheses exclude an initial block T_(i,a,b,c,d)=e_i delta_ab delta_cd/D with its two index pairs self-contracted: its proper cuts can be at most one while the resulting vector has norm D. Replacing those self-contractions by identity blocks would violate the requirement that every block retain a physical slot. A later mark-consuming operation must recheck this restriction; this lemma is not a bound for arbitrary tensor networks.

An actual one-Hilbert refinement is also available within the stated class. Start from a block carrying a declared HS energy h. When attaching another block, flatten the already merged group as (old external)|(all shared indices), and the new block as (all shared indices)|(new external). Ordinary HS-times-operator multiplication bounds the new HS norm by the old h times the new proper-cut bound. Grow a connected group, contracting all shared edges at each attachment. Uniform proper cuts of unmarked blocks permit a single actual marked energy to remain integrated over any shared old roots.

Apply this with each B block the intact four-force root spine, which has two physical and two side slots. Its proper cuts are the native tree-cut bounds. Every trace-family coefficient is a connected ring of B/B^T blocks with their two physical slots still exposed, not a scalar with all indices closed. The u,v terms add marked side-leaf blocks. Hence every displayed offspring has all proper cuts Lambda and full HS Lambda sqrt(D), times its exact scalar amplitude. This lemma is uniform at every fixed contraction order. A separate additional-score certificate is needed whenever a consumer differentiates a grouped coefficient.

The first cycle's clock factor is w^4/sigma_2^4. Its node sum, also with one explicitly charged original-root inverse shield, is summable: Delta_2^4/sigma_2^5 is summable. Thus this concrete eighth-grade coefficient is Lambda A^8 sqrt(D), with all proper cuts Lambda A^8. There is no hidden D cost or first new inverse-A replication count at this bounded step.
