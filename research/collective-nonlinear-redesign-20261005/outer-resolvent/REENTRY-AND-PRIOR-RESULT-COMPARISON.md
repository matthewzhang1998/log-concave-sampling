## 2. The reusable analytical input contract

The conditional-noise lemma accepts:

- Joint standard Gaussians (X,Y), correlation q<1, sigma=sqrt(1-q^2)
- Conditional on Y, a private Gaussian source b(Y,Z) independent of X, with FULL-bank operator first <=L
- A consumer F with ordinary Lipschitz constant K, without requiring F to be a gradient
- The actual outer resolvent R1 on the final conditional-X mean

Comparing two such b sources with conditional-mean mismatch delta_m costs

    K delta_m + (K/2)(L1^2+L2^2)sqrt(D)(1/2+1/sigma).

There is no private-bank dimension factor. It preserves all conditional means in the proof and needs no derivative of b in Y. Original VALUE construction remains a separate requirement; the theorem does not execute m(Y).

The lemma does not export a strong sample approximation to m, a W2 comparison of the raw shifted laws, or a joint result retaining Y or either private Z as an output observer. The dual test is specifically smoothed by the outer R1. Those distinctions are essential for reuse.

## 3. First new debt under a putative repeat application

Applying the new source theorem again does not automatically improve accuracy. The finite output residual is near-gradient with private first O(A), curl O(A^2), and energy O(A^2); it is not a newly certified anchored convex gradient in the original input class. The completed consumer returns a Gaussian mean law, not a strong mean vector that can be inserted into the next force.

More specifically, the weak lemma gives the quadratic small factor L^2 only when the next COMPLETE private-bank first L is genuinely smaller. The new target bias A^(11/4) does not itself shrink that next L. In the present graph the future-force bank first stays O(A), and the final outer-root residual first stays O(A). Reusing the same estimate therefore returns the same fixed-grade allowance. Assigning the smaller bias as a new private first or source radius would be an unsupported type conversion.

The present proof also pays `A^3sqrt(D)` to replace the genuine nested force F2 by F1. For a goal beyond grade three, that omission must be corrected at the actual nonlinear-history target; it is not removable by reducing the quadrature tolerance or changing the delay alone.

To turn this mechanism into an any-order recurrence, at least the following concrete gates remain:

1. An executable new conditional source preserving the needed nonlinear ancestry, whose next conditional mean is the actual required next target.
2. A proved decrease of its full-bank first/conditional-cumulant error, or a higher cancellation that removes the A^3 nested-history term, without strong replication or an expectation leaf.
3. A native returned type usable by that source, with all required retained variables and correlations, rather than a terminal mean law relabeled as a coupled force.
4. Complete original-VALUE and dimension/radius/clock/precision/replay costs for each step, with an established recurrence exponent.

No such recurrence is proved here. The new accepted next consumer is exactly the pinned gradient-plus-near-gradient own-mean compiler at the exported ports. Original anchor/scale normalization preserves the original gradient interval, but rescaling the same theorem merely reproduces the same fixed exponent; it is not an order upgrade. If the cutoff depends on a live scale, its derivative and precision chains are additional recorded obligations.

The new mechanism directly defeats the old unchanged-graph A^2 radial witness with a small finite coherent source. It does so uniformly over the unrestricted C2 class using only source firsts. It does not close the user's larger general-class any-order sublinear-cost objective. A report should state both facts together.
