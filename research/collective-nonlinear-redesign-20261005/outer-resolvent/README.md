# Outer-resolvent construction and covariance-matched reentry gate

## Accepted results

1. A new finite delayed original-VALUE graph has canonical-m3 exact-outer mean bias <=5 A^(11/4)sqrt(D), uniformly over the original anchored C2 convex-gradient class for A<=1/12. It has J+2 terminal VALUES, J=O(log^2(1/A)), and 3D private Gaussian roots after the finite outer rule. The actual near-gradient ports, full cost, floors and guards are audited.
2. A covariance-matched conditional weak lemma gives a quartic one-energy outer-resolvent remainder. With consumer Lipschitz K, private-bank firsts L1,L2, conditional Gaussian gap sigma, mean discrepancy delta_m and covariance L2-HS discrepancy delta_Sigma, its exact bound is

       K delta_m +(K/2)(1/2+1/sigma)delta_Sigma
        +(Ksqrt(D)/3)(L1^3+L2^3)(1+1/sigma+sqrt(2)/sigma^2).

   The proof uses only source firsts and exactly two Sobolev derivatives of R1. It is independently audited. It is a consumer contract, not an executed covariance producer.
3. A finite original-C-infinity four-clock fixture with perfectly matched conditional force means has an outer first-chaos covariance debt >A^3/500 for A<=.01. This verifies the precise missing target-four input rather than merely proposing a norm barrier.
4. The existing fullCovF2/F3 reserve is checked against that port. It has the correct named conditional covariance and caller, but returns a fixed-buffer terminal LAW. Neither its known carrier nor its residual first supplies the small RAW covariance-matched force. The alternative genuine joint LAW join and its shrinking-buffer obligations are written explicitly.

## Novelty and limits

The standalone 11/4 graph is not a grade upgrade over the active transport-based general-C2 A^3 force-mean result; that old mean theorem also approximates m3 to order A^3 after a coherent target comparison. Its new content here is the direct conditional covariance/outer-resolvent mechanism and finite realization, without importing that transport theorem.

No new endpoint order, general-class any-order recurrence, c(P)/P result, or fully executed native completion is claimed. An A^(16/5) tradeoff is shown only CONDITIONAL on a new compatible small-RAW covariance return. The existing fixed-buffer covariance LAW cannot simply be relabeled to satisfy it.
