# Ports and full cost for the frozen-clock collective-history graph

This exports the finite source in STRATIFIED-COLLECTIVE-HISTORY.md. It does not independently implement or certify an imported native completion service. All such services retain their actual guards and complete occurrence bills.

## Fixed-clock finite outer graph

Draw one clock list U from the displayed stratified law. Conditional on U, retain it as a frozen independent label across EVERY bank, compared source, caller capture, and replay used to produce one completed output. A new final sample may draw a fresh U. The positive mixture, rather than an unexecuted expectation over U, supplies the final clock integration.

Use the pinned deterministic positive outer Hermite rule (omega_l,t_l), with t_l in [0,1], sum omega_l=1, and sum omega_l t_l=1/2. For the same private roots G,Z_0,...,Z_(M-1) at every outer node, put

    x_l=t_l z+sqrt(1-t_l^2)G.

Run the exact finite OU Markov chain and the two collective convex averages of the companion theorem with caller x_l and the common Z bank. Let B_l denote its force Bhat, T_l=g(x_l-B_l), and

    B_raw=sum_l omega_l g(x_l),
    E_raw=sum_l omega_l[T_l-g(x_l)].

The private Gaussian dimension is (M+1)D. The clocks are M independent scalar labels; they can themselves be generated by M standard Gaussian scalars through their CDF, but they are not differentiated coordinates of the conditional native source. A completed conditional service is mixed over these labels only AFTER its conditional guarantee is established.

The Gaussian mean of B_raw+E_raw is Q_out mu_U(z). Every original site is real. The terminal g is evaluated only after the complete collective B_l has been assembled; it is never replaced by a finite Taylor stencil.

## Exact first/curl bounds

For fixed clocks every OU row is

    X_i=q_i x + R_i Z, ||R_i||<=1, 0<q_i<1.

The inner Hhat_i is a convex combination of g(X_j). Thus both its x first and its whole-bank Z first are bounded by A. Both firsts of Bhat are bounded by

    K=A(1+A).

Write H=Dg(x-Bhat), H0=Dg(x). For each outer term,

    D E=(H-H0)D x-H D Bhat.

The matrix H-H0 is symmetric and has norm at most A. The second term is bounded by AK=A^2(1+A), with original factor order intact. Let beta=sum omega_l sqrt(1-t_l^2)<=sqrt(3)/2. Then

    raw private first <=sqrt[beta^2(A+AK)^2+(AK)^2],
    raw retained-z first <=(A+AK)/2.

Lift E_raw into the G block and zeros into the remaining M Gaussian blocks. The symmetric leading G-block term contributes no skew. All other diagonal skew and off-diagonal terms give

    raw curl <=(2 beta+1) AK
              <=(1+sqrt(3)) A^2(1+A).

For 0<A<=1/2, safe half-variance normalized ports are

    private first <=3A,
    curl <=6A^2=(3A)(2A).

Subtract and restore the actual caller origin at ALL private Gaussian roots zero, keeping the same frozen clocks and every descendant. This leaves private first/curl unchanged and at most doubles the retained-z first. Original exterior source/anchor/scale dependencies remain live and must receive their own recorded first/adjoint chains; the z bound is not substituted for an arbitrary exterior caller.

These bounds do not grow with M. This follows from full-bank operator norms and convex absolute weights, not from a coordinatewise bound mistakenly summed over M.

## Energy, zero, and original numerical floors

Because each OU node is marginal standard under z and private Gaussian roots, positive averaging gives

    ||Bhat||_p <=A(1+A)||N(0,I_D)||_p,
    ||E_raw||_p <=A^2(1+A)||N(0,I_D)||_p.

Conditionally on z, each full row has caller coefficient in [0,t_l] and Gaussian part of norm at most one. A convenient caller envelope is

    ||E_raw||_(Lp|z)
      <=A^2(1+A)[|z|/2+kappa_p sqrt(D)].

The caller origin is at most A^2(1+A)|z|/2. No nonzero origin is silently erased. When caller and all Gaussian roots are zero, the entire finite chain, both averages, and every source site are exactly zero for the anchored original source. When g=0 the source vanishes for every clock list.

If each actual original VALUE has absolute error at most nu, then every first-bank Y_i has error at most nu; each Hhat_i has error at most nu; each second-bank value has error at most (1+A)nu; Bhat has the same bound; and T has error at most

    (1+A+A^2)nu.

The residual adds one baseline nu, giving (2+A+A^2)nu. Captures, changed numerical versions and replays have their own full allowances. Positivity of all average weights is essential to these node-count-free floors.

The exact-real theorem does not make numerical random-clock or row errors free. Encode q_i strictly inside its bin, retain the order, evaluate H coefficients as (q_i-e^(-b_(i+1)))/q_i and w_j/q_i, and verify nonnegative normalized weights on the actual version. Tiny Gaussian time gaps require stable scalar known-row arithmetic, an actual row-error budget, and any necessary precision/adaptive sampling allowance. Last-bin Hhat is exactly Y_last and should not divide a nonzero suffix by q_last. No unpriced clipping of negative variances or weights is allowed. All coefficient, original-HVP, clock, finite-mode, filter, fill, arithmetic and replay floors remain absolute.

## Positive own-mean service and complete VALUE bill

Use independent COMPLETE baseline/residual banks conditional on z and U, positive half-variance shares, and the pinned coisometry/own-mean services. A safe parameter choice for the residual interface is

    ell_E=3A, a_seed=2A, padding mu=A,
    private dimension=(M+1)D.

A<=1/12 supplies only ell_E<=1/4. All actual native radius, caller, clock/filter, mode, active-dimension, tolerance and precision guards remain imposed. The existing bracket at these declared parameters is

    ell_E(a_seed+mu)+ell_E^3(1+mu^(-1/2))
      =9A^2+27A^3+27A^(5/2)=O(A^2).

Combined with residual energy O(A^2 sqrt(D)), it gives a guarded O(A^4 sqrt(D)) residual completion allowance. Higher target accuracy still requires an actually proved higher-order completion service; merely increasing M does not remove a fixed compiler floor. The baseline's gradient order remains separate.

A terminal occurrence has exactly 2M+1 original VALUES. A residual occurrence has 2M+2 before exact-key sharing. Thus the full original-VALUE bill is

    Q_rule + Q_clock/known_setup + Q_captured
      + N_out N_B +(2M+2)N_out N_E
      + Q_known/numerical/replay.

N_B,N_E include every occurrence in the fully expanded native programs, all later replays, independent complete banks, fills, buffers, marks, clocks, finite modes, captured origins, and their new dimension/radius parameters. Each derivative/adjoint sweep uses one original HVP at each recorded original VALUE site. A changed input, source version, scalar coefficient or clock list replays the corresponding complete graph. Discarded primals pay full replay. The complete cost cannot be inferred from 2M+1 alone.

With a conditional completed-service allowance epsilon_comp(U) and a deterministic outer-rule tolerance delta_out, the positive-mixture integrated W2 error to N(m3(z),I) is at most

    24 A^2(1+A)sqrt(D)/M
      + delta_out A(1+A+A^2)sqrt(D)
      + ||epsilon_comp(U)||_2
      + restored absolute floors.

The outer allowance follows from ||mu_U||_2<=A(1+A+A^2)sqrt(D) uniformly in U and the pinned Hermite multiplier operator certificate. Freezing a single outer resolvent clock instead of using this valid outer rule is NOT covered.

Choosing M>=24(1+A)A^(2-p) gives a general-class target-bias term <=A^p sqrt(D). Its occurrence count therefore has inverse-A exponent p-2; private Gaussian dimension grows by the same factor. This is a rigorous linear-cost collective alternative to naive nested M^2 replication, but it is not a zero-exponent or o(p) any-order mechanism. Completion and dimension costs may further increase the complete bill.
