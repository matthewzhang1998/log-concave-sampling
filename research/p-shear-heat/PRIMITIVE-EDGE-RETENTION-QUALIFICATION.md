## 1. The real cross-width edge

The scalar-shear side source has terminal width s_j and scalar width
eta_j=b s_j^2. Each individual block pair uses matched widths internally.
However the middle known skew J0 swaps the two active channels.
An output-nearest scalar primitive in pair1 has readout rho/eta_j and feeds
an original terminal query in pair2 with receiving width s_j. The resulting
ORIGINAL-PRIMITIVE edge is

    rho s_j/eta_j = rho/(b s_j).

Its worst value at the chosen orientation-only clock s_min comparable to b
is Lambda rho/b^2. This row is not removed by the small complete first of a
pair, nor by a covariance law. The opposite edge is
rho eta_j/s_j=rho b s_j and is already small.

With b=A^zeta and rho=A^gamma, a sufficient strict small-edge condition is

    gamma>2 zeta.

Together with the complete outward root/caller guard3zeta+gamma<g, it requires

    5 zeta<g,   2zeta<gamma<g-3zeta.                    (1)

For example g=1,zeta=1/10,gamma=3/10 satisfies both strictly. Logarithmic
factors are included before the common smallness guard is chosen.
