# Root-heavy amplitudes close the permanent-leaf C3 cycle sector

2026-10-05. New local construction. The conditional/auxiliary cycle sector has a finite well-founded correction rule. This is not an all-current, whole-old-bank or terminal sampler theorem.

## 1. Useful result

The same-grade return in the sealed doubled-four-cycle test is an artifact of assigning one alpha power to every force occurrence. It does not survive a different exact allocation of those powers.

Let tau=alpha^(1/3). In a graph with n C3 centers and one permanent physical C0 leaf at each center, write its scalar amplitude as

    alpha^A H z^n,       z=w_c/sigma_c^3,
    A=n+d,              d>0.

All tensor labels, primitive private shields, original clocks, old query roots and positive readout normalizations remain literal. H is the remaining root-held clock/coefficient factor. Put amplitude alpha^(1/2) z at each C3 center, alpha^(1/2) at each nonroot C0 leaf, and alpha^(d+1/2) H at the physical root leaf. Known coefficient and inverse readout constants, and the required sign, belong at that root.

The product is exactly alpha^(n+d) H z^n. Every center source has radius at most Lambda alpha^(1/6), and the root has radius at most Lambda alpha^(d+1/2). The larger nonroot radii are intentional and are paid in the old selected-pair order and actual path ledger.

Choose any center spanning tree and any oriented edge a-b in it. Root at a's physical leaf, take the four-vertex root spine leaf(a)-a-b-leaf(b), and give every other center its own physical leaf as its selected child. Those side spines each have two force vertices. This choice is possible for every connected loopless center graph; it does not require a longest path.

For every connected nonmain conditional/auxiliary h-cluster coefficient, h>=2, let n' be its number of center occurrences. Its exact scalar amplitude is

    alpha^(n'+h d) H^h z^(n').

Its new surplus is d'=h d>=2d. Its center-width effective exponent is (2/3)n'+h d. Thus a queue ordered by surplus, with an effective-exponent output cutoff, terminates at every fixed target. Force count and effective exponent need not increase at each transition.

For the sealed eight-force input n=4,A=8, one has d=4. Its first four-center variance is now alpha^12 H^2 z^4, with effective exponent 32/3 instead of the input's 20/3. The old equal-amplitude realization had alpha^8 H^2 z^4. No coefficient contraction assumption is needed for this repair.

## 2. Precise graph family and legal VALUE execution

An admitted member consists of:

- A connected loopless 4-regular multigraph on n>=2 center occurrences. Parallel edges are allowed. Each center is the same kind of normalized, privately averaged original D^4 g coefficient as in the sealed two-cycle packet; original center labels may differ, but in the present width calculation each uses the same recorded primitive clock/shield type z.
- One distinct original C0 occurrence joined to each center by a leaf edge. That C0 has one permanent physical output slot. There are n physical marks, 2n force occurrences and no physical mark at a center.
- The exact original correlated query bank Q, its complete ancestry and captured observer rows; no independent resampling of Q is made.

Choose a center spanning tree. Keep the n leaf edges and its n-1 center edges, and open its remaining n+1 center edges. Each opened edge gets its own fresh D-dimensional Gaussian G_e, shared between its two endpoints and with zero physical readout. There is no loop, so each G_e occurs at most once at each center.

If a center has degree j in the center spanning tree, it has j+1 tree edges including its physical leaf edge and 4-j auxiliary slots. Once one incoming and one outgoing tree slot are selected, it has exactly three external probes. It is therefore the SAME legal C3 source, not a higher derivative or a tensor oracle. A C0 source is used at each physical leaf.

The source is the literal finite signed affine original-g VALUE sum C3 with its finite separate first-chaos filters, numerical private shields and Sobolev preparation. Its private selected Jacobians remain scalar sums of symmetric original Jacobians. Every old pair call uses a fixed finite order b, chosen before execution. A source sign changes an amplitude, never a probability or a variance share.

Given the entire actual Q and the complete auxiliary bank G, execute disjoint side subtrees with independent complete private tapes. Their ideal Gaussian outputs are independent standard Gaussians conditional on Q,G, even though their conditional relations with their physical leaf publics are nontrivial. The root-spine comparison is made before side replacements, following the sealed conditional chronological convention. It is not a backward conditioning of an already completed marginal comparison.

Read out the root output, the other n-1 physical leaf publics and an independent keep, with positive square shares in a newly reserved variance portion. All old public readouts stay unchanged. Source zero is the known affine Gaussian row; its equality with the reserve row is recorded before joining. Auxiliary Gaussians have zero physical readout.

## 3. Exact first cluster and complete finite return

Condition on Q,G. Collapse each selected Gaussian chain to its reverse Gaussian pair. Write q0 for the product of the four root-spine amplitudes, and q_s for the two amplitudes of each side spine. For exactly multilinear coefficient targets, the reverse recursion is

    Ytilde_s(theta) = Y_s + a_s q_s M_s(Ytilde_children(theta);Q,G) theta,
    V(theta) = c a_0 q0 theta^T M0(Ytilde_children(theta);Q,G) theta.

All Y_s before the shifts are independent standard Gaussians conditional on Q,G. Every side slot is separately linear and occurs once in its parent field. Therefore, in E_Y V, an unshifted side Y_s has zero conditional mean. Iterating through the finite tree forces every side spine to be inserted exactly once. The only one-cluster term is the full intended tree with every physical leaf retained. Auxiliary averaging closes precisely its original cut edges. No connectedness condition on the cut-edge complement is needed.

This argument is important: averaging a partially inserted side subtree as though it were an arbitrary G-dependent estimator would produce false lower-order terms. The chronological reverse variables are Gaussian and independent of the already fixed auxiliary bank.

Every other connected log coefficient has h>=2 root-spine copies. Expand its finite reverse-recursion jet, then apply the labelled Gaussian moment-cumulant formula to ALL side and auxiliary variables together. Each root-spine copy has two C3 centers, two physical C0 leaves, and alpha exponent d+2 under the chosen allocation. Every inserted side-spine copy adds one center, one marked C0 leaf, and alpha exponent one. If j side spines are inserted in total, then

    n'=2h+j,
    A'=h(d+2)+j=n'+h d.

The exact z power is n'. The exact root-held factor is H^h. Gaussian pairings, readouts, orientations and finite cumulant coefficients are computable scalar constants. They do not change these identities.

This is a finite-coefficient identity. No exponential integrability of the unbounded polynomial coefficient target is assumed. The actually executed finite fields remain bounded; Section 7 explains how to cut off the finite positive-law program rather than execute a formal series.

## 4. Closure of the permanent-leaf family and one Hilbert factor

Group each C3 center together with its own attached C0 physical leaf. Every initial chunk is exactly the two-force marked chunk used in the sealed packet's one-Hilbert proof. It has one permanent physical slot and four center-boundary slots; all proper cuts are bounded and one full HS norm costs C sqrt(D).

Every original tree contraction joins distinct chunks. A side Y color occurs once at its parent chunk. Every cut-edge auxiliary color occurs at most once in each initial chunk because the input center graph is loopless. Thus every Wick pair joins different initial chunks. A repeated old center label means a different force occurrence with its own private tape, not identification of the two graph vertices.

After every side/auxiliary Gaussian boundary is paired, each center retains precisely its attached marked leaf and has four inter-center half-edges. No operation consumes the chunk's physical mark. There are no center self-loops. A connected cumulant retains only connected diagrams across its already connected root-spine argument groups. Hence every nonzero connected offspring is again a connected loopless 4-regular center multigraph with one permanent C0 leaf per center.

The sealed restricted block-contraction proof now applies at EVERY generation: start one chunk in HS norm, attach adjacent chunks, and contract all edges to the previously built group at each attachment. Each new chunk has a nonempty contracted cut and a retained physical slot. HS-times-operator multiplication gives a single sqrt(D); the proper-cut version uses the same retained-mark block rule. This does not assign a physical mark to a center, and it never estimates an arbitrary self-trace from an abstract operator bound.

The critical improvement over the longest-spine proposal is structural: the selected spines are deliberately short and every retained center is always bundled with its own physical leaf. The scalar grade is then controlled by amplitude surplus instead of force-spine growth.

## 5. Surplus termination, including a countertest of the wrong order

It is useful to state the parameterized rule. Let 0<gamma<beta, tau=alpha^gamma, and put

    A=2 beta n+d,
    rho_root=H alpha^(d+beta),
    rho_nonroot_leaf=alpha^beta,
    rho_center=alpha^beta z.

Then every h-cluster offspring has A'=2 beta n'+h d, so d'=h d. The center-width exponent is

    e=(2 beta-gamma)n+d.

Choose 2 beta-gamma>0 and an initial strictly positive surplus d0. At a requested effective output cutoff P, every retained coefficient has d<P and n<P/(2 beta-gamma). At each recursive repair, d at least doubles. Thus the depth is at most floor(log_2(P/d0)), and each node has a finite amplitude/moment cutoff h<=floor(P/d). Its finite reverse expansion and Gaussian pairings give only finitely many histories.

The queue is not ordered by e alone. For beta=1/2,gamma=1/3, a hypothetical admitted input n=100,d=4 has e=212/3, while its root-only h=2 offspring n'=4 has e'=32/3. That feedback can be much larger than the input's norm allowance. It is nevertheless admitted at the next strictly larger surplus d'=8; after finitely many surplus doublings every emitted coefficient exceeds the requested cutoff. Coefficients newly exposed below the cutoff must be added to the queue even if their parent was small.

Consequently one must NOT drop an input solely because its own e>=P if choosing to execute its repair would expose lower-e feedback. The finite algorithm repairs exactly the initial required inputs and the below-cutoff descendants it emits. An above-cutoff offspring is left unexecuted and is charged as a residual current. No repair of a discarded high-grade offspring is performed.

With beta=1/2,gamma=1/3 and the actual initial n=4,d=4, all source exponents are positive and all retained n are bounded at a fixed P. The large numerical constants determine a computable small-alpha threshold; they do not spoil finite termination.

## 6. Actual radii, caller first, prior and precision ledgers

The old positive clock bound is retained:

    max z <= Lambda/tau,
    sum_nodes z^m sigma_c^(-r) <= Lambda tau^(-(m+r)).

Initially H is the sealed hatomega^4 times the known scalar coefficient/readout normalization. Under h-cluster feedback it becomes H^h. Its squared old leaf-clock factors are never removed. If a new root chooses a different physical leaf occurrence, that leaf's original clock remains in H. All old structural clocks, including ones whose force occurrences disappear from a particular root-only feedback, remain in the recorded coefficient.

The actual maximum center source radius is Lambda alpha^(beta-gamma). A nonroot leaf has radius Lambda alpha^beta; the root has radius Lambda alpha^(d+beta). Pick alpha so all complete finite-source, fixed-b pair and positive covariance guards hold. The original center injection at the m-th center on a root-to-node path has, after summing its actual center clock,

    Lambda alpha^[d+(m+1)beta] tau^[-(m+1)]
      = Lambda alpha^[d+(m+1)(beta-gamma)].

Extra leaf ancestors only improve this estimate. Root own-caller paths are bounded by Lambda alpha^(d+beta), using its retained positive leaf-clock integrability. Other leaf caller paths retain their own H clocks; even replacing their shield by the common tau floor is no worse at the present exponents. Auxiliary/private paths have at most the same shield count. A nonroot carrier has radius one and is charged with its strict-ancestor attenuation, rather than incorrectly multiplied by its own source radius.

For beta=1/2,gamma=1/3,d>=4, the worst displayed center first is at most Lambda alpha^(d+1/3), and root residual/private first is at most Lambda alpha^(d+1/2). These are far below O(alpha). Shared Q or auxiliary callers add their actual paths. The known complete old injection-row norms multiply these formulas and are never assumed to be one. Anchors on full recorded keys give the corresponding energy bound.

Every original VALUE perturbation at a source site follows the same path product with its explicit 1/(A sigma_v) source normalization. This determines downstream amplification for choosing its absolute arithmetic and source-error floor; it is not omitted from the query/replay bill.

The native prior bill is the actual finite tree sum

    Lambda sqrt(D) sum_v rho_v^b product_(a strict ancestor of v) rho_a,

with all clock/readout constants retained. A conservative bound may ignore the helpful ancestors and use rho_v<=Lambda alpha^(1/6). Thus b>6P, plus the explicitly computed downstream inverse-width/precision powers, suffices for a target alpha^P. The sharper ancestor bill is available but not needed for termination. Filter, calibration, finite means, positive clock quadrature, gate and arithmetic errors each get separate absolute floors after their downstream amplifications. Ideal mean-zero identities do not erase actual finite mean floors.

These are direct derivatives and value paths of the executed original-g graph. No law estimate was differentiated. They do not, by themselves, prove a terminal genuine-gradient/curl statement for an arbitrary joined old program.

## 7. Finite truncation and cost

The algorithm for a fixed P is finite:

1. Fix gamma=1/3,beta=1/2 and all initial physical/caller/clock data. Enumerate the required initial permanent-leaf currents, here the first eight-force return of the sealed two-cycle packet.
2. For each queued coefficient, choose a center spanning tree and the short-spine orientation above, allocate a positive reserve share, and form its finite original-g VALUE C0/C3 sources with the exact root normalization.
3. Enumerate its complete conditional/auxiliary reverse jet through the necessary positive-amplitude degree; use full labelled cumulants, retaining all signs and old labels. Add every below-cutoff nonmain offspring to the queue with d'=h d. Above-cutoff coefficients are residuals, not executed corrections.
4. Choose fixed pair orders, finite filters and absolute numerical floors for the entire finite queue before running it. Freeze all covariance shares and complete tapes, then execute the finite joined graph.

Positive variance shares may depend on the complete fixed-P list and the finite quadrature count. Their inverse readout powers are retained in Lambda and in the computed small-alpha guard. A graph with n centers has 2n complete old pair/filter calls, n+1 shared auxiliary D-roots, n marked public roots and its positive keep. Its local original-g VALUE work is

    n Q_C0,b + n Q_C3,b + Q_capture + Q_readout,

where Q_C3,b contains the eight-term signed affine source, all filters, private shields, pair/calibration calls and the chosen precision. For a finite queue T,

    Q_total = sum_(T) [n_T Q_C0,b_T+n_T Q_C3,b_T+Q_capture,T+Q_readout,T]
              + Q_required_ancestor_replays.

If an old caller changes, its full literal ancestor graph and every affected complete Q call are replayed. Primal records cannot be dropped for free. HVPs occur only in requested recorded-site first/adjoint sweeps, never as VALUE tensor queries.

The positive-law remainder is cut off on the finite VALUE/ideal-channel comparison, not justified by convergence of an unbounded polynomial MGF. First use the sealed root-first calibrated value hybrid to bounded native fields and their finite multilinear comparison. All actual selected matrices have a numerical covariance gap. Expand numerical covariance roots only to a fixed degree at the current bounded/gated child values, choosing a degree from the minimum positive radius exponent beta-gamma=1/6. A degree exceeding the requested propagated cutoff is finite. The square-root Taylor tail has an operator-norm bound times sqrt(D) when acting on its fresh Gaussian. Gate complements and calibration differences are paid in their required fixed moments. Only after this bounded finite comparison are the resulting polynomial jets used for the coefficient census.

At a fixed P this procedure uses finitely many Gaussian moments, finite matrix-gap Taylor bounds and finite node/path sums; it has no inverse-tau Monte Carlo bank. It imports the same admitted fixed-order pair, source calibration and bounded polynomial comparison interfaces as the sealed packet. The diagnostic code executes exact algebra and graph/exponent checks, not those imported native pair programs. No claim about eventual sublinearity c(P)=o(P) follows; even the conservative b>6P bill already shows why a separate complexity argument is required.

## 8. Complete old bank and exact boundary

The old bank is retained, not silently deleted. If B is the entire original Gaussian bank and nu_theta its actual physical tilt, the joined symbol remains

    Gaussian baseline + log E_(B under nu_theta)
       exp[L_old(B;theta)+sum_T K_T(B;theta)].

Each packet's intended coefficient cancels its queued coefficient pointwise in B. Therefore the tilt derivatives of that canceled coefficient cancel too. This does not remove the old-bank covariance of uncanceled conditional feedback with other old terms, or higher tilt derivatives of uncanceled coefficients.

This note does NOT admit the native producer and temporary covariance-test norms for those whole-old-bank currents. Derivatives can increase C3 order, create extra old-shield factors and leave the permanent-leaf family. Mixed old/new clusters need not have h>=2 copies of the new root spine, so the surplus-doubling proof cannot simply be applied to them. Their complete first, retained-observer and terminal curl proof is still separate.

Thus the precise result is a new bounded exact-amplitude mechanism that removes the same-effective-grade cycle obstruction in the closed conditional/auxiliary permanent-leaf C3 sector. It is useful input to an all-current construction, but it is not a completed all-current construction itself.
