## What can be inferred for the current fixed-rank return

If the **full stated LOW30 fixed-order compiler theorem** is admitted for the actual normalized C_k source at hand, and k,B are fixed, then its local inverse-alpha query exponent is **zero** when all radii, absolute numerical floors and source-label scales have fixed-power/public-log dependence on alpha. This is stronger than leaving the local `M_native(k,b,epsilon)` completely unknown. The citation and its eligibility assumptions must be imported explicitly. The short frozen-width port by itself does not display this serial recurrence. This audit located and checked the relevant statement and accounting; it is not a fresh independent verification of LOW30's entire finite-order induction.

Varying external original labels require a separate uniform source-radius bound. Lines 6517-6523 explicitly prohibit inferring it from a rough matrix label's operator moment alone.

The proof is a simultaneous induction for every fixed constructed order, not only the order-two seeds or a special small b. Its path analysis adds every shared-root direction. Applying it to a full graph still requires the graph's actual external-source bounds and strict-ancestor path accounting.

This stronger raw bound can help establish the common-carrier environment-stable hybrid proposed in the addendum. The carrier-compatible hybrid port remains unproved by the citations already checked.
