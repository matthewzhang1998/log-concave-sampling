Split a current into its conditional mean over the null/private bank and its centered part. Null/private Riesz differentiates only the endpoint; there is no root-free source-zero row in these directions. For the remaining finite G0-polynomial, base-row branches lower visible Hermite degree. ## 5. Precise sufficient replacement ports

Either of the following would complete the missing step, with B/Y retained as required and all native versions frozen.

**A. Carrier-retaining local replacement.** For each h, supply

    [ integral W2^2(Law(u_h^act | H,B,Y),
                     Law(u_h^ideal | H,B,Y)) dLaw(H) ]^(1/2)
         <= e_h(B,Y).

The H in this comparison is the same actual source-zero carrier, held exactly. Independent conditional couplings for the remaining packet tapes then produce a whole-group error at most `sum_h e_h`; no derivative of the other packets is needed. Uniform B/Y bounds suffice, or retain and integrate their complete L2 envelope. Strong same-raw-root error bounds are an even stronger sufficient version.

**B. Direct group replacement.** Prove, for the actual raw common-carrier group and its ideal counterpart,

    W2(group_actual, group_ideal)
       <= sum_h e_h + C_hybrid S^2 sqrt(D).

Both groups must use the stated shared-carrier geometry and untouched reserve. An environment-stable one-packet estimate with added term `C Gamma_h|r_h| S_-h sqrt(D)`, uniform across all hybrid stages, implies B by telescoping. Such a port can be weaker than A.

A marginal W2 certificate does not imply A. For example, in two dimensions let `X_r=R_r H`, where R_r is a rotation by r, and compare it with H. Marginal Gaussian laws agree exactly and `X_0=H`; energy and first of `X_r-H` are O(r). Yet the H-retaining conditional error is O(r), not an arbitrarily small native floor. This does not refute B: shared rotations generate quadratic group effects consistent with S^2.

One possible proof route for the weaker port requires an additional global carrier-Lipschitz envelope. Couple the packet outputs at their marginal error e_h and disintegrate their carriers. Their L2 carrier displacement is at most

    e_h + ||u_h^act||_2 + ||u_h^ideal||_2.

A **global** H-Lipschitz bound of size S_-h for the remaining environment prices that displacement at the required cross-root scale. The current Gaussian-Lp first bounds do not justify this step under an arbitrary coupling or along its non-Gaussian interpolation. A valid stronger envelope or replacement argument must be supplied.
