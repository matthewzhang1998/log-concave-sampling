## 2. The two occurrence calculation

In one spatial dimension let F(z_1,z_2)=f(z_1)g(z_2). The exact identities are

    H_ind F = (f''g+fg'')/2,
    H_com F = (f''g+2f'g'+fg'')/2,
    B_cross F = f'g',
    H_ind F = H_com F-B_cross F.                         (2)

If the two vertices were already joined, f'g' adds a PARALLEL edge. It does not split the original graph into two independent copies or create a new clock. The original edge and all physical marks survive.

For a current of rank r, take the concrete affine caller z_1=z_2=B and endpoint X=x(B)+eta Z, where B,Z are independent standard scalar Gaussians and eta>0. At the unconditional equality level, Gaussian integration by parts gives

Consequently the FULL local trace family is the three rows in (3), minus the bridge current f'g'. Even in this smallest example, a scalar score and two endpoint-chain rows remain. ## 3. Exact finite jets

For independent formal center variables set

    H_ind = (1/2) sum_v Delta_v,
    H_com = (1/2) (sum_v D_v) dot (sum_v D_v),
    B_cross = sum_(u<v) D_u dot D_v.

These are constant-coefficient commuting differential operators, and H_ind=H_com-B_cross. For every integer m>=0,

    sum_(q=0)^m s^q H_ind^q/q!
      = sum_(l+j<=m) s^(l+j) H_com^l (-B_cross)^j/(l! j!)
                                                    modulo s^(m+1).   (4)

All product hits, including repeated hits at one endpoint of several distinct-vertex bridges, occur with their exact multinomial coefficients. B_cross^j produces an ordered sequence of j choices of unordered occurrence pairs; quotienting by identical resulting labeled terms restores the usual multiplicities. A self-loop is never introduced by B_cross, including when a pair is chosen repeatedly.

This is a finite formal jet identity. It neither asserts convergence of a heat Taylor series at zero private width nor represents exp(-s B_cross) as a positive kernel. That operator need not be positive.

For nonnegative unequal variance rates lambda_v, the same statements use H_ind,lambda=(1/2)sum_v lambda_v Delta_v, H_com,lambda=(1/2)(sum_v sqrt(lambda_v)D_v)^2, and B_cross,lambda=sum_(u<v)sqrt(lambda_u lambda_v)D_u dot D_v.
