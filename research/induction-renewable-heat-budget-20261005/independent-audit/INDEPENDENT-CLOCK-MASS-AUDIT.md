## 1. Positive quadrature and its new information

    c+13a/24 = 1-23a/24 < 1.

This proves a genuine uniform scalar Hermite-multiplier approximation with J,m logarithmic in the inverse tolerance, not merely a sampled low-Hermite-order rule.

Every individual positive Lebesgue weight is at most the panel mass a. Every node satisfies 1-r>=a. Consequently its actual resolvent mass obeys

    omega = w r^(j-1) <= a <= 1-r <= 1-r^2.

This is stronger information than total quadrature mass control. It is attached to the original primitive node, not to its derivative descendants or to an invented clock. A fixed-rank constant covers the small-k cases.

The revised scalar preparation is adequate: rounded nodes stay inside their original panels, positive Lebesgue weights are renormalized to exact panel mass, and omega uses the actual prepared node's density. The bound sup_L |d(r^L)/dr|<=C/(1-r), together with a node displacement O(epsilon_p a), makes its weighted panel error O(epsilon_p a). ## 8. Non-renewal and limits of the sharpness claim

The center of the star has k=n-2. On an endpoint panel of distance comparable to h^2, t is comparable to h and original panel mass is comparable to h^2. Positivity and pigeonhole give a node with omega t^(-(n-2)) at least a constant times h^(-(n-4))/M_panel. Thus a public-log number of positive atoms cannot uniformly remove another algebraic width power by subdividing that same panel mass. To gain h^eta at every such node requires at least order h^(-eta) atoms. Other primitive/structural clocks can be held in fixed interior panels, so their positive masses do not supply a compensating algebraic gain for this star obstruction.

The proposed linear-in-P cost consequence is correctly restricted to eliminating the remaining star charge by positive atomization, together with the unchanged heat-remainder requirement. It is not a lower bound for arbitrary algorithms or for signed cancellation. The theorem does not assert that such atomization alone would suffice for arbitrary restoration: reserve, first and old-mixing obstructions remain.

A bank hit creates no fresh original clock; cloning duplicates both source and weight and is paid by the root-copy recurrence. A dummy clock is unrelated to the old private width. The warning is mathematically appropriate.

With the mass charge already used, the maximum grade of the present heat/one-Riesz-old-mixing certificate is exactly 41/5 at rank eight with s=1.
