## Boundary

The fixed-family native returns import the marked-spanning-tree width-zero conditional port, actual old-first/zero-readout endpoint hypotheses, positive reserve/readout allocation, and complete numerical/native floor propagation. Those contracts must be checked at their real constants. No marginal pair LAW certificate is upgraded to expose an internal carrier; no new shared-carrier amalgamation is assumed. A small ideal tensor does not certify an actual caller path.

This work creates no new target-heat mismatch. It also does not erase any original clock truncation debt. It does not prove a general all-rank queue, uniformly bounded higher caller losses, public-log n-copy cubature, or c(P)=o(P). The conservative finite node count can alter amplitude/readout grades when accuracy scales with alpha; the executable admission test must expose that cost.
