## 1. Exact one-pair stationary channel

Let H_R be the selected symmetric heat matrix of an admitted genuine-gradient source h_R of first at most one. Let D be a known symmetric contraction. Run the finite genuine-gradient pair on

    rho diag(h_R,h_R),

with its actual conditional reference selected matrix

    A_R=c_sel rho diag(H_R,H_R).

The two active gradient blocks use the same center, heat width and finite source version; their coordinates remain distinct. Define known contractions

    V=[D;I]/sqrt(2),       U=[I,-D]/sqrt(2).

The exact known Gram identities are UU*=V*V=(I+D^2)/2. The input fill uses the full 2d-by-2d matrix I-VV*, as displayed below. Thus the following complete positive channel is valid:

    x=V p+sqrt(I-VV*) Z_in,
    y=P_B(rho diag(h_R,h_R);x,Omega),
    Q_C(p)=U y+sqrt(I-UU*) Z_out.                         (1)

At fixed coefficient records R, p is standard. All new pair and fill records are independent, and every known square root is charged. The exact selected coefficient is

    K_R=U A_R V=(c_sel rho/2)(H_R D-D H_R),               (2)

which is skew. The reference is stationary. The actual source-dependent incoming/private first is O(rho), with root/caller O(rho/v) for the original affine clock chart. The known source-zero carrier Z_C is standard and independent of p,R. Its residual has ordinary profile O_p(rho sqrt(d)). No sample of H_R is evaluated.

## 2. Exact affine-source specialization and fork normalization

For the projection-D source f=r[g((I+aD)W)-g(W)], use the held common-input source

    H_R=J_B(X)/L0,
    h_R(z)=[h_B(cX+v z)-h_B(cX)]/(v L0),

    c_side=-q tau/(b c_sel^2 rho).                        (3)

Using independent complete marked and skew banks conditional on the SAME new p,R, emit

    b Q_E(p)+c_side Q_C(p)+sqrt(v0-b^2-c_side^2) Z_keep.

The shared-passive cross covariance is

    M_R K_R*+K_R M_R*
      =-c_sel^2 rho Sym(J_f[H_R,D]).

Equations(2)--(3) therefore give exactly v0 I-q O_R, O_R=-Sym(J_f C_R). The actual signed tau is used; no norm bound is substituted for its value. The tau=0 branch is literal c_side=0 and requires no division by tau.

These inverse norms are numerical constants, not inverse protected widths. The projection-D formula above is simpler and sufficient for the survived counterexample.

## 3. Actual energy, first, finite law and work

The exact retained split is still

    [b Z_E+c_side Q_C+known keep] + b(Q_E-Z_E).

The deleted mark is b e; the unmarked side is RETAINED. Its ordinary residual profile is now

    O_p(sqrt(d) |c_side|rho)=O_p(sqrt(d) kappa/b),

and its root/caller path is O(kappa/(b v)) before the original positive clock weights are attached. Its private first is O(kappa/b), not O(kappa/(b rho)). The physical public p-first is also O(kappa/b); the actual coefficient op may be smaller after calibration, but is not used as an executed first bound.

At fixed R the finite law bill is the safe triangle

    |b| delta_E + |c_side| delta_C,

where delta_C is the one gradient-pair prior/restoration error. No coupling retaining the actual source-zero carrier Z_E is needed or asserted. Only the new p is retained by the conditional pair theorem. All old root observers are handled later by the held whole-R Riesz/Price current, under its stated mean/gap premises.

Using the positive original-clock readout sqrt(w_j), the weighted absolute root/caller sum is kappa/b times sum sqrt(w_j)/v_j, and the complete independent-block root first has the corresponding square sum. Both are polylogarithmic. Therefore for kappa=A^(1+g), b=A^z the derivative/retained-output guard is simply

    0<z<g,

with logarithmic slack. Let rho=A^gamma for any fixed 0<gamma<1+g-z. Then c_side has positive exponent 1+g-z-gamma, so b^2+c_side^2 fits a fixed variance share for small enough A. Unlike the two-pair construction, gamma does not consume the retained first budget.

For a side prior delta_C<=Lambda sqrt(d) rho^B, the amplified bill is Lambda sqrt(d)(kappa/b)rho^(B-1), plus all declared finite-source/row floors. A finite B can be chosen for each fixed nominal accuracy, with its actual B-dependent radius guard, source dimension and caller profile. This is not an energy-relative guarantee when e is smaller than its assigned envelope.

Each clock now executes ONE marked pair and ONE complete two-block genuine-gradient pair, plus known fills and source-center replays. A block source evaluation calls both displayed original-gradient charts; it is not billed as a single unknown matrix action. The whole real work and tape remain counted after auxiliary deletion.
