# A stationary marked-input skew channel for admitted common-root heat matrices

## 0. Exact scope

This constructs a finite VALUE response to a specified skew matrix word times a FORWARD marked coefficient. It has one actual incoming energy mark and avoids an unsupported shift of a Gaussian passive. The primitive heat matrices are analytical targets of already-admitted genuine-gradient pair sources. This note does not identify separately heat-averaged primitive Hessians with the unaveraged Hessian word in a nonlinear native graph, and does not discard the two-curl/adjoint residue in true orientation.

The source uses original gradient VALUES, known affine Gaussian maps, and the admitted finite VALUE pair routines. All finite versions and source labels are fixed. The whole actual difference is retained for its energy/first proof; its two mean-defect terms are bounded marginally. No joint law of a marked pair and its source-zero carrier is assumed.

The symbol c_sel below is the fixed numerical selected-pair constant denoted s0 in the admitted theorem. The source widths s_t,s_0 are separate. Choose a KNOWN signed word coefficient τ with |τ|≤κ; bounds use κ, while the actual readout uses τ. A native application must supply this actual coefficient, not replace it by a mere norm bound.

## 1. Marked pair and the two genuine-gradient blocks

Let the complete square marked source f_R have first A, curl at most κ, and conditional centered energy e_R. The public G is standard CONDITIONAL on R,θ; in the intended owned Gaussian-root construction R is independent of G conditional on θ. All R records are retained during the following conditional calculations. This conditional standard-marginal premise is separate from their later independence from the old consumer. Take the existing near-gradient VALUE pair

    Y=Pv(f_R;G,Ω_E)=Z_E+R_E^val,
    M_E(R)=c_sel E Df_R.

Here G is the retained n-dimensional standard public, and Z_E is the routine's KNOWN standard Gaussian source-zero carrier, independent of G and R. The actual carrier-subtracted energy is at most Λ e_R after G and its private tapes are integrated. Complete residual first is O(A) with the usual padding choice. The printed conditional pair comparison has error δ_E(R), and its analytical reference is

    M_E(R)G+sqrt(I−M_E(R)M_E(R)*) Z.

After G is integrated, this reference is standard conditional on R. The joint statement retains G. The actual source pair need not have a known joint coupling with its own Z_E; none is used below.

For example, the existing source with κ≥A² and μ=A^(4/3) gives

    ||δ_E||L2(R)≤Λ[κ+A^(7/3)]e+ε_E,

where e²=E_R e_R². Its actual source/query clock factors remain in A,κ,e_R and the caller certificates if the source was normalized from a smaller original width.

At the SAME R, let q_t(R),q_0(R) be literal saved finite labels. Choose declared positive widths s_t,s_0 and exact original gradient sources g_t,g_0 of first at most one. Define a block-gradient source on R^(2d) by

    h_R(x_t,x_0)=ρ( [g_t(q_t+s_t x_t)−g_t(q_t)]/s_t,
                    [g_0(q_0+s_0 x_0)−g_0(q_0)]/s_0 ).

Its full private first is at most ρ and it is a genuine gradient in its two blocks. Let

    D_R=diag(H_t(R),H_0(R)),
    H_i(R)=E Dg_i(q_i(R)+s_i Z_i).

A complete fixed-order gradient pair on h_R has selected matrix c_sel ρ D_R. Its mean error under a standard passive is ε_h(R), bounded by the actual √(2d)ρ^B prior, amplified source/numerical floors, and the fixed-order radius guard. Its carrier is independent of that passive; its incoming first is O(ρ), and its whole residual/private first is O(ρ).

The two matrices D_R below are the SAME coefficient: two independent complete pair banks use identical source versions, q_t,q_0,s_t,s_0. They do not sample independent coefficient roots.

## 2. Known rows and the stationary chain

Let P,C:R^n→R^d be known contractions, with P coisometric where the physical source requires it. Set

    L=[P*,C*]:R^(2d)→R^n,
    J=[[0,I],[-I,0]],
    B_in=L*/sqrt(2),      B_out=L/sqrt(2).

Both B rows are contractions and J is a KNOWN orthogonal Gaussian map. Execute

    x0=B_in x+sqrt(I−B_in B_in*) Z_in,
    y1=PB(h_R;x0,Ω_1),
    y2=PB(h_R;J y1,Ω_2),
    K_R(x)=B_out y2+sqrt(I−B_out B_out*) Z_out.          (1)

Every square root in(1) is of a known constant matrix. No unknown selected matrix is queried. All pair banks are complete and independent conditional on R; all displayed fills are independent before being reused for a paired difference.

For the exact linear Gaussian pair references, the chain is stationary and its selected matrix is

    N_R=(c_selρ)² L D_R J D_R L*/2.                       (2)

In particular L D_R J D_R L* is skew, and equals

    P*H_t H_0 C−C*H_0 H_t P.                           (3)

This is the declared heat-matrix word. The inverse order in the second term follows from symmetry of each H_i, not from transposing a completed VALUE source.

## 3. Literal marked difference and normalization

Run the SAME complete chain tapes and BOTH same fill tapes in(1) at x=Y and x=Z_E. Emit

    T(G)=2τ/(c_sel²ρ²) [K_R(Y)−K_R(Z_E)].                 (4)

The carrier of the outer pair and its output fill cancel pathwise. The incoming first of the full chain is O(ρ²), including the known input/output rows and J. Consequently

    |T|≤Λ κ |Y−Z_E|,
    ||T||Lp≤Λ_p κ e_p.                                (5)

This is the ACTUAL one-energy return, not a bound on its target coefficient. It remains valid with all common coefficient roots and incoming aliases unchanged.

The complete chain-private first is O(κ/ρ), because the differences of private derivatives need only their ordinary O(ρ) bounds. The marked-pair's common carrier paths cost O(κ), and its residual paths cost O(κ A). Thus the safe complete first is O(κ/ρ) for ρ≤1. The retained public first is O(κ A), since Z_E has no G path.

An old coefficient label entering either primitive q_i has caller bound

    Λ κ/(ρ s_min) times its actual q_i first,

plus the κ multiple of the marked-pair caller. This is a complete path bill; coefficient roots that are later owned must be included in the private first at that scale. No root is made free by temporarily conditioning on it.

## 4. Mean calibration without a carrier-joint-law assumption

Let Ψ_R(x)=E_chain K_R(x) and d_R(x)=Ψ_R(x)−N_R x. Let δ_C(R)=ε_h,outer(R)+Λρ ε_h,inner(R). Composition of the two admitted pair comparisons at a STANDARD x gives

    ||d_R(G_std)||2≤δ_C(R)≤Λ ε_h(R).

The actual incoming first of K_R is O(ρ²), and ||N_R||op=O(ρ²), hence Lip(d_R)=O(ρ²).

The marked pair Y has a coupling to its stationary reference conditional on G,R, with error δ_E(R); the reference is marginally standard at fixed R after G is integrated. Therefore

    ||d_R(Y)||L2(G,Ω_E)≤Λ[ε_h(R)+ρ²δ_E(R)].

Separately, Z_E is exactly standard at fixed R, so its mean-defect has L2 bound Λ ε_h(R). This argument never replaces the JOINT actual pair (Y,Z_E) by a fictitious joint Gaussian law. The exact difference N_R(Y−Z_E) remains until its final conditional expectation. Since E[Z_E|G,R]=0, the marked pair's conditional mean gives

    E[T|G,R]
      =τ L D_R J D_R L* M_E(R)G + error,

with integrated error at most

    Λ κ[δ_E+ρ^−2 ε_h].                               (6)

More precisely add κ η_E for the independently supplied conditional mean bias of the marked pair; the stated δ_E bounds η_E, while its actual first-filter calibration may be sharper. All harmless fixed c_sel factors are included in Λ. The high-order gradient-pair floor is explicitly amplified by κ/ρ². It is not replaced by a source-sensitive prior that was never proved.

This also explains why the earlier arbitrary p+v instruction was insufficient: that input need not have a standard-marginal reference. Here both actual inputs are calibrated at their proper marginals while their literal coupled difference retains the mark.

## 5. A positive coefficient use, owned roots and work

If every R record in this coefficient source is independent of the old consumer conditional only on G,θ, put all those records inside the new private comparison and leave a fixed independent numerical Gaussian keep untouched. At numerical primitive widths and with the declared root first absorbed into O(κ/ρ), the ordinary buffered Riesz comparison of T to its complete conditional mean costs

    Λ κ² e/ρ.

Choosing ρ≥κ/A gives at most Aκe. Add the calibrated mean error(6), including the original marked-pair error κδ_E and the actual side priors. The residual energy remains κe, complete fresh first at most A, public first κA, and caller remains its stated κ/(ρs_min) row. This is a finite positive insertion using the ordinary keep; no new A-power copies occur.

If any R root is retained as an external observer, that all-private comparison is unavailable. Its conditional field and later root/current obligations must be returned instead. A source label q_i may be a nonlinear function of the original common root; its actual replay and first costs are counted and do not change the heat-matrix target in(3).

The literal bill is Q_marked-pair+4Q_block-pair+Q_fill, including the arithmetic/storage of the known contractions and Gaussian completions. Known does not mean free: for arbitrary dense P,C an actual supplied fill implementation must be charged. The cheap native specialization requires the original scalar-block Gaussian rows or another explicitly bounded-cost completion. For scalar-block rows their Gram and fill maps are finite known coefficient matrices tensored with I_d, including singular aliases; compute that known finite completion and apply it on the literal Gaussian blocks. No unknown coefficient matrix or dimension-rich eigenvector oracle is introduced.

There is one COMPLETE marked VALUE pair plus FOUR complete block-gradient-pair evaluations: two passes through the chain, reevaluated at the two changed passives, while common q_i records are cached only on identical arrays. Each block source call expands into its displayed two original-gradient differences. Complete source FIRST/adjoint sweeps and all discarded-cache replays remain. The block pair order B and every actual width guard are fixed before execution; no polynomial dependence on growing B is asserted.

An equally valid odd-mark version uses the same-record endpoints Y^±=Pv(f_R;±G,Ω_E), and emits τ/(c_sel²ρ²)[K_R(Y^+)−K_R(Y^−)]. Its carrier cancels and its target is the same. This version executes both complete marked endpoints; it is not silently billed as one call unless their actual common subexpressions are cached.

For an explicit numerical side-prior example, if ρ=A^γ, e_bound≈sqrt(Dim)A^3.9 and the actual gradient-source block dimension is n_h≤Λ Dim A^−κ_n, its amplified prior is Λ κ sqrt(Dim) A^[γ(B−2)−κ_n/2]. A strict fixed order B>2+(4.9+κ_n/2)/γ pays the nominal Aκe_bound budget, subject to the actual B-dependent radius guard and source-restoration tolerances. This is an assigned numerical budget, not a relative error promise when the actual e is smaller.

## 6. Remaining native orientation obligations

The construction has an exact conditional stationary-reference skew word and a small executed mark. It does not establish that the matrices H_i(R) in(3) are the original correlated nonlinear-query Hessians appearing in Curl_f. Independent primitive heat can change that target, even when each marginal query is legal. A native application must supply its exact common-root/Mehler genealogy and charge every restoration.

It also produces the skew word acting on the FORWARD M_E. The true orientation is −Sym E[R_E Curl_f]=Sym E[Curl_f R_E*]. Replacing R_E* by R_E leaves the genuine marked two-curl term, which is indispensable even in the hostile affine fixture. That word is not removed by(4). Therefore this is an executable typed generator for an admitted heat word, not an all-source true-covariance theorem.

Floating-point subtraction at a fixed precision is not a proof of the κe energy return; the exact VALUE identity and its assigned absolute numerical error are what is used.
