## 7. Why this does not solve arbitrary heat restoration

The coupled difference itself has no uniformly small normalized first. Before radial preparation,

    |f_D|<=min(a_k|x|, delta|Z|/(2t)),

but its z first is still O(1/t) and its active first is O(1). Dividing by delta or by delta/t to claim an extra source-amplitude grade would divide these first bounds by the same factor. For general C2 sources the missing factor cannot be supplied by Hölder smoothness of Dg. The new construction deliberately does not make that division.

Heat interpolation of a coefficient at variance h^2 formally inserts h^2 and two derivative slots. At a matching floor h=alpha^gamma this has zero net contribution 2gamma-2gamma to the old reserve. Those two slots are a SAME-VERTEX trace, not one of the admitted distinct-vertex bank bridges. Neither the all-cut theorem nor (6) licenses that trace. A proof exploiting it needs a separate source/frame and dimensional certificate.

For subsequent shells with the SAME a=N=n, K=n-2 and a fixed chosen Gamma, a strictly increasing reserve requires

    n(beta_j-beta_(j+1))-(n-2)(gamma_(j+1)-gamma_j)>0.     (9)

Therefore positive beta_j can pay only a finite total increase in gamma:

    gamma_j < gamma_0+n beta_0/(n-2)
             =1/n+n/[2(n-1)(n-2)].                     (10)

Every scale must ALSO satisfy gamma_j<=the preselected Gamma. At n=8 the allocation ceiling is 37/168, so the permissible supremum is min(Gamma,37/168), with strictness at the allocation endpoint. For the particular one-step Gamma=1/7, the floor cap is already 1/7. A larger finite sequence would have to choose its larger Gamma in advance and recheck all old reserves/first guards; it cannot change Gamma silently during the induction. The 37/168 bound is an allocation-budget ceiling of this particular marked shell rule, not an impossibility theorem for other source classes. A requested terminal bound alpha^P from (4) would require gamma_j>=P-n, which is incompatible with arbitrary P here. In addition, if old mixed currents are merely discarded using one first-Riesz bound, their grade n+1-(n-2)gamma balances the heat grade at gamma=1/(n-1), the one-step choice above. Going further would require explicit mixed-current repair as well as a new source gain.

An infinite sequence of tiny reallocation steps could have positive gains tending to zero. Strict gain alone would not make that heat queue well founded; a proposed finite target schedule must specify a positive minimum gain and a finite final scale. No such infinite schedule is used here.

Thus the construction replaces one bare heat remainder by an executable, higher-reserve shell plus a provably smaller bare heat remainder. It supplies one concrete rank-eight reentry, not an all-order heat-restoration engine or c(P)=o(P).
