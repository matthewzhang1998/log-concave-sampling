# Independent audit: marked heat-shell source reentry

2026-10-05. The primary sources were read but not edited by this auditor. Exact audited hashes, diagnostics and unchanged sealed-input checks are recorded in `independent-checks.json`.

## Verdict

**PASS as a scoped one-step source-reentry theorem relative to the stated sealed finite native interfaces.** The construction gives an executable original-VALUE marked source for the exact complete shell between two positive heat levels. The heterogeneous potential has the claimed bridge and conditional identities when one maximum width reserve is fixed beforehand. The rank-eight reserve rises from 179/28 to 46/7, and the simultaneously rebuilt positive return has grade 57/7 with its stated computed constants and numerical floors.

This is **not** a new best rate: direct finer-heat retuning already supplied 57/7. It is not a full original heat-defect source, an arbitrary-order restoration theorem, an executed native compiler, or a proof that every unspecified law residual belongs to the source grammar. The imported compiler, conditional-current grammar, ownership, complete first/readout/gap certificates, and numerical Sobolev floors remain real hypotheses.

The source files incorporate the audit's three clarifications: public logarithms remain in Lambda_8; later scales also obey the preselected Gamma cap; and the optional convex chord has increment first (1-s) delta/t, distinct from the marked half-difference's delta/(2t). No further mathematical correction is required for the stated one-step scope.

## 1. Literal mark and complete source certificates

The cold and warm source evaluations use the same private variable, probes, anchors, smaller private width and radial map. Their only changed argument is the captured center z -> z+delta Z. A half-difference of the two private gradients is still a private gradient, and both vanish at the same origin. The common radial pullback preserves those exact statements. Neither result would follow from unrelated private variables or a scalar cutoff.

The triangle half-sum proves the ordinary normalized radius, active first and all-proper-cut/one-HS bounds. Differentiating z hits both terms and gives at most 1/t; differentiating Z hits only the warm term and gives delta/(2t). These rows must be injected into the actual shared bank and added when their coordinates overlap. They cannot be treated as independent merely because the formula has two displayed summands.

Oddness in each active probe survives the mark and the radial pullback. Its centering conclusion requires that probe to remain independently symmetric in the integration. The k=0 mark is generally not privately centered: our admissible one-dimensional example has private mean approximately 0.00828554 at fixed centers. The retained finite mean/filter/calibration ledger is therefore essential.

The response identity has the exact factor b_k t^k/(2A). A same-center anchor has zero selected X response; the k+1 active Gaussian directions contribute total variance t^2. A subsequent W average contributes another t^2, and the warm Z average contributes delta^2. Thus the two total primitive variances are exactly 1-r^2+sigma^2 and 1-r^2+tau^2. The factor two restored at the root is necessary. No score-weighted tensor estimate is executed.

A marked slot is a linear two-branch alternative, not a simultaneous product of two force occurrences. Each branch still has N=n and K=n-2; the extra VALUE queries and finite branch counts are charged separately. A derivative in an increment row can remove the mark and leave an ordinary warm source with its literal delta factor. Admitting those unmarked branches is necessary for closure, and assigning no mark-smallness credit makes their grades safe.

## 2. Exact shell on the real common bank

The tensor telescope is pointwise multilinearity on one fixed labelled tree and one fixed complete coefficient dictionary. Every physical slot, internal edge, ancestral row and original clock remains unchanged. Therefore it can be integrated against the true shared bank without discarding cross-history correlations. Independent original-coefficient heat increments at different force occurrences give the corresponding independently heat-smoothed force insertions after integration; repeated complete keys retain their original shared row.

The independently implemented diagnostics checked three rank-eight tree degree patterns on nontrivial shared structural Gaussian ancestry. The full-bank cold and warm products agree with their intended heat targets. The old coarse/private split and the new warm split agree after bank integration, while their conditional products differ at a fixed bank sample. Replacing shared structural ancestry by independent occurrence rows changes the mean; one tested discrepancy is approximately 2.97e-6. These are deliberately separate checks of the three distinctions that the proof needs.

We also contracted rank-eight tensor networks with all eight physical legs kept, independently verifying the eight-mark telescope and its restored factor two. This tests full multilinearity rather than only scalar products.

An old completed transport cannot simply be added to an independent new transport and declared to cancel. The stated construction instead rebuilds old plus shell on one common physical reference H and then averages their complete coefficient sum. At common finite clocks Q that sum has bank mean T_(n,Q)(sigma). The separate standard-Gaussian-retained-caller clock theorem pays the passage to continuous clocks. There is no uniform arbitrary-Y quadrature assertion.

The original defect decomposes exactly as the executed shell plus T_n(0)-T_n(sigma). Only the latter is paid by the complete-target Appell bound. The source does not realize that remaining unsmoothed term.

## 3. Optional positive chord

At each occurrence the convex source f_s=(1-s)f_w+s f_c has the same private-gradient, active-probe and z-first guards. Its increment derivative is bounded by (1-s)delta/t. The graph response is n-multilinear in the occurrence fields, so its derivative along this diagonal chord is polynomial of degree at most n-1. An m=ceil(n/2)-point Gauss-Legendre rule has positive weights and is exact through degree 2m-1, hence exactly integrates that derivative at each fixed bank and original clock tuple.

After all independent occurrence heat rows are averaged, the intermediate original force is the convex mixture (1-s)P_tau g+s P_sigma g. It need not be P_h g for any Brownian heat parameter h. The construction therefore avoids introducing a same-vertex Laplacian/self-trace. The sequential coordinate chord is precisely the cheaper ordinary telescope.

The optional simultaneous chord bills 2 n ceil(n/2) V_n raw VALUES: each of n marked graphs at each interpolation node evaluates both local cold/warm branches. At rank eight this is 64 V_8=1,729,331,200. The finite node count has no inverse-tolerance exponent, but scalar node/weight errors still require certified arithmetic floors.

## 4. Heterogeneous potential and native allocation

For the actual occurrence labels, let Bbeta=sum beta_v, Hheat=sum gamma_v k_v, d=a-Bbeta-Hheat, and Psi=d-2Gamma. Distributing alpha^(beta_v+gamma_v k_v)t_v^(-k_v) at each occurrence leaves the exact product alpha^a times its original inverse-width coefficient. Each normalized local multiplier is bounded by a fixed geometric factor times alpha^beta_v; the genuine root has the additional alpha^d.

This distribution is a real rebuilt source/amplitude version. It is compatible with the frozen native proof because the local compensating factors alpha^(gamma_v k_v)t_v^(-k_v) are uniformly bounded, are frozen during dynamic differentiation, and can be absorbed into heat-uniform normalized local guards. Selected/readout constants and signs remain explicitly charged. One cannot simply relabel an already executed all-width-at-root program and inherit its conditional amplitude jet.

A connected r-packet bank tree introduces exactly r-1 bridges. Their extra width loss is the sum of the actual two endpoint gamma labels. Therefore

    Psi_child = sum Psi_i + sum_bridges(2Gamma-gamma_u-gamma_v).

It is superadditive because both endpoint labels are at most the same preselected Gamma. Literal alpha-dependent injection factors must still be entered into the nominal exponent, or conservatively bounded if they improve it.

For h copies of one genuine root, every retained side occurrence carries its own beta_v+gamma_v k_v, while every root copy supplies d. Omitting side occurrences therefore gives d_child=h d and Psi_child=h Psi+2Gamma(h-1). This is an identity for the imported source-qualified finite grammar, not a proof that an arbitrary current extraction has that grammar. For heterogeneous root families, the corresponding expression uses the sum of their individual root d's.

The sufficient exponent-level complete-first test follows from a path containing a genuine root and one final width hit: its exponent is at least beta_min+d-Gamma=beta_min+Psi+Gamma. The actual path count, affine injections and numerical first floors remain to be summed. Positivity of Psi alone would not prove an O(alpha) first.

The concrete Gamma=1/(n-1) belongs to the old/shell pair. Including any other recursively repairable old source types requires their width labels to obey that same preselected Gamma, their positive reserves, and their own actual first/source-grammar certificates. The outside component in the positive Riesz join needs only its full O(alpha) first certificate; that condition does not promote it to a source-qualified mixed-queue packet.

For a fixed finite queue, positive beta_min and a positive minimum reserve gain bound force count, reserve depth, bridge arity and primitive orders below a fixed cutoff. A family whose beta or reserve increments tend to zero need not satisfy that termination lemma. The paper correctly limits its application to the supplied grammar and a fixed finite schedule.

The own-return bound uses the square of the sum of weighted actual root sizes. It includes old/shell and history/history cross-group branches. Disjoint owned dynamic tapes do not justify dropping those cross terms. Its heat-uniform character is imported from the frozen native comparison, with every finite source/filter/mean error kept separately because such a floor can produce a root-linear branch.

After native comparison, the centered coefficient field on the same H has physical degree seven. The first Gaussian Riesz identity is applied to the full shared B at the same positive path endpoint. Transfer through the single untouched independent keep gives the conservative factor 3^(7/2)/sqrt(kappa) times energy times the sum of the old and new complete bank-first envelopes. It requires a B-uniform, fixed-Y-uniform stacked Jacobian certificate for the actual outside old endpoint, including every retained observer. Jointly averaged Jacobian control or a derivative of a law-error bound is insufficient. An exposed unattenuated B observer fails the hypothesis.

The complete old tau family is part of the rebuilt new collection. Its different conditional coefficient representation causes no problem only because its full bank mean is correct and the common-H bank comparison pays the remaining fluctuations. Original structural ancestors stay shared; no old private shield is silently renamed or integrated twice.

The final grade statement includes Lambda_8(alpha,D), with its computed node/readout/moment/public-log factors. Logs at the tied heat/mixing exponent do not disappear into a log-free uniform constant. Lower target currents, terminal literal-gradient/curl baseline, covariance and unrelated old debts remain separately matched obligations.

The added terminal-port statement is also correct and appropriately conditional. For the literal map y -> g(L(y)-R(y)) with D L=sI and ||D R||<=J_R, the chain derivative is H(sI-D R), H=Dg(L-R). ## 7. Finite allocation budget and counterexamples to stronger claims

Keeping a=N=n and K=n-2, reserve growth between two shell stages requires

    n(beta_j-beta_(j+1)) > (n-2)(gamma_(j+1)-gamma_j).

Telescoping and beta_j>0 give gamma_j<1/n+n/[2(n-1)(n-2)]. At rank eight this allocation endpoint is 37/168, and every stage also has gamma_j<=the originally fixed Gamma. Thus the permitted supremum is min(Gamma,37/168), with strictness at the allocation endpoint. The actual one-step Gamma=1/7 already caps the chosen floor at 1/7.

A bounded total increase does not by itself bound the number of arbitrarily tiny positive steps. The text now explicitly excludes using an infinite succession of vanishing gains as a well-founded queue. More importantly, any finite terminal heat error alpha^P would require gamma>=P-n, impossible for arbitrary P within this particular budget. The old first-Riesz mixing grade worsens as gamma rises and balances the heat grade at 1/(n-1), so later improvement also needs explicit mixed-current repair.

There is no uniform delta-small active first for the marked source. For an admissible family g_delta(x)=x/2+(epsilon/4)sin(x/epsilon), epsilon=delta/pi, the k=0 mark at t=1, Z=1, x=z=0 has D_x f_D=1/4 for every delta>0, despite f_D(0)=0. Dividing by delta would blow up the normalized first. The source's general C2 assumptions do not supply the missing gain.

A Brownian heat derivative would instead add two derivative slots at the same force vertex. Those slots form a local trace; a proper-cut/spanning-tree certificate does not automatically bound that trace. The optional convex chord avoids the operation but supplies no new smallness. These boundaries prevent interpreting this source reentry as arbitrary restoration.
