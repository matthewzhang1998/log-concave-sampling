## 1. Exact matrix mode decomposition

At the original recorded queries put

Then, without commuting any matrices,

    K0-K1=.5[(T0-T1)M(A0+A1)+(T0+T1)M(A0-A1)].        (1)

Both terms can be of the leading ra curl grade. In orthogonal ancestor-output coordinates p+=(p0+p1)/sqrt(2), p-=(p0-p1)/sqrt(2), the physical ambient S row has derivatives

    D_p- G_S=(ra/sqrt(2))(T0+T1)M.

Along the actual common-ancestor variation dx0=dx1=dx,

    dp+=(A0+A1)dx/sqrt(2),
    dp-=(A0-A1)dx/sqrt(2).

Their sum is exactly ra(K0-K1)dx. Thus selecting only the common p+ direction does not remove the second term in(1).

## 3. The opposite direction exposes the other word but has an unmarked body

Instead set p0=g0(x0)+sigma V and p1=g0(x1)-sigma V. Then

    F_minus(W,V)=r[gt(t0+a sigma MV)-gt(t1-a sigma MV)]. (3)

Its V derivative contains the terminal SUM, as required by the second term of(1). The same M* output projection is a genuine V-gradient: it is the gradient of r/(a sigma)[Vt(t0+a sigma MV)+Vt(t1-a sigma MV)]. Its derivative is

    ra sigma M*[Ht(t0+a sigma MV)+Ht(t1-a sigma MV)]M,

which is positive semidefinite under the strong terminal hypothesis.

However at the actual source slice Z=0, one has x1=x0,t1=t0 and E=0. Strong monotonicity gives

    |F_minus(W,V)| >=2m ra sigma |MV|.                 (4)

At that slice F_minus is odd in V, so its centered conditional L2 energy is at least2m ra sigma||M||HS. It therefore has no pointwise original-E mark. This is a real unmarked stationary-side candidate, not an impossibility result: a construction may retain it, but it must retain its actual carrier/body, roots, widths and finite path costs.

There is an exact warning about baseline subtraction. Since t0 does not read the original Z, define the literal Z=0 baseline

    B0(W,V)=r[gt(t0+a sigma MV)-gt(t0-a sigma MV)].

Then direct cancellation yields

    F_minus(W,V)-B0(W,V)
         =r[gt(t0-a sigma MV)-gt(t1-a sigma MV)]
