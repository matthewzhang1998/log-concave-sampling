## 1. Corrections discovered or confirmed in review

### A. Retained-bank differentiation does not commute with the unreplicated bridge

The initial §6 claimed a derivative/join commutation without distinguishing the two derivatives. Its checker differentiated an ordinary differential product at independent occurrence centers. It did not differentiate the actual retained-bank construction

    K(B) = ∫ E[F′(√s B+√(1−s)U+)G′(√s B+√(1−s)U−)] ds.

The latter obeys

    K′(B) = ∫ √s E[F″G′+F′G″] ds,
    K″(B) = ∫ s E[F‴G′+2F″G″+F′G‴] ds.

These factors are independent of the rule that the ORIGINAL covariance-bridge derivative slots use their original injection rows. A subsequent derivative through the retained B has a new replica chain row.

Concrete admissible diagnostic: F=G=c+d cos B arises from a positive monotone sine-perturbed gradient after heating. Its node coefficient is

    d² exp(−(1−s)) sin²(√s B).

At s=1/4 and B=π/2, omitting √s doubles the derivative. For the complete integral, K″(0)=2d²/e, whereas the unweighted shortcut gives 2d²(1−1/e). Thus this is not merely a naming issue. The producer corrected §6, the BANK_BRIDGE row and the checker with a separate REPLICA_RETAINED_ROW_CHAIN_FACTOR branch.

### B. Covariance tangent nonmembership is a retained-record statement

For every fixed E, the conditional endpoint is a shifted Gaussian with positive variance. Its rank-two and rank-four density derivatives are linearly independent. Requiring equality after every bounded retained test χ(E) therefore proves that a nonzero q(E)² D⁴ is not a conditional covariance-only tangent p(E)D².

This statement is false in general after E is consumed. Let E be standard Gaussian, W(E)=E,

    q(E)=exp(−E²/2),
    p(E)=(9E²−3)exp(−E²).

Both coefficients are bounded and computable. If ρ is the standard Gaussian density, (ρq²)″=ρp. Integration by parts twice yields

    E[q(E)² φ⁽⁴⁾(X0)] = E[p(E) φ″(X0)].

Thus the rank-four current belongs to the expanded covariance tangent image in this consumed-record quotient. The producer made the retained-E test convention explicit in §1 and repaired §5 with this exact counterexample. Differentiating a retained χ would create extra branches; it cannot be silently dropped.

### C. Consuming a bank does not remove its endpoint dependence

The T2 formulas E_B[F]D², Var_B(F)D⁴/2 and (E_B F)²D⁴/2 require a B-independent physical base, or an additional exact tilted-bank/endpoint relation. Merely declaring B consumed is insufficient when W(E) reads B.

For example, with W(B)=B and F(B)=c+d cos B,

    E[F(B)e^(i t B)] − E[F(B)]E[e^(i t B)]
      = d exp(−(1+t²)/2)(cosh(t)−1),

which is nonzero away from t=0. Fourier witnesses can be replaced by smooth compactly supported cutoffs using Gaussian dominated convergence. The final T2 row must preserve its local no-B-readout hypothesis and OLD_ENDPOINT_BANK_READOUT escape. The producer added that qualification.

### D. An aggregate native norm floor does not identify individual residual coefficients

ROOT-SEED supplies a whole grouped comparison with a floor quadratic in the complete summed root envelopes. It does not certify termwise support, sign, cancellation, or a particular native quadratic tensor. The initial phrase that all first nonmain native terms have grade at least six was too strong. The producer replaced it with the grade-six aggregate discrepancy statement and explicitly identified the boundary norm as the relevant filtration.

### E. Source-version differentiation needs derivative preparation, not ordinary response bias

A small prepared response error does not imply a small derivative of that error. The difference

    D_z(μ_k T̂_k) − μ_(k+1)T̂_(k+1)

must remain a literal defect unless separately certified derivative/Sobolev preparation floors control it. Ordinary radial/value response floors alone cannot be differentiated. The producer identified and repaired this issue during the audit. A first-hit scalar radial-tail estimate can be proved directly from the literal source map; it does not license arbitrary higher hits or differentiation of a LAW error. The compared uncut identities must use the same frozen width and heat target.

## 2. Independent checks of the principal formulas

### Primitive responses

The C0 two-VALUE and C1 four-VALUE formulas have the stated normalizations. Gaussian integration by parts gives

    T0 = E[g′(z+tZ)]/A,
    T1 = t E[g″(z+tZ)]/(2A).

For the original C²-potential class, higher force derivatives in these expressions are weak Gaussian derivatives, as in the pinned ACTIVE source. The restore factors 13A and 26A/t include the explicit /13 preparation normalization. These are local responses, not by themselves pure-rank completed currents.

The pinned LOW30 filter interface and frozen-width addendum are relevant imports: the multilinear physical-probe symbols require finite first-chaos selection and separately paid filtering/Sobolev floors. Raw unfiltered f1 is not replaced by a linear probe merely because one response has been computed.

### Rank-four connected versus raw currents

An independent route uses the exact two-Gaussian integral, with z=λθ²:

    log E exp[z(P+aθ)(Q+bθ)]
      = −log(1−z²)/2 + [zabθ²+z²(a²+b²)θ²/2]/(1−z²).

Its finite expansion agrees through λ⁴ with the dictionary. The raw second law coefficient includes λ²a²b²D⁸/2 in addition to the connected ranks 4,6,6. That term is half the square of the leading law coefficient. It can be canceled only by a comparator whose actual quadratic law jet has been supplied. A formal exponential of a rank-four operator is not such a positive sampler certificate.

### Whole-bank bridge defect

For the two-force coefficient F=(c+d cos B)², conditional independence of the two replica innovations gives the displayed square integrand and K(0)=0. Since F is nonconstant, Var(F)>0. Continuity makes the defect nonzero on a neighborhood of zero, so it is detected by a bounded retained-bank test; this is not a defect existing only on a Gaussian null set.

The full bank mean of K is Var(F). Its four frequency-pair contributions match the finite covariance identity. Retained-bank equality and complete-bank coefficient equality remain distinct.

### Tilt and grouped overlaps

The complete shifted-Gaussian Wick formula and complete partition extraction commute. Performing connected extraction only on the zero-tilt answer loses the two rank-six terms. This is a real missing-legs error.

The mixed two-packet covariance polynomial is correct. An identical shared pair P,Q is a useful formal example, but its cross block I₂ cannot be produced by one scalar common carrier: coisometric cross blocks there have rank at most one. There is also an explicit permitted scalar-carrier witness: take each packet's P,Q readout correlations with a unit H equal to 1/2. Then all four cross covariances are 1/4, a_i=b_i=1/2, and

    Cov(Y1,Y2) = 1/8 + θ²/4.

The full covariance matrix is positive semidefinite and the remaining carrier variance can be allocated to the root and buffer. Thus the grouped/isolated discrepancy is present within the intended ownership geometry, not merely in an abstract larger shared bank. Its coefficient remains an ideal diagnostic; the native quadratic jet is still unidentified.

### Covariance/time source

The exact positive scalar variance path is correctly expanded by

    exp[(s q+s²r/2)D²].

This yields the acceleration rD²/2 and square q²D⁴/2 at order s², and qrD⁴/2+q³D⁶/6 at order s³. The moving-endpoint identity (q+sr)D² is also correct; the different endpoint conventions must not be mixed. Positivity requires the stated actual variance gap.

The arithmetic is correct for nominal a=4, β=1/4 and γ=1/8:

The cutoff 11/2 is strictly below that floor. Any displayed public-log factors must remain in the bound; the strict half-grade margin can absorb fixed logarithmic powers at sufficiently small α.

Under those hypotheses the imported native leading extraction and owned-carrier addition give a nonempty finite associated-graded right inverse. This is a legitimate first-jet witness. It is not a proof that every unidentified native residual has a known coefficient, and it does not close standalone differentiation, retained-bank substitution, arbitrary extra labels, heat traces or a quadratic native repair queue.
