    −κ0[sin(k(q+σv_1))−sin(kq)]/(kσ),

while its zero jet is −κ0 cos(kq)v_1. There is no uniform pointwise C1 rate as k grows. A stronger subsequent scalar resonance showed failure even of the generic matched-source weak calibration; see the separate diagnostic metadata below.

    Sym[Curl_f(R_f*−R_f)].
