    ||(R_Omega F)(D_Omega F)^T||_(L2;HS)

There is exactly one Hilbert factor. The Riesz identity along `base+tF+sqrt(kappa)G_keep` has the factor t because the base is Omega-independent after conditional centering. ## 5. The integrated retained-public child kernel exists

This is the most delicate ownership point, and the separate lemma supplies the right construction.

First work in the ordinary single-packet Gaussian law, before conditioning on H. Follow a distinguished spine, freeze its own nonleaf publics and side outputs, and chronologically couple actual and ideal channels starting from the same primitive leaf passive R. The one-step prior is uniform in those frozen external values, and composition preserves R. All other publics remain untouched.

The completed ideal spine can be represented as

    Y = A R + sqrt(I-AA^T) Z,

with Z standard and independent of all side banks and public primitives. This is a new representation of the completed conditional Gaussian spine. It is legitimate only because its intermediate outputs and innovations have been consumed. It is not a claim that the original intermediate innovation coordinates are retained.

Next recursively couple a side subtree using only that subtree's public tuple and private randomness. This coupling is independent of the enclosing spine's R and Z. If A and A' are the old and new terminal matrices, integration over R gives exactly

    E_R |(A-A')R|² = ||A-A'||_HS².

The uniform input-to-matrix-HS estimate bounds this by the squared side coupling cost with the actual ancestor factors. The covariance completion is controlled in HS by the gapped covariance-root inequality, and its independent Z supplies the same one-Hilbert conversion. There is no extra sqrt(D). Product differences in A give the stated strict-ancestor attenuation; summing the finite spine/side stages gives the original bound.

All primitive marked publics have been retained identically. Disintegrating the constructed joint law over their tuple Q produces the claimed measurable coupling kernel C_E(q). This yields an integrated conditional-W2 statement, not a uniform-in-q statement. It is not necessary, and would be wrong, to treat R as Gaussian after fixing its numerical value q. R's Gaussian independence is used when the kernel construction is integrated over the original public marginal.

Now append the physical carrier. Its root source-zero primitive bank T is independent of the child-private bank. Given Q and E, the child's private conditional kernel does not depend on T or H. One can therefore first sample the original coisometric (H,V_h) geometry and then sample the same kernel C_E(Q). Each packet's Q still has its original Gaussian marginal. Integrating the squared coupling cost over H and V gives precisely the old integrated cost d_h².

The method does not assert independent marked publics conditional on H. In fact that is generally false. Independence is used before adjoining H; the post-H argument uses conditional kernels and unchanged marginals. Across packets the child couplings can be chosen independently conditional on H and their null/public banks. Minkowski supplies the total output cost, so no false unconditional cross-packet independence enters.

Finally, M_h is E-measurable. Hence the child replacement propagates simply by the fixed linear map c_h M_h at cost `|c_h| ||M_h||op d_h`. No first bound for an ideal nonlinear mixed environment is needed.

## 6. Exact covariance restoration and the original polynomial endpoint

The root ideal innovation must be the same known G_root appearing in the literal source-zero row. This is possible: in the original packet marginal it is standard and independent of the entire child subtree and all marked public primitives. Write C_h = sqrt(I-M_h M_h^T). The decisive endpoint identity is pointwise:

    H + c_h M_h I_h^ideal + c_h (C_h-I) G_h^root
      = c_h [M_h I_h^ideal + C_h G_h^root]
        + B_h P_h^pub + G_h^buf.

This is precisely the original ideal-forward root and marked readout represented in the original physical carrier geometry. The common-carrier construction leaves each complete packet Gaussian marginal unchanged, so the standard root and marked-public covariance identities survive. Conditional on H, the root innovation and its packet publics may be correlated, but that conditional correlation is the intended one.

For ||M_h||op bounded away from one,

    ||C_h-I||_HS <= C sqrt(D) ||M_h||op².

Since M_h depends only on E and G_root is standard, the strong restoration costs the displayed `C |c_h| ||M_h||op² sqrt(D)`. No independence between packets is used. The ideal polynomial comparator must be formed on this same root innovation and the same other ideal Gaussian roots. The strong LOW30 comparison then applies packetwise to the unchanged marginal bank, and Minkowski gives F_poly even after the packets share H.

All intermediate and final laws are positive pushforwards. The original keep is carried once; neither its covariance nor a per-packet replacement reserve is added. Couplings and conditional means are analytical proof objects and alter no executed VALUE program.

## 7. Quantitative conclusion and limitations

The displayed equation (1) follows by triangle inequality over the stages above. The only dimension factor introduced by the argument is sqrt(D); finite source/path constants and permitted public logarithms remain in the literal envelopes. There is no `D^((m-1)/2)` polynomial-taming factor. Fixed v and kappa are essential to the stated constant convention.

For random E, the correct integrated bound is the L2(E) norm of the complete conditional right-hand side. The amended file explicitly makes this requirement. Products of merely L2 envelopes are not automatically L2; one must use uniform bounds or the necessary higher integrability.

With root grade 17/2, all new root-quadratic terms have grade 17. A child prior at nonroot radius alpha^(1/16) and fixed native order b contributes at grade `17/2+b/16` after multiplication by the root. Thus b=24 reaches grade 10 and b>24 leaves a strict margin for the declared logarithms. This verifies the manuscript's arithmetic. It does not eliminate the native/source/filter/calibration/cubature/old-bank debts or establish a growing-order complexity theorem.

The following scope boundaries are genuine:

1. If a child-private innovation is included in the physical source-zero row, the retained-public kernel cannot simply be appended with H fixed. The current proof no longer applies.
2. If an outside observer retains a root-native private tape, the ordinary root prior cannot consume it. Charging only H movement would miss that observer.
3. If the root has dynamic external probes, M_h is not necessarily fixed during child replacement. The linear propagation argument requires an additional proof.
4. The new carrier must be independent of E and old tapes. A coefficient caller cannot simultaneously be frozen in E and reidentified with H.
5. The theorem does not imply a carrier-fixed high-order packet W2 bound. Gaussian rotations still show why an arbitrarily small marginal prior can have first-order error after carrier retention.
6. An O(S² sqrt(D)) native bridge transfers ideal jet coefficients only below that floor unless the floor itself is explicitly retained. No exact all-orders native jets follow.
