# The precise algebraic operation supplied by the root-seed bridge

Status: the root-seed theorem has independently audited PASS under its stated hypotheses. This note states its relevance to algebraic current and positive-realization closure.

## Admitted operation: owned common-carrier addition

Take a finite labelled family of already realizable native marked-tree generators T_h(E), possibly of different fixed physical ranks. Every tree has a marked leaf usable as root, at most one physical mark at each original vertex, the original admitted source normalization, and a positive finite native implementation. Each root amplitude r_h carries the desired sign. The original coefficient bank E may be shared and correlated arbitrarily across h within its existing observer ledger. All new native tapes are private between packets except the known physical source-zero Gaussian carrier.

The new operation combines their actual native implementations as

    H + sum_h [X_h(r_h)-H] + the original one keep,

using the explicit Gaussian coisometries, and consumes the entire family as ONE output. It realizes the sum of their root-linear currents, with new intrinsic/native-ownership error O((sum_h Gamma_h |r_h|)^2 sqrt(D)) plus the literal prior, filter and strong-polynomial floors. The full coefficient bank is retained until its separately justified original-bank join. The complete source/caller firsts are the existing raw path sums.

This removes inverse physical variance-share powers from the addition operation. The proof does not pay polynomial dimension factors, does not add per-packet reserves, and does not add any executed VALUE calls. The number of summands still costs its actual full source/replay bill. Large l1 root weights still enter the guard and error.

## Compatibility with a formal rewrite

A formal identity may be followed by this positive-realization operation when each term on the rewritten right-hand side is one of the admitted native marked trees, with its exact force occurrences, ordered physical slots, original source rows and restored scalar factors. Arbitrary signs are root-amplitude signs, not negative probability weights. Coefficient correlations belonging to E are preserved. The formal rewrite must preserve the coefficient tensor before any already-paid final bank expectation, or explicitly carry its bank-mean/current identity and associated observer conditions.

The root-seed proof adds only a theorem about ownership and summation of those generators. Its seed means, Gaussian regression and conditional couplings are analytical proof objects. They are not new VALUE primitives or executable tensor/mean oracles. No derivative of a LAW error enters a rewritten source.

## What it does not add to the generator class

- It does not turn an internal Wick loop or a multiply marked force vertex into an admissible native tree.
- It does not identify a product of current tensors with a sum of already realizable currents.
- It does not permit a completed native output to be differentiated or reused as a new original gradient source.
- It does not expose the internal carrier, individual packet values, source-private probes, or a private keep as external labels.
- It does not allow an old caller to keep reading a tape after the rewrite consumes it.
- It does not prove a carrier-fixed high-order packet prior. The exact native seed example disproves that inference in general.

If a proposed algebraic rewrite produces a rooted graph with dynamic external probes at every possible root, the present proof does not cover it: the key root mean must be M_h(E) I_h with M_h independent of new dynamic tapes. A legal reorientation to an existing marked leaf suffices; inventing a new mark or erasing a live contraction does not.

Thus the new closure statement is finite additive positive realization with shared coefficient labels and one owned physical carrier. Genuine graph-class expansion still requires a separate algebraic representation theorem or a new analytic realization of the missing graph type.

## Grade and caller boundary from the algebra audit

The native-to-ideal bridge has an O(S^2 sqrt(D)) floor. Consequently it transfers the complete ideal common-endpoint jet only at grades strictly below that floor, or with that row explicitly retained. It is not an exact all-orders native-jet identity. In the current triple-cubic application S has alpha grade 17/2, so its floor has grade 17 and the alpha^10 error target is unaffected. Higher-order formal rewrites must check this inequality anew with their literal root allocation.

The NEW physical carrier H must be independent of E. The old bank B can belong to E and remain readable by W_old, but it cannot simultaneously be identified with H. The algebra worker's stress test makes this distinction concrete: for g(x)=c x+epsilon sin x, the law of W=B+alpha g(B) has first alpha derivative of its log-MGF

    c theta^2 + epsilon exp(-1/2) theta sin(theta),

which contains infinitely many nonzero ranks at one grade. Treating that same B as a frozen coefficient caller and an independent new source-zero carrier would be invalid. The current port may add a new independent owned-carrier repair conditionally on that old B, but it does not truncate or repair the old component's own infinite-rank current. Any old-bank join and observer transformation remain separate analytic operations.
