## Exclusions, privacy, and portability

Unsealed algebra-current-image, collective-heat, and observer-block drafts are excluded. The unsealed sibling-regrouping audit directory is excluded despite a historical directory link. Unselected live extras, bytecode caches, and staging trees are excluded; in particular, the extra carrier seal-verification report, nonlinear bridge gap note, and unsealed skew-square exact-OU trio are not admitted by proximity.

The full LOW30 and HIGH13 manuscripts remain external. Their provenance hashes may be recorded, but their contents are not bundled. Optional external-source verification must disclose omissions and reject incorrect supplied bytes; it must not remove mathematical assertions.

Deterministic scans and inspection of the selected research files and recovery dependencies found no credentials, private conversations, or operational prompts. Original workspace paths are retained as historical research locators, not claims that those paths exist on a reader's machine. This is a bounded privacy check, not a universal secret detector.

Most historical checkers write reports beside their source files. Run them only in disposable copies with explicit path mappings, preserving the archived results. Builders and seal-generators must not be mistaken for read-only validators. Public reproduction must distinguish source verification, mathematical assertions, optional external checks, and work that was never executed.
