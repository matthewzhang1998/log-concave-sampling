### V8: strong-mean barrier and scalar law-only components

**Uniform strong-mean barrier.** The [proof](research/cost/sublinear-coarse-mean-20261004/STRONG-POSTERIOR-MEAN-ORACLE-BARRIER.md) and [independent audit](research/cost/sublinear-coarse-mean-20261004/independent-audit/INDEPENDENT-STRONG-MEAN-BARRIER-AUDIT.md) show that every adaptive randomized original-gradient VALUE / first-action algorithm with at most N query sites has, for some potential in the stated uniform C² Hessian-sandwich class,

    RMS error for the canonical posterior force mean ≥ c A/(N+1)^(3/2).

The necessary heat-cost exponent for the uniform strong target O(A^j), P=2j−1, is therefore c(P)≥(P−1)/3. This applies to the complete program, including all original-query preprocessing, ancestors, control variates and reused work; it also covers a uniform worst-case expected query budget. The hard potential may depend on A and N. Constants and small-heat thresholds must be uniform over the potential class. This is neither a single-fixed-potential lower bound nor a posterior-sampling or buffered-law lower bound. Additional free potential-value, expectation, normalization, smoothed-score or posterior-sample oracles are outside its model. The bound rules out eventual o(P) cost while retaining this strong-mean contract; it does not assert the lower slope is attainable.

**Positive Stein time quadrature.** The [packet](research/cost/endpoint-stein-quadrature-20261004/ENDPOINT-STEIN-PACKET-AND-POLYLOG-QUADRATURE.md) and [audit](research/cost/endpoint-stein-quadrature-20261004/independent-audit/INDEPENDENT-ENDPOINT-STEIN-AUDIT.md) use O_J(log²(1/A)) original-gradient VALUE queries at fixed J. Positive dyadic Gauss quadrature gives ||v_Q−v||_L²≤δA√D for the analytical Gaussian-conditional mean. The literal finite packet supplies actual private/caller firsts, anchored zeros and full query/record counts, with no algebraic dimension/heat guard. At the executed center its weak identity uses the anchored finite-center potential; the missing linear tilt is restored by the separately priced finite-mode residual.

The executed bridge is not strongly close to that mean: its fluctuation remains O(A√D), already visible in a quadratic example. Its limiting quadratic covariance coefficient is 1/4+π²/16≈0.86685 rather than the target coefficient 1. V9 above separately proves the packet’s marginal order-two law and a matrix-quadratic amplifier. General nonlinear higher-law correction, covariance descendants, strong adjacent pairs and a complete sublinear-cost source remain unproved. Arbitrarily accurate conditional-mean quadrature is not an arbitrary-order posterior-law theorem.

**Bounded scalar positive law factory.** The independently derived [construction](research/cost/sublinear-coarse-mean-20261004/SCALAR-POSITIVE-HERMITE-MEAN-LAW-FACTORY.md) and [audit](research/cost/sublinear-coarse-mean-20261004/independent-scalar-factory-audit/AUDIT.md) transform K independent complete scalar records W with an executable anchor b and |W−b|≤σε into a positive approximation to N(EW,σ²). The conditional density has exact known normalization and stays between one-half and three-halves of a Gaussian density. The original-query bill is K complete provider executions plus the anchor; a selected finite rejection cap is explicit. No unknown expectation is queried. Its arbitrary fixed-order conclusion uses shrinking ε and appropriate cutoff/cap choices, not K alone at a fixed cutoff. Fresh factor records must be integrated out and cannot be retained as later observers.

**Polylogarithmic scalar refinement.** The [extension](research/cost/sublinear-coarse-mean-20261004/POLYLOG-SCALAR-MEAN-LAW-REFINEMENT.md), with [independent audit](research/cost/sublinear-coarse-mean-20261004/independent-polylog-refinement-audit/AUDIT.md), uses an executed pilot, fresh independent batch averages, known clipping and rank-uniform Hermite bounds. For a known bound |W−b₀|≤S, it achieves W₂≤Cσδ from

    O((1+(S/σ)²) log³((e+S/σ)/δ)) complete provider records.

The literal count is (K+1)M, not an exponential replay tree. All provider ancestors and independent replays remain charged. This adds no inverse-heat count when S/σ is at most public-logarithmic. The constants are highly conservative; no practical speedup or enormous-batch simulation is claimed. The pilot may be retained in an integrated same-pilot coupling, with rare bad-pilot outcomes paid explicitly. This is not a uniform exact-target guarantee conditional on every bad pilot, and the fresh factor banks still cannot be retained.

Both scalar results are marginal or explicitly conditioned buffered-law lemmas in an ideal Gaussian/uniform and known real-arithmetic model. They supply no strong mean statistic, smooth first/adjoint map, actual paired-caller port, dimension-free vector factory, full numerical implementation, posterior-bias correction or recursive source theorem. Scalar retained-Z compatibility with the Stein packet targets Z−v_Q(Z)+σN; its added σ² covariance is a real outstanding debt. Even with σ comparable to A, that buffered Stein target is not automatically the posterior.
