Excluded: private communications and preparation, raw conversations, personal information, credentials, confidential external manuscripts and their derived discussion, unrelated workspace material, duplicate archive containers, and superseded drafts.

Original working files were preserved. This directory is a copy-only snapshot. No publication license is assigned by this bundle.

Later active law-correction research and unreviewed drafts are excluded. These bounded scalar components do not imply vector, first/paired, full numerical, posterior-law, or end-to-end sublinear closure.
